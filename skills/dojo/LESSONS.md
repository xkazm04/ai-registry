### Redesign proposal
- Phase 4/5 assume the forge's manifest shapes. The pair-spec runner + a `judge prepare/park` helper (blind maps, Gemini with model fallback, unblinding, pick-rate arithmetic) were rebuilt in the consuming repo this run; if a second repo needs them, they belong in the skill's references as a named contract (gen-spec.json / readbacks.json / judging-worksheet.json) rather than re-derived.
- Control-arm cycles (challenger = a deliberate violation of a standing law) are the cheapest evidence a window can buy, but the gate reads backwards: the human must REJECT the challenger to confirm the law. Say so in the cycle's claim, or the verdict row will lie to the next reflection.
- A CORPUS-STUDY cycle (sample an A-tier work with the local recognizer, cross-analyze the annotations against the repo's own prompt-surface assumptions, convert the sharpest gap into a pair A/B) is fully automatable including the source pick: rank by corpus gap, acquire OFFICIAL promotional material only via yt-dlp with a candidate fallback ladder, and let the analysis numbers write the claim. Two studies on gravitone each produced a parked, judged A/B in ~1 GPU-hour; the second study's finding refined a rule the human had gated only a day earlier (one light source -> one dominant + one environmental layer), which is the loop feeding itself.
- When the studied property maps to a field the annotator already reads (composition, light_sources), the pre-filter stops being blind: state the expected readback delta in the claim so the cycle carries its own measurement.
- A corpus study that returns ONE clause-sized A/B has under-delivered (operator finding after two approved-but-thin studies). The study yield contract is three lanes, all owed per study: (1) styles - run the consuming repo's style-extraction engine over the frame set; (2) a BEAT MAP - technique distributions per position in the cut, the "which technique at which narrative moment" lesson the frames actually carry; (3) A/B claims as verification only. The report states what each lane produced or why it is empty.
- A dimension can ban a judge by operator instruction (video: no Gemini, cost). The park path must tolerate a missing judge rather than fabricate ties for it - absent is absent, and pick_rate alone carries the pre-human signal.
- Video cycles, first window (Wan 2.2 ti2v 5B, 8 clips, 0 failures): judge briefs over poster triptychs work, but must quote each pair's AUTHORED motion intent (a global one-readable-move standard punishes deliberate near-stillness), and serial continuity prompts must carry visual clauses only - editorial meta-language is rendered as content, the model painting the shot it was told it cut from.
- A one-seed serial video A/B flipped verdict on the next seed (meta-language continuity lost, then won, n=2 each). Video world-drift variance dominates single-seed pairs: a serial-continuity claim needs >=4 seeds per arm before it earns a cycle, and a contradiction across windows is a demand note, not a re-run.
- Train the engine the product ships. gravitone's first three image windows A/B'd prompt recipes against Flux 2 - the forge's lab instrument - while the product generates through its imaging router (Nano Banana); the transfer was never proven, and the operator caught it as rediscovering the wheel. Phase 3 must read the consuming repo's LIVE routing plan and generate through its chokepoint; a seedless product engine changes the protocol (k repeats per arm, distributions not rolls), and that is a protocol cost worth paying for product truth.
- Check the registry's provider-routing knowledge BEFORE choosing a runner: gravitone's Wan-over-H3 choice happened to be right, but for the wrong reason (H3's license excludes EU local deployment including outputs - a fact the registry already held while the session weighed models by VRAM).
- When a replication's failures are IDENTICAL in both arms, the variable under test is not the lever - close the claim as unmeasurable-by-that-variable and name the successor instrument, rather than running more seeds. (gravitone serial continuity: 3 windows, 10 pairs, phrasing a coin flip while both arms missed the shot's content; the successor is reference conditioning.)
- A window can run one GPU cycle and one vendor-API cycle in parallel - the budget is per-resource, not per-wall-clock.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-01 - gravitone-gcloud (Fable vs Opus bake-off, one local image cycle each)
- Two loops on one card is an interlock problem, not a convention problem. Both cycles honoured "one dojo loop per machine" through an agreed lock directory and still failed: a third job (another session's `serial-3seeds`) held the engine, one pass spent 10h48 on two renders, and the guard's refusal to recycle another job's engine was the first thing that told the truth. Phase 4 now reads the engine's run-id before launching and treats a foreign owner as `runner: busy`.
- "Demand outranks scan points" must name whose demand. `librarian-scan`'s deviation counts are fleet-aggregated; `visual-style-locking` showed six consumer deviations and 26 points while gravitone has no conformance pair for it at all. The Fable cycle ranked on that number; the Opus cycle read `.ai/conform-detail.json` and trained a subject this repo actually deviates on. Phase 1 now says so.
- A subject's live demand can be unreachable by the loop's only instrument: two of `frame-direction`'s three deviations here are a schema gap and a cross-frame lifecycle, which a seed-matched still cannot measure. Name the blind spot and pass rather than rediscover it each cycle.
- Whether a reference-governance claim is A/B-able depends on the runtime's reference channel, not on what the prompt says: Flux 2 via ComfyUI admits a reference only as an img2img latent, so `reference-role-map`'s ordering rule has nothing to order on the $0 lane. Check the channel in Phase 3, before the plan is written.
- `build-index.mjs` writes; a cycle on a shared or read-only registry checkout runs `--check`. `Out-File` stamps a BOM that `json.load` rejects; write manifests from Python or read `utf-8-sig`. The runner truncates the ComfyUI exception to 80 characters and starts the engine hidden; capture its stdout to `pipeline/foundry/logs/` or the cause is unrecoverable after a recycle.
- The chokepoint judge's blind is weak whenever the claim names the visible difference (an emblem versus a document); say so in the judge protocol rather than implying a strong blind.
- A lane bake-off truncated by the operator at ONE complete unit can still be decisive when the wins are categorical (figure present vs absent, interior vs exterior) rather than marginal - park it with the truncation named, and let the full-plan spec ride along for a resume.
- Big-model unit costs on consumer boxes are RAM stories, not GPU stories: H3 ref2va ran at 12x its reference rate and GLM-5.3 vision at 30x, both from host-RAM starvation paging experts. Record the hardware lever (RAM to spec) beside the verdict, or the lane reads as worse than it is.
- Before buying parameters, buy a better question: a 321B eye's apparent class-jump over a 27B local eye vanished when the small eye was re-prompted with the vocabulary the big one demonstrated (roles, shapes, layers - free prose, not enums). When comparing eyes, hold the PROMPT constant or the comparison measures elicitation, not capability; and a big model's cheapest contribution may be showing what to ask the small one.
