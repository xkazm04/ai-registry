## 0.1-1.0 - 2026-08-20 - media-generation + civic-intelligence + grant-funding + llm-observability (the validation arc)

- Counter-evidence was the highest-value lane per token in every run, and its honest
  negatives mattered as much as its hits: sync thresholds verified against broadcast
  standards and left untouched; EU award criteria found being *codified* rather than
  drifting; a "well-cooked" probe verdict with 4/6 techniques surviving. A lane that
  can only find problems produces fake ones.
- The instrument lesson, twice: a coverage gap reported at 0/267 was actually 267/267
  (the counter read a different shape than the parser emitted), and a planned
  "backfill pass" dissolved into a one-line counter fix. Verify the instrument before
  reporting a content gap.
- The demand matrix (consumer app use-cases × bundle index) broke flat-score ties AND
  falsified a forge consolidation call in its first cycle — it found a folded-out
  subject as a live-demand coverage hole. Coverage holes are forged, not deepened
  around.
- Batch quality held at 8 concurrent across two domains with zero Director reverts —
  but only with the hard scope rule (own folder; proposals for everything else) and
  the Director reading diffs, not reports. Two workers independently converging on
  one concern produced two complementary techniques plus mutual proposals instead of
  a collision; a third worker declined to mint a home-ambiguous technique and routed
  it up. The proposal channel is doing real design work.
- Three-round loop on a fresh bundle measured the curve the design predicted:
  high → moderate-high → confirmation-heavy, with workers self-forecasting
  saturation. Nothing was force-found; the dry=2 rule has data to run on.
- Session-wide search budgets exhaust under multi-round batches; training-data-first
  probes held quality — later rounds probe, they don't re-survey. Promoted to design.
- When a new domain neighbors an existing bundle, write the boundary contract into
  index.md and have every golden path name its neighbor in prose: 16 subjects landed
  against a 5-subject collision surface with zero duplication.
