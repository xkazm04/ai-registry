[`scripts/upstream-check.mjs --ledger`](../scripts/upstream-check.mjs); the columns are
derived, and the **Note** column is the only place to write by hand.

what did we see?** A repository checked and found unmoved still gets its date moved

deleted; an archived or retired tree is struck through and dated.

[`docs/plans/upstream-lane-2026-09-04.md`](../docs/plans/upstream-lane-2026-09-04.md);
