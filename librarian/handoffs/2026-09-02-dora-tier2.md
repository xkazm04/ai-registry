# dora, second tier: seven more subjects the scouts found and one wave could not carry

The first-tier wave forges five subjects (declared-process-graph, edge-queue-policy,
fault-signal-propagation, data-plane-transport-selection,
correlated-exchange-over-broadcast). The scouts returned candidates for seven more,
each with 4-6 technique slugs and a stated boundary, plus four EXTENDS. They are
recorded here as the forge's input, verbatim in intent, so a later wave starts from
the ranking rather than re-scouting. Anchors are in the scout reports summarised in
the source note; every anchor below was opened by a scout on this commit.

## Proposed laws (not added; three sightings each, director's call after the wave)

- *A guarantee stated for one path must be re-proved on every other path a message
  can take, or documented as lost* (queue policy across two transports; recording
  broken by the direct plane; ordering across two receive APIs).
- *A mismatch that would misparse must be refused at handshake, not discovered in
  the data* (topic-data protocol version; recording format floor; type hash in the
  channel key; inverse cases where positional encoding had no guard and the fix was
  field-order discipline).

## Deviations recorded for the wave that reaches them

- Installers verify no checksum or signature (`install.sh:28` pipes a download into
  an extractor) while the dependency policy is closed-world - a supply-chain deviation
  against `security/supply-chain` and `signed-artifacts`; do not lower the standard.
- No deployment-side shared-memory tuning exists anywhere in the infra territory;
  the only `/dev/shm` reasoning is a diagnostic WARN because the runtime falls back
  to heap copies.
