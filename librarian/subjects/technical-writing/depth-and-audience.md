and one `process` application. The subject had no subject note.

## Touch log

### 2026-10-10 - `/deepen`, single subject (run dp-da-1010)

Dispatched by Curator's projection of the attention scan on **single stack (process)**. The
rank was real. `check-currency` reported nothing expired or at risk, so the stack was the
whole finding.

Three lanes ran:
- **Field lane** on `personas-web` at `01eaee60` (origin/master), the same ten-post product
  blog the `article-structure` pass used. It counted terms of art per post, the rungs per
  argued post, and where the position sits. Nothing was committed to the project. The
  Director re-checked nine quotes and found one lane error: the lane said no tutorial
  states a limit, but the email tutorial does, in-step. It became the positive case.
- **Counter-evidence web lane.** Sources: Kalyuga et al. 2003; McNamara et al. 1996;
  O'Reilly and McNamara 2007; Shulman et al. 2020 (abstract via Crossref) with the method
  from Bullock et al. 2019 (OSU-hosted PDF); Martínez and Mammola 2021; Trudeau 2011-12;
  Hinds 1999; Camerer, Loewenstein and Weber 1989; Pew 2016; NN/g 2018; Chartbeat via
  Slate 2013 and Time 2014; Berger and Milkman 2012. The pages re-read were Diataxis
  "Explanation", Evans and Graham. Quotes were matched in raw text. Blocked: the
  academia.edu and SAGE copies of Shulman 2020, the Kimble judge studies, the Medium
  seven-minute post, and the Newton dissertation.
- **Training-data-only blind lane.** It independently reached Shulman 2020, McNamara 1996,
  Hinds 1999, Pew 2016 and "pick the buyer" on a vendor blog.

**Corrected:**
- The Graham citation in the golden path was rescoped. His reader is a non-native speaker
  and his target is needlessly intellectual words. He does not support term-in-a-clause.
- "A post that doubles in length loses the readers" was restated as a position effect. Pew
  found long-form completion near short-form. Attention falls down the page.
- The clause's "half a second" cost and "worth citing" are now labelled as judgment.

**Conditions gained:**
- **term-in-a-clause.** Plain word first. A definition did not restore fluency (Shulman), so
  the clause is for the terms the reader must carry (blind + web convergence, field
  simulation). The author's own term list is biased (Hinds), so a nearer reader checks it.
  Define once *per post*: the field renderer has no links, and five posts repeat the
  keyring line.
- **Golden path.**
  - A limit section: one text cannot be best for both readers (McNamara). It survives for
    skilled readers (O'Reilly and McNamara), and support larger than a clause gets its own
    post.
  - Name one primary reader specifically, with the floor kept for the other (Evans's
    remedy; field + blind + web convergence). On a product blog the second reader is a buyer
    outside the field, and the primary reader flips.
- **four-layer-depth.**
  - Tutorials keep the limits rung as a caveat on the action (field, three cases).
  - A positioning coda is not the philosophy rung (field: 6 of 10 closes).
  - A post longer than about two screens states its position early (web). That one is
    unmeasurable on this witness.

**Verified and left untouched:**
- The four rungs themselves. The blind lane found no validated framework of that shape,
  only analogues (Toulmin's qualifier and rebuttal, Rozenblit and Keil on mechanism). The
  ladder stays a judgment checklist, as written.
- depth-by-replacement and non-obvious-detail-selection. No lane refuted them.
- The Diataxis quote, verbatim. The subject drops "to the same question", which is
  harmless.
- Evans's pattern names.

**Declined:**
- The expertise reversal effect as a refutation of the two-reader article. It was measured
  in instruction (worked examples, integrated diagrams), not in article reading. It landed
  as the limit, not as a reversal.
- The Newton tapping study as curse-of-knowledge support. It is an unpublished dissertation
  known through secondary sources. Hinds 1999 carries the claim instead.
- Medium's seven-minute optimum. The page was not fetched, and a secondary summary only.

**Banked leads:**
- depth-by-replacement's "hold the length" assumes the post is already at its working
  length. A field post that calls itself a deep dive is 233 words with a stated eight-minute
  read. Return: when a second witness shows a post far below its own promise.
- Proposal for `evidence-and-sources`: on a renderer with no links, a source must be written
  as a method and a date in prose. Hypothetical scenario figures written in the indicative
  need the mood fixed, not a citation: 20 numeric claims in ten posts, none sourced. Return:
  that subject's next pass.
- No study compares an inline gloss, a glossary and no definition across reader expertise.
  Return: if one appears.

**Fact-check notice** (personas-web copy, kept apart from the language findings, unverified,
not acted on):
- One post says the data path is a single hop while the launch post discloses telemetry.
- One post claims compliance is simpler when "data doesn't leave your infrastructure",
  although prompts go to the model provider.
- The Slack tutorial promises a bot-token walkthrough it never gives.
- The feature doc's line references to the data file have drifted.
