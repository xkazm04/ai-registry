# contested-acquisition

First note. Forged 2026-09-04 by `/intake` from
[[../../sources/2026-09-04-wigolo]] via the XL spec at
[[../../handoffs/2026-09-04-wigolo-contested-acquisition]]: a new subject in
`integration`, 5 techniques and 1 source-tree application, drafted by one forge
worker under director review.

## It exists because a mature subject was thorough from stage two onward

`web-scraping` is well forged and its centre of gravity is rules-as-data
extraction into a dataset. Its own pipeline table gives Acquire one row, and six
of its eight techniques sit at Extract, Reconcile, Schedule and Detect. The
missing-stage-one shape the method predicts was exactly here: four load-bearing
mechanisms, all at the acquire stage, none with a home, and not variations of
one idea — a classifier, a precedence rule, a credential-reuse rule and a
trust-propagation rule.

The routing count fired on both v2.2 clauses (per-system 4 NONE in one system;
the same 4 sharing one `HOME IF NEW`), so the handoff was scoped to the system
rather than run as a `/forge` over the repository.

## The boundary is a discriminator, not a correction

`scrape-scheduling` models the refused state as a terminal run outcome — pause,
page a human, never retry — and that is correct for a scheduled harvest of a
target you must still be welcome at tomorrow. This subject governs the other
side of one question: a single-shot retrieval of one document a caller is
waiting for, where stopping is not restraint but a silent empty answer. Both
subjects now state the question; neither corrects the other. A later run that
finds them "contradicting" should read this paragraph before re-litigating it.

## What the worker decided, and was right about

Open question 2 was whether a sixth technique survived. It did not: half of it
was plain dependency inversion and the other half a single observation, so both
were folded into a golden-path section. The worker also chose `refused` as the
subject's word for the state (no collision with `web-scraping`'s `blocked`) and
placed the declared refusal as the Report stage's *normal output* rather than as
a failure of it — which is what makes the placement load-bearing, because
mis-filing it produces two named pathologies: refusal rate measured as a defect
rate, and callers who cannot distinguish "we tried" from a genuine attempt.

## Where it is thin

The subject is forged from **one** tree. Every technique is authorized by real
code read in that clone plus training-data convergence, and none by a second
independent source — which is the standing weakness of a one-source subject and
the reason `/deepen` should reach it before it is cited widely. The source's own
shortfall against the standard (a refusal that names the class but not the
elected set) is recorded as a lead in the source note rather than softened into
the technique.
