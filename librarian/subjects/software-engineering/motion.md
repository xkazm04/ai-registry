## The boundary this subject now states about itself

The preset contract was forged for **one curve, started by something, running to completion on its own clock** — and three classes it blesses elsewhere each break a different one of its assumptions. This was invisible to the slug map and to any summary; it was reachable only by reading the contract's required fields against the sibling technique's list of permitted engines.

- A **spring** has no duration and no easing role. `engine-selection` recommends one; `preset-vocabulary` demanded two fields it cannot fill; `taste-budgets` filed it as "one characterful curve" — three incompatible readings in one subject. Resolved: timing and character are declared **per track**, and a physics track declares a **settle bound** instead of a duration, because a gesture with no duration was escaping the millisecond entrance cap entirely.
- A **composite gesture** is more than one curve. Per-property tracks are now the stated common case.
- **Value-driven motion** has no clock of its own, so the one-shot policy that governs scroll-*triggered* reveals is a bug when applied to scroll-*driven* ones.

`gesture-decomposition` closes the missing stage in front of all three: the subject was thorough from "a preset exists" onward and silent on how a movement becomes one.
