Landed:

- `end-to-end-unit-of-optimization` — the unit of optimization is the unit the
  payer is charged for; anything narrower can be improved by displacing work
  across its boundary. Carries the workload-locality corollary (a result does not
  transfer between surfaces even on a shared harness).
- `compressibility-follows-the-producer` — classify by what produced the bytes,
  not by the bytes or their size; exact-preserve → lossless reorganization →
  lossy, and only the third for output repetitive by construction.
- `formatting-before-information` — information-preserving transforms first,
  because their saving is unconditional; the vestigial affordance and its
  per-emission-against-per-consumer asymmetry.
- `escape-hatch-usage-as-the-safety-metric` — the recovery path is the transform's
  own regression test, with the advertisement precondition and the conditional
  denominator.

## Boundaries stated, not linked away

- vs `prompt-assembly`: *assembly asks what fits; this asks what the result costs
  by the time the task is done.* Written into the golden path's opening.
- vs `prompt-assembly/elision-to-a-refetch-pointer`: recoverability vs production

  recoverability gates elision-vs-compaction) and run at different stages.

  run): an instruction contains deferrals that compression makes; a tool result
  contains none.
- vs `eval-harness/metric-role-contract`: roles vs scope. Independent failures,
  both required. The forge worker confirmed nothing in `eval-harness` owns scope.
