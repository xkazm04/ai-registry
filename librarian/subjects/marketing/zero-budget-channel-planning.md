## 2026-09-20 - intake-ltcwn-0920 (sponsored SEO review)

enumeration hunt run twice over this subject's own declarations.

- **The kind table's own claim invited the question.** `channel-family-taxonomy-by-lifecycle-kind`
  says "The table is the whole technique" and folds seven families into five kinds,
  every one of them *won by doing work*. A supply won by enrolling is in none of them.

  statement about labour. The quick-win rule surfaces the first untracked low-effort
  channel with fit >= 70, so a channel that pays in inbound links for no work is
  promoted by construction. The gate is therefore a stage upstream of the scoring,
  not a flag beside it - a flag on a ranked row still renders the row first.
- **The discriminator came from the primary, not the source, and inverted the obvious
  rule.** Not reciprocity - this subject already names the arrangement next door as
  legitimate - but whether the party whose page carries the link chose it and was free
  to qualify it.
- **Asymmetry worth remembering:** the bundle has a whole subject on whether a page set
  deserves to exist (`local-page-doorway-prevention`) and, until now, nothing on
  whether a channel deserves to be on the plan.
- **Second touch in twelve days, and the two touches share a root.** The 2026-09-16 run
  landed `owned-push-as-its-own-kind`, whose `use_when` ends "reading why a channel that
  costs nothing to send is still spending something". Both findings are about a channel
  whose cost is a spent asset rather than labour. Two instances; a third would make the

## Architecture review - 2026-10-31

First architecture review, against `6e9ae5cbb`. All 17 documents read in full.

- **Trigger:** a free-channel plan is being designed, generated or reviewed for a business
  with no media budget, and its ranking, grounding, measurement or onboarding order is in
  question.
- **Intended benefit:** the plan refuses inadmissible channels before scoring them, gives
  each kind its own lifecycle and query eligibility, grounds only on facts, and keeps a
  measured click from rewriting a predicted fit.
- **Failure if ignored:** the quick-win rule promotes a zero-effort link scheme, a
  newsletter or directory is dealt a target query, a sample row is asserted as a fact, and
  "0 clicks" reads as a verdict.
- **Boundary:** organic channels only. Paid channels' pacing, the depth of any one channel
  (profile, reviews, query intent) and cadence enforcement belong to sibling subjects.

Primary sources re-read this run. The search engine's spam policy supports the
admissibility technique's three examples and the carve-out for qualified paid links. Its
wording is "a third-party content owner", which the technique paraphrases as "the content
owner". The mailbox provider's sender pages support the 0.30% / 0.10% spam-rate figures
and the claim that reports degrade future placement. Its bulk-sender definition is "close
to 5,000 messages or more to personal Gmail accounts" within 24 hours, and the status is
permanent once reached. The technique says "exceeding five thousand messages a day". The
2017 forum-admin announcement confirms that the 1-in-10 self-promotion rule stopped being
enforced site-wide. That supports the taxonomy technique's "formally retired" note.

Disputed: the golden path's map-pack figures. It puts citation signals at about 7% and
links at roughly twice that. The 2026 survey's group weights are published only as a chart
image, which could not be read. Secondary write-ups of the same survey give map pack as
links 8% / citations 6%, and local organic as links 24% / citations 7%. The golden path may
have mixed the two rankings. This is `reverify`, not an edit. The survey's top-five AI
visibility list was read in text, and it confirms the three citation-shaped factors. The
"forty-nine communities" count was not traced to a source.

Internal inconsistency: one decision rule in `keyword-to-channel-visibility-plan` names
only listing and outreach as kinds that get no query, though the same file's body also
excludes push.
