**Corrected (sources verified by this run, not by the lane summaries):**
- **"The follow-up is where structured interviewing gets its validity" - refuted as worded.** Levashina, Hartwell, Morgeson & Campion 2014 (full text): probing research is "almost nonexistent", unlimited probing may lower reliability and validity and raise faking, limiting follow-up is a listed component of structure; planned probing is only a proposition. The four-minute floor stays as a planning heuristic that buys a planned, uniform probe. No per-question time figure exists in the sources read.
- **"Past a certain number a question only subtracts depth" and the one-hour ceiling - not evidence.** Thorsteinson 2018 (abstract): length unrelated to reliability and validity; more questions is a listed component of structure. Both reframed as the arithmetic of a fixed slot and a scheduling choice.
- **Disclosure "before assessment begins" in the opening - too loose.** Illinois 820 ILCS 42/5 (verbatim, statute mirror): notify, explain and obtain consent before the interview. NYC: notice 10 business days before use (DCWP page). EU AI Act Art. 50(5): at the latest at first interaction, applying 2 August 2026 (article text read; the omnibus date for Art. 50 rests on one secondary source, the Annex III date of 2 December 2027 is corroborated in other recruiting subjects here). The opening restates; GDPR Art. 13 and a recording announcement are the parts the opening can meet.
- **Unfed axis - right but too narrow.** Kuncel & Sackett 2014 (abstract): the largest source of dimension variance is a general factor. An assessment-centre analogue, labelled as such.
- **Debrief.** "Within the hour" has no source and is now hedged. Notes raise recall but not by themselves judgment accuracy (Middendorf & Macan 2002, abstract); rate each answer, take notes and do not discuss applicants are structure components (Levashina 2014); rate individually before discussion (OPM page, verbatim).
- **Defence question.** "Defensiveness reads worse than the gap" has no source outside kp's own scaffold string (exact-phrase search, and the application already quotes it from the repo). Gained two conditions: the same question for every record that shows the feature (ACAS and OPM read verbatim), and ask about the work, never the reason (EEOC caregiver and pre-offer disability guidance read verbatim; Correll, Benard & Paik 2007 cited from a snippet and from recall of the paper, not re-read).
- **Candidate reaction to an under-run interview** - stated as design reasoning; no study found.
