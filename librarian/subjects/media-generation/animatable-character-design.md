- **Counter-evidence (one lane, web, read verbatim).** The golden path argued
  "forgive almost nothing in a near-human face that moves slightly wrong".
  Kätsyri et al. 2015 (Frontiers in Psychology) review: the near-human dip holds only
  under specific conditions, movement modulation lacks consistent support,
  perceptual mismatch is well supported. Golden path reason corrected to cue
  mismatch, with the condition it implies (a stylised being is forgiven only
  while its parts sit at one realism level) and a new failure mode, "the
  mixed register". `default-attractor-rejection` gained the same condition;
  its "behavioural over photographic realism" line already agreed.
