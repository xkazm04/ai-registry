## 2026-08-31 — /intake breeze-tts2-local-voice ([[2026-08-31-breeze-tts2-local-voice]])

- **`spoken-delivery-direction` amendment — the audition script cannot catch a
  defect that is constant across scripts.** The technique's casting rule builds
  a fixed audition from four kinds of hard *passage* (dense numerics, a hedged
  claim, a rhetorical question, a joke) and casts the voice that survives all
  four. That enumeration is a completeness claim about what an audition tests,
  and it is entirely about what the voice **says**. A synthesized voice also
  carries what it was **recorded through** — microphone, room, and any broadcast
  processing present in its training audio, learned into the weights as part of
  the timbre. Every passage returns it identically, so it reads as the voice's
  character until the take is cut against the channel's existing audio. The
  amendment gives the audition a second half with a different shape: play the
  candidate *beside* a finished piece, and listen for whether it sounds recorded
  in the same place.
- **This is a boundary, not a duplicate.** The same source amended
  `software-engineering/llm-agent/runtime-and-io/voice-io/tts-pipeline` with the
  plumbing consequences — catalog integrity, compare surfaces, a fallback that
  is audible before it is visible. Cross-bundle links are forbidden and this is
  the correct shape for it: the *defect* is a property of the engine and is
  owned there; the *casting consequence* is owned here. Each side names the
  other in prose. A later run meeting this shape should recognise it rather than
  re-litigate which bundle owns it.
