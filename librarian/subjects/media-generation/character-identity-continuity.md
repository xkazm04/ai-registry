# character-identity-continuity

## Touch log

### 2026-08-27 - `/librarian run`, one technique earned against the floor

Dispatched at 9 points: 3 techniques against the design floor of 4, single
stack, never swept. The subject was **one day old** - a parallel `/intake`
session landed it that morning (`dc8c32e`) - so the worker was told to read
that commit first and to write nothing if nothing could be earned. A floor is
a prompt to look, not a quota, and padding a subject to clear a counter is
worse than leaving it at three.

**Landed `reference-shows-only-invariants`** on three-lane convergence: the
training-data lane, written before any search, independently produced
"copy-paste / pasted face", "anchor carries expression and light" and "neutral
multi-view anchor"; the research lane produced the measured trade-off; the
practitioner lane produced both the construction rule and the repair.

The claim: a character reference is a photograph of a face *doing something*,
and the conditioning channel carries the expression, eyeline, head angle and
key light alongside the identity it was supplied for. The failure is a
sequence with one performance in it, and it is invisible to every instrument
this subject owns - the identity axis scores it at its **best**, because a
recognition embedding is built to be invariant to expression, and the
difference axis is satisfied because the frames genuinely differ.

Subject is now 4 techniques / 3 applications; bundle 121 -> 122 techniques.

## Counter-evidence run, and what it changed

Four refutations against the landed claim, all survived; one reshaped it.

- *"Prompts can override the reference's expression."* Refuted by a primary
  source: ID-preserving methods fail to make a neutral-reference subject smile
  and often cannot adjust head pose or gaze.
- *"It is a tuning error, fixable by weight."* Refuted and promoted to the
  technique's spine - a 2025 benchmark over 435 multi-identity test cases finds
  face similarity and copy-paste bias tracking a single regression curve across
  most published methods. One dial, not two. Written with n, date and method,
  and explicitly not generalized to any particular generator.
- *"Solved in 2026."* Refuted; still generating papers. Gave the technique its
  cost-at-each-end shape. The extremes claim is marked **reported, not
  measured**, because this corpus cannot measure it yet.
- *"`identity-split-from-state` already owns this."* Survived on a real
  distinction: that technique governs the sentence and is enforced by deleting
  words. An image cannot be scrubbed, only composed.

Not asserted: lighting transfer as a mechanism. It could not be corroborated
above wiki-class sourcing, so it appears only as a probe axis and a human tell.

## Declined

- **Escalation ladder from reference conditioning to a trained subject adapter**
  (when to stop conditioning and start training). Not owned by any sibling, but
  2026 sources treat the two as *combined layers* rather than alternatives, so
  the decision is weaker than a rule. Banked, not written.
- **"The identity anchor imports its style"** - direct duplicate of
  `generated-shot-sourcing`'s "The anchor imports its maker's texture", which
  uses the import as a deliberate lever. Dropped; cross-referenced instead, so
  the same law now reads with opposite signs in two places.
- **Multi-view / turnaround anchor set** - not independently technique-sized;
  folded into the landed technique's variant-reference section.
- **Cast separability** (inter-character distance so two characters do not
  swap) - the ceiling rung of `identity-ruler-calibration` already computes it.

## Open leads

- **Owed, and the application says so: the coupling probe has not been run.**
  The technique tells you to measure once per generator how much state rides
  along with the face; nobody has. Until it runs this subject has **no
  measurement of its own on the state axis**. Cost: one lane of GPU minutes
  plus two schema fields in the consumer tree.
- **Return condition:** a generator whose coupling probe returns "the briefed
  state appears" at the extremes would demote the variant-reference section
  from requirement to optimization. Identity-state entanglement is an open
  research problem as of 2026, so expect movement.
- The consumer's consistency spike has an **unrecorded eyeline constant**
  ("looking slightly off camera") inherited by every downstream shot - the
  technique's own failure mode, live in the tree, and cheap to fix by
  recording it.

## 2026-08-28 - /intake run 35 ([[2026-08-28-media-generation-batch-4]])

- `reference-shows-only-invariants` gained **the ground's brightness as an axis hiding inside "plain"**. The composition list already said "even light and a plain field", and plainness is a claim about *content* - nothing behind the subject competing for the channel. Brightness is a second decision inside the first, and the default everyone reaches for, a white sweep, is the wrong end of it: a subject rendered against blown white was exposed for white, and dropped into a brightly lit scene the exposure compounds - the face returns washed and the shadow structure recognition runs on is flattened.
- Why it hides: the batch stays internally consistent and every shot is competently lit, so nothing reads as broken and no instrument in the subject reports it. The identity embedding is built to be robust to exposure, the difference axis is satisfied, and per-shot review passes. **The tell is a character reliably a little brighter than the room they are standing in.** Same argument as even light, applied to the axis most reference sheets never decide.
- Corroborated by convergence rather than by the source: mid-grey grounds are standard practice in turnaround and product capture precisely because they give the least-committed tonal centre.
- Untriaged from the same batch, with an anchor: **identity anchors are not only for faces** - any prop that must survive a cut needs its own sheet. Scope widening of this subject, whose four techniques all presume a performer. Nobody verified it; recorded so it is not re-derived.

## 2026-09-01 - /intake ([[2026-09-01-reallusion-ai-studio]])

- **Nothing landed; two catches, both against `reference-shows-only-invariants`, and both worth recording because the proposals were plausible.**
- Proposed as a missing stage-zero: the subject reasons from "an image cannot be scrubbed, only composed", which holds for a *captured* photograph and looks false for a reference *rendered* from a parametric source, where the state the reference carries is a render-time parameter. A vendor now productizes exactly that (multi-angle character sheets from a 3D character or a few images). **The technique already assumes minting, not choosing** - "the next person re-mints the reference", "Mint the variants as one batch from one sitting" - and already enumerates every render-time decision the proposal would have carried.
- Second proposal, and this one would have been **wrong**: that game-production's rule against synthesised views ("elaborated guesses, not evidence") inverts here, since an identity anchor has no ground truth to be wrong about. The technique already holds the correct version - a variant "minted *from* the neutral reference inherits whatever that reference got wrong, silently and everywhere" - which is the same inheritance rule without the overclaim. The proposed discriminator would have licensed the failure that sentence warns about.
- Residue, not landed: the "size the variant vocabulary rather than maximize it" argument is priced against a photographic **sitting**, where each variant costs. From a re-renderable source that marginal cost approaches zero - and the recommendation survives anyway on the inheritance risk, so nothing moves. Recorded so a later run does not re-derive it.
- Corroboration read but not needed: the multi-view identity literature's answer to a state-laden reference is **canonicalization** into a re-renderable neutral artifact, which is this technique's composition list arrived at from the other side.
