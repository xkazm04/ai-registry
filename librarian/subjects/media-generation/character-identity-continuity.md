**Landed `reference-shows-only-invariants`** on three-lane convergence: the

The claim: a character reference is a photograph of a face *doing something*,
and the conditioning channel carries the expression, eyeline, head angle and
key light alongside the identity it was supplied for. The failure is a
sequence with one performance in it, and it is invisible to every instrument
this subject owns - the identity axis scores it at its **best**, because a
recognition embedding is built to be invariant to expression, and the
difference axis is satisfied because the frames genuinely differ.

Subject is now 4 techniques / 3 applications; bundle 121 -> 122 techniques.

## Counter-evidence run, and what it changed

Four refutations against the landed claim, all survived; one reshaped it.

- *"Prompts can override the reference's expression."* Refuted by a primary
  source: ID-preserving methods fail to make a neutral-reference subject smile
  and often cannot adjust head pose or gaze.
- *"It is a tuning error, fixable by weight."* Refuted and promoted to the
  technique's spine - a 2025 benchmark over 435 multi-identity test cases finds
  face similarity and copy-paste bias tracking a single regression curve across
  most published methods. One dial, not two. Written with n, date and method,
  and explicitly not generalized to any particular generator.
- *"Solved in 2026."* Refuted; still generating papers. Gave the technique its
  cost-at-each-end shape. The extremes claim is marked **reported, not
  measured**, because this corpus cannot measure it yet.
- *"`identity-split-from-state` already owns this."* Survived on a real
  distinction: that technique governs the sentence and is enforced by deleting
  words. An image cannot be scrubbed, only composed.

Not asserted: lighting transfer as a mechanism. It could not be corroborated
above wiki-class sourcing, so it appears only as a probe axis and a human tell.

## Declined

- **Escalation ladder from reference conditioning to a trained subject adapter**
  (when to stop conditioning and start training). Not owned by any sibling, but
  2026 sources treat the two as *combined layers* rather than alternatives, so
  the decision is weaker than a rule. Banked, not written.
- **"The identity anchor imports its style"** - direct duplicate of
  `generated-shot-sourcing`'s "The anchor imports its maker's texture", which
  uses the import as a deliberate lever. Dropped; cross-referenced instead, so
  the same law now reads with opposite signs in two places.
- **Multi-view / turnaround anchor set** - not independently technique-sized;
  folded into the landed technique's variant-reference section.
- **Cast separability** (inter-character distance so two characters do not
  swap) - the ceiling rung of `identity-ruler-calibration` already computes it.

## Open leads

- **Owed, and the application says so: the coupling probe has not been run.**
  The technique tells you to measure once per generator how much state rides
  along with the face; nobody has. Until it runs this subject has **no
  measurement of its own on the state axis**. Cost: one lane of GPU minutes
  plus two schema fields in the consumer tree.
- **Return condition:** a generator whose coupling probe returns "the briefed
  state appears" at the extremes would demote the variant-reference section
  from requirement to optimization. Identity-state entanglement is an open
  research problem as of 2026, so expect movement.
- The consumer's consistency spike has an **unrecorded eyeline constant**
  ("looking slightly off camera") inherited by every downstream shot - the
  technique's own failure mode, live in the tree, and cheap to fix by
  recording it.
