# Run 2026-08-22-3 - the first evidence-earned technique edits

Two claims reached twice, independently, inside [[2026-08-22-2]] — deepen's
convergence rule met within one wave — and this run places them in the
technique layer. Four files edited, by the director, not a worker: the upper
layers are the transplantable standard, and their voice is the product.

## What changed, and where each sighting came from

**Persist the verdict where the next boot reads it.** Sighted as a migration
runner's failed-at mark written beside the version, and as an engine writing
its journaling mode into the store's own header. Landed in
`migrations/error-propagation` (outcome 3 now has a stronger form than a
message: a durable mark, set before the step and cleared only by its success,
with its two corollaries — every advancing entry refuses while it stands, and
clearing it is a named repair) and in `embedded-db/journal-and-durability-modes`
(the contract's two halves have opposite persistence; "assert at boot" is two
acts at two frequencies, and the pool that skips the per-connection half has
downgraded durability for every caller after the first).

**Lifecycle state is not health state.** Sighted as a replication daemon
reporting `replicating` from the bare fact that its loop is running, and as an
ORM deciding "already inside a transaction" from the shape of a handle. Landed
in `sync-replication/sync-observability` (running/enabled/open published under
a health key is worse than no status — health derives from outcomes only) and
in `data-access/transactions-and-units-of-work` (discriminating ambient state
by shape conflates "cannot open a boundary" with "already inside one"; the
discriminator must be typed).

Each edit names its sibling sighting in the abstract — "the same move appears
wherever…" — so a reader of one technique learns the claim is cross-cutting
without the technique naming a product. Purity grep over all four: clean.
Status stays `forged`; nothing self-promotes.

## Discipline

Read every file in full before drafting. Edits inserted into the section each
claim belongs to, in that file's sentence rhythm, not appended as a ledger.
Seventeen single-sighted leads stay banked in the subject notes; the next wave
is where they earn or lose their second sighting.

## Declined

Applying any of the single-sighted leads, however attractive — "close streams
with their error", "checkpoint OK is not checkpoint complete", "the dead
instrument" — on one tree's evidence. The rule that made these two edits
trustworthy is the one that keeps those out.
