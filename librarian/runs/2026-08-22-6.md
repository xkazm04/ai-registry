## Family 1 - fail closed when the gate itself breaks (two sightings)

Sighted as a progressive-delivery controller treating a dying measurement
pipeline as abort-worthy, and as a webhook gateway refusing the create when
its dedup lookup or lock fails. Landed in:

- `self-healing/auto-rollback` — a new decision rule, "a broken gate is a
  verdict, not a gap": a change never keeps running unjudged because the
  judge died; repeated instrument failure resolves toward abort, honest
  ambiguity toward pause-for-a-human. Confidence gates autonomy; absence of
  evidence gates it harder.

  fails, the delivery is refused, not admitted: fail-closed costs one sender
  retry, fail-open mints a duplicate nothing can unmint.
