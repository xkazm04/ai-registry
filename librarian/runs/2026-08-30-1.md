# Run 2026-08-30-1 — the worklist was one defect wearing thirty costumes

A `/librarian` sweep that found its own instrument wrong, fixed it, and only then
dispatched. The corrected ranking is the run's main artifact; the six subjects are what
it bought.

## The finding

The scan's top thirty was **entirely demand-driven** — every entry ranked on consumer
deviations. Before ranking on that, provenance was checked, per the rule the
[[2026-08-29-4]] run earned. It did not survive.

`librarian-scan.mjs` summed `deviations` across contributor files. But a deviation is a
**state of a tree** — "how many places this repo falls short" — and one fleet checked out
on two machines writes two signals files describing the same shortfalls. They were:

- both files declare the same `app`, with stacks identical on every capability but the
  node major (24 vs 20);
- both filed a **byte-identical `llm-observability` block**, fourteen hours apart;
- **17 of the 31** software-engineering subjects both named carried identical deviation
  counts (`agent-memory=14` on each, `markdown-vault=11`, `diff-comparison=10`).

Summed, the corpus read 550 deviations. The floor is **370** — a 1.49x inflation.

**It did not inflate the ranking, it reordered it**, because the 1.49x was concentrated
in 33 of the 89 demand-bearing subjects. `quality-gates` sat 13th when it belonged 2nd;
`fleet-orchestration`, `terminal-multiplexing` and `import-normalization` occupied a top
ten they had no claim to. A wave dispatched at that head would have spent six workers on
a ranking artifact.

**No gate could have caught it.** `check-signals` validates *shape* — one file per
contributor, counts and slugs only — and passes both files by design. Nothing in the
schema can prove two contributors independent: `contributor` is a self-declared id, and
one fleet on two machines is a legitimate thing to be. **A gate that checks each file
against the spec cannot see a defect that exists only between files.** That generalizes
past this instrument and is written into [[standard]].

## The fix

Events sum; states do not. `consults` still sums (two installations reading a subject
four times each produced eight reads). `deviations` and a citation's `gone` take the
**max across contributors**, carrying `deviationsSummed` beside them. The scan now prints
demand as a **range** (`14–28 consumer deviation(s)`), names duplicate blocks loudly, and
ranks on the floor — because a floor cannot manufacture a worklist and a sum can.

CI runs `librarian-scan --top 10`, so the instrument change is gated by the same run that
uses it.

## Structure: saturated, and that is the other half of the finding

Strip the demand term and the entire corpus is flat — **maximum 5 points across 338
subjects**. Zero missing `use_when` (2142/2142), zero expired, zero at-risk, zero cap
breaches, zero taxonomy errors, zero subjects under four techniques, zero dry streaks ≥2.
One subject with no application (`media-generation/trailer-structure`).

So demand was not merely *a* signal in the ranking, it was the **only** one — which is
exactly why its defect was worth a run.
