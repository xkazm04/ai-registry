# Run 2026-08-21-2 - the use_when backfill

The work [[2026-08-20-1]] banked as "the first real unit of work for run 2", executed:
every technique in `software-engineering` that carried no `use_when` now carries one.
The registry is at full `use_when` coverage for the first time.

This was the systematic pass the founding sweep argued for, not a fleet of per-subject
dispatches. One defect repeated across 106 subjects is one finding, and the standard
says so.

## What was done

A bulk-model pass, orchestrated from this session and executed by a free preview model
over an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Two new scripts carry it: `scripts/lib/fleet.mjs`
(the dispatcher every future bulk pass shares) and `scripts/fleet-use-when.mjs` (this
task). The shape is deliberately three commands - propose, review, apply - so nothing
reaches a document in the same breath as the model that suggested it.

Every proposal was read before it was applied. Nothing was accepted on the model's word.

## What the instrument says now

`software-engineering` at 768/768 `use_when`; the registry at 1528/1528. The bundle's
attention points fell by roughly two thirds and the registry's by half. Those are
records of this moment, not inputs to the next run - recompute them.

## Four incidents, each of which changed a script

**The first full pass wrote nothing.** It was killed at 125/627 having assembled results
in memory to save once at the end. Results are now appended to a JSONL checkpoint the
moment they land, and a re-run skips what the file holds. A pass over this bundle is now
interruptible on purpose.

**A dead model silently halved throughput.** One roster member began returning internal
errors for every call. Because the dispatcher spread items across the roster by index,
half of them burned a full attempt and its timeout on a model that was simply down -
throughput fell by half and nothing said why. Roster order is now PREFERENCE order, and
a model that fails repeatedly is benched for the rest of the run.

**A case-insensitive denylist rejected good work.** Matching product names with `/i`
threw away "an uncertain removal candidate" (the word, against a product called
CandiDate), "one slice to react to another" (the verb), and "notifications that resolve
state" (a video editor). The bundle gate already made the opposite choice for the same
reason; this validator now matches it. Because the checkpoint stores what the MODEL said
and re-derives the verdict on every read, fixing this cost no re-inference at all.

**The first 608 proposals were legal and wrong.** Correct shape, purity-clean,
parser-safe - and a median of 75 characters against this bundle's own median of 43. Every
one would have read as a different author, which is the bar `[[standard]]` sets and no
gate checks. The prompt had given a LIMIT ("under 90 characters") where it needed a
TARGET, and had mandated present participles that the corpus itself does not use: its own
best entries are symptoms, like "rung labels keep flipping between runs". Regenerated
with three real exemplars from this bundle quoted at the model. The second pass landed at
a median of 45, and about half its entries are symptom-shaped.

## Declined

**Per-subject notes for the 106 subjects touched.** The founding sweep's own argument
applies to its record as well as its work: this was one systematic finding, and 106 notes
each saying "got a use_when in the backfill" would be noise in a vault whose value is
that everything in it was worth writing down. The domain note carries it.

**Promoting anything past `forged`.** Nothing here was reconciled against a codebase, and
a routing hint is not evidence. The ladder stays empty and honest.

## Noticed, not fixed

out of the existing catalog and only refreshes `invokes30d`. Three skills added in a
parallel session sit in `skills/` and are absent from `catalog.json`, and `--check` stays
green because it compares a fresh build of the same blind logic. This is the same class
of defect [[2026-08-21-1]] recorded against the bundle counts, in the lane next door.
Left for a run that owns the skills lane.
