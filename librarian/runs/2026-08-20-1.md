## The finding that dominates everything else

`software-engineering` carries 1,778 of the 2,028 attention points in the registry, and
almost all of it is one defect repeated: 632 techniques with no `use_when`. Every other bundle is at 100%.

That is a **systemic** finding, not 106 individual ones.
The standard's own rule applies: when one defect dominates the worklist across dozens of
subjects, the fix is one systematic pass, not forty dispatches. Ranking it as a per-subject
worklist would have produced forty workers each writing four frontmatter lines, which is
the shape of a loop that looks busy and is not.

Banked as the first real unit of work for run 2, with its return condition: a single
authored pass over `software-engineering`, subject by subject, writing the consult
triggers a routing agent selects on.
