# Run 2026-08-27-1 - the first content dispatch in media-generation, and a counter that would have produced the wrong work

Full-corpus sweep, then `run` scoped to `media-generation` on the operator's
call. Three workers, all landed. The corpus-wide finding is in none of them.

  when it was taken.

## Dispatched

**[[sound-effect-generation]] - the registry's last zero-application subject.**
The dispatch was re-scoped mid-flight, and that is the run's methodological
lesson. The counter said "the only subject of 335 with no application". The
subject note said the 2026-08-26 intake run had left it that way *on purpose*,
with a written return condition: the tree had no effects pass, so nothing could
be verified honestly. So the worker's question became **"has the condition
arrived?"** rather than "bind the techniques" - and the answer was *partially*:
the tree rendered its first effect on 2026-08-26 (`762aef7`, "2.000s briefed,
2.038s delivered", n=1, by hand), but the seam's only caller is a playground
bench and no effects lane exists in the pipeline. Three applications landed;
`layered-element-assembly` was **declined outright** because the generator
returns one file and the bench drops the previous take. An invented binding
would have been worse than the zero.

*A counter is a reason to look. It is not a reason to make the counter go away.*

**[[character-identity-continuity]] - 3 techniques against a floor of 4**, and
one day old, landed by a parallel intake session that morning. The worker was
told a floor is a prompt to look and to write nothing if nothing could be
earned. One was earned on three-lane convergence, after four refutations that
all survived and one that reshaped the claim's spine. Three other candidates
were dropped - one a duplicate of a sibling's technique - and one banked.

**[[generative-provider-routing]] - the third dispatch, chosen on converging
signals rather than points.** It scored 3. It was picked because three things
pointed at it at once: never swept, holder of the bundle's only drifted
application, and holder of the inverse of a proposal banked earlier the same
run. All three jobs returned work.

Job 1 cleared the drift: the consumer's router grew 303 -> 424 lines the day
after the last verification, so 13 line citations were corrected and 6 held.
Two claims had genuinely moved - the error union is ten kinds now, not nine, and
a gap the document declared open had been closed by a probe landing three days
earlier. One over-claim was narrowed on the way past.

Job 2 adjudicated the banked proposal **down**, which is the outcome worth
recording. The omit-when-undefined idiom appears in two modules, but it is one
author's idiom copied across them - one voice, one sighting, which fails the bar
for a new technique. The inversion framing was rejected on mechanism:
`non-silent-elimination`'s unit is a vendor *candidate*, and an omitted request
field eliminates no candidate. It landed instead as an enumeration repair - the
elimination has an **outbound door** - on the principle that *an enumeration gap
is repairable on a single sighting, because the sighting's job is to prove the
door exists, not to establish a rate.*

Job 3 was not dry, and it is the run's second instrument finding:

> The folder's OTHER application had rotted identically against the same source
> file and had escaped every scan the registry runs, because it carried no
> `verified_against` at all. **A missing witness does not read as unknown. It
> reads as not drifted** - the one wrong answer of the three.

One folder, two rotted documents, one invisible. That is why the registry-wide
drift number is a lower bound over 11% of the runtime corpus rather than a
count, and it is now written into [[standard]] as the gap's demonstrated case.
Bundle-wide: **630 of 704 runtime-bearing applications (89%) are invisible to
drift detection**, three bundles at 100%.
