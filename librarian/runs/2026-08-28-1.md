# Run 2026-08-28-1 - the remedy was forbidden, and the bar was a dashboard

Two follow-ups from [[2026-08-27-1]], taken together on the operator's call: the
witness backfill, and `generated-output-grading`. **One of them could not be run,
and finding out why was the more valuable half.**

## The backfill did not happen, and must not

Yesterday's amendment to [[standard]] named the remedy for the currency gap as
"one backfill pass that stamps witnesses from each consumer's declared
manifest". Executing it meant first resolving where the versions live. They are
not in the manifest - `signals-collect.mjs` lifts them from each project's
`package.json`, and the fleet figure is a **max across projects**. Following that
thread reached `docs/rkb-profile.md` §3.1, which forbids the whole idea in terms:

> `verified_against` is written going forward, never backfilled. Only something
> that has actually read the cited tree can state it truthfully.

And it is right. Stamping a version out of a consumer's `package.json` would
assert that a document's citations *were checked at* that version when nothing
checked them at all - a runtime fact laundered into a verification claim, which
is `unmeasured-is-not-pass` wearing a maintenance hat. **`check-currency.mjs` had
carried a comment saying exactly this the whole time**, and the gap was drafted
without reading it. The instrument disagreeing with the standard is worth more
than either agreeing quietly.

So the gap is **expensive** to close, not cheap. The only honest closure is
re-verification: something reads the cited tree, resolves the citations, and
writes the witness as a by-product - a worker per subject, not a script over a
corpus. The same run then demonstrated it costs what it costs: the dispatched
worker re-resolved eleven citations in one application, found all eleven holding,
and wrote `node@24` as a by-product. **One application, one worker.**

What was available instead was the reporting half, and it landed:
`check-currency.mjs` now reports `driftUnknown` - a `drift blind` column, the
denominator in the drift header, and a block naming bundles with no witness at
all. Its existing honesty note fired only when NOTHING carried a witness, so it
went silent at the first one and stayed silent through the next six hundred,
exactly when a reader most needs the denominator. No document changed, and none
may.

## A bar is not a dashboard

The second correction was not planned and is the run's other lesson.

Yesterday's demand gap carried a table - two contributors, seven of eight bundles
witnessed, 87 consults, six deviations - and built an argument on four bundles
being *witnessed and silent*. Overnight a sibling session's collector re-ran. The
reading this morning: **three bundles witnessed, 43 consults, 63 deviations, and
not one witnessed-silent bundle.** Neither measurement was wrong. The signals
lane is a 30-day rolling window over installations that regenerate on their own
schedule, so **a reading of it has a shelf life of about a day.**

Both gaps now state what is true regardless of the reading and point at the
instrument for the reading. The three-state `demandKnown` argument survives and
is sharpened by having watched its own example evaporate - the difference between
"nobody needs this" and "nobody told us" collapsed into one boolean twice in two
days.

The knock-on: **the registry worklist is transformed.** 26 subjects now carry
deviations and the head of the list is entirely `software-engineering` -
`accessibility` 33p, `quality-gates` 27p, `error-handling` 23p, `authorization`
20p. Yesterday's reasoning about what a deviation *means* is unchanged (it is the
consumer falling short, not a subject defect), but at 63 it is the dominant
signal in the corpus and the next sweep should be scoped there.

## Dispatched

**[[generated-output-grading]]** - the banked proposal adjudicated as an
**amendment** to `vision-model-grading-schema`, with both rejected homes reasoned
out rather than merely declined. Not `unconditional-fail-criteria`: a performer
freeze fails that technique's own three tests, decisively on two. Not its own
technique: the trigger would duplicate an existing `use_when` verbatim.

The adjudication turned on a **second voice found inside this corpus**.
`cinematic-language` briefs the performer thoroughly - enumerated verifiable
beats, "state gaze direction in every brief" - while `vision-model-grading-schema`
contained zero occurrences of performer, expression, gaze, eyeline or briefed.
That reframed the claim from "schemas omit performers" to an **asymmetry between
briefing and grading vocabulary**: a schema assembled from an existing vocabulary
inherits only that vocabulary's enumerated half and drops the half its source
expressed as prose. Two artifacts, one fleet, corroborated in shape and **no rate
written** - and three sibling schemas in the same tree correctly declined as
instances rather than voices.

The deepen pass was not dry: a version witness written by genuine
re-verification, a rotted citation pointing at the wrong function, and a
measurement that contradicted itself by 9x - a closing caveat still saying `n=4`
while the title, body and table said `n=36`, because the commit that upgraded the
measurement retracted the pilot in the body and left the caveat unedited.

## Declined

- **The backfill**, above. Recorded as forbidden rather than as not-done.
- **A repo-wide line-ending fix.** The worker reported the checkout is CRLF on
  disk against an LF index (repo-local `core.autocrlf=false` under a system
  `true`, no `.gitattributes`), so any file a worker edits first presents as a
  whole-file diff - its four files showed as 514+/466- before it normalized them
  back to 58+/8-. That nearly made diff review impossible, which is the
  Director's one non-delegable job. **Not fixed here**: committing a
  `.gitattributes` renormalizes the entire tree, which is a fleet-level decision
  with a large blast radius and belongs to the operator, not to a sweep.

## Where media-generation stands

20 subjects, 128 techniques, 48 applications, 8 version witnesses, **44 attention
points - up from 38**, because a sibling session landed `trailer-structure` (6
techniques, no applications) yesterday. The registry had gone exactly one day with
every subject reconciled against real code. That subject is the bundle's clear
next dispatch at 9 points, and the pattern is worth naming: **a bundle's health
