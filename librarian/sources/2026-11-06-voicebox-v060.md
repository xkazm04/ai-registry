## What landed

- **`mcp-tools/techniques/name-grammar-of-the-narrowest-hop`** (new). The
  protocol allows dots and 128 characters and gives a dotted example; the
  function-calling grammars between host and model allow neither; a host that
  validates refuses the *whole* listing. Rule: spell in the narrowest grammar,
  validate once over the listed catalog, and never list a relayed foreign name
  verbatim - alias it or dispatch it by argument. Primary fetched in-run: the
  protocol's 2025-11-25 tools page, section "Tool Names" ("dot (.)" in the
  allowed set; `admin.tools.list` as a valid example). Two applications:
  - `python--name-grammar-of-the-narrowest-hop` (source tree): four dotted
    names, the whole list refused (#790), a flag-day rename with no alias
    (0.6.0's one breaking change, `CHANGELOG.md:92`), and still no check.
  - `rust--name-grammar-of-the-narrowest-hop--personas` (fleet): the browser
    bridge validates page-declared names against the *standard's* alphabet
    `^[A-Za-z0-9_.-]{1,128}$` - the proxy the technique names - and is immune
    only because page tools reach the model as an argument. Its declared next
    change (`hands.rs:221-227`) would list them.
- **`voice-io/techniques/render-acceptance`, amended: "Exhaustion with a
  repair step".** The source reversed "ship nothing on exhaustion" in 0.6.0
  (commit `a2b453a`, #964): an engine with a trim step now ships the trimmed
  clip unexamined. Measured on the tree's own two functions (extracted by AST,
  five synthetic renders with known ground truth): the detector rejected the
  repaired clip **0 of 5** times while **3 of 5** had lost half the requested
  speech; and the trim's 1.0 s cut bound sits below the detector's 2.0 s, so a
  legitimate 1.3 s pause truncates the render **on the ordinary path** with no
  detection. The amendment keeps the file's rule and states the three
  conditions under which it inverts. The application was rewritten around the
  reversal and the table.

   broke on first execution.** The numpy compatibility hook's `NameError` was
   fixed (`6955272`), after which the now-running patch wrapped a function
   before its host module finished importing and raised; a second fix had to

   `force_offline_if_cached` was revived into the load path

   not checked to completion. Shape: **reviving an inert guard is shipping
   new code; its first execution is its first test.** Sighting 2 of prior lead
   3, but from the same source, so not independent. **Return when** a second
   source shows a revived guard failing on first run.
