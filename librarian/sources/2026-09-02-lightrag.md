## Directions (Phase 7.6) - the peer shape

`politicas/.ai/directions/2026-09-02-lightrag-comparison.md` (committed `52a86e8`): **39
unique points** (62 before consolidation; the four faces of one provenance model and the
three clauses of one path rule counted once) - adopt 5 / adapt 6 / **keep ours 21** /
different forces 14 - plus **7 seeded points corrected against the trees**, two of them
against the front half's own structural counts (15 backends behind five base classes, not
18 behind four; 21 root test modules, not 22). The governing asymmetry, in the study's
words: politicas is a ledger of public allegations and the source is a retrieval index
over arbitrary text; every place politicas looks more expensive - three provenance axes,
a closed vocabulary instead of a vote, a rejection instead of an auto-created node - is
that asymmetry paid for on purpose. The peer is the *other side* of the corpus's own
discriminator, which is why it was worth more than a same-design peer. Three proposals in
the schema: `entity-id-grammar-door` (civic-knowledge-graphs), `durable-parsed-text-sidecar`
(import-normalization), `offline-path-oracle` (retrieval). The study worker stalled on
an optional trim after writing everything; nothing was lost.

Not proposed, with reasons (companion §10): kp (document-heavy but nothing in scope names
an entity graph or a retrieval engine), ascent and pof (list `retrieval` as a candidate
but do not face the forces), tracklight (the judge-swap point is coverage, not direction).
