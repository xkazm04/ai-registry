## Design record

One system: an efficiency layer that composes through a harness's public extension
events. Grouped by mechanism.

**D1. Delegated log reading admitted only as hash-bound, byte-exact quotes.**
- decision: a cheaper model reduces a diagnostic log to a receipt; the receipt is admitted only when every quote is a contiguous substring of the archived source, the status agrees with the runtime's error flag, a failing log carries failure evidence, and the receipt is smaller than the source.
- forces: delegation without trusting a fluent summary; the reader keeps diagnosis authority; the source must stay recoverable.
- buys: misattribution impossible by construction; every fallback journaled from a closed set.
- rejects: paraphrase; any receipt on schema/quote/size failure (falls back to the original).
- where: `src/sol-pi/extensions/evidence-preserving-reducer/receipt.ts:119 "!body.includes(quote)"`; `src/sol-pi/extensions/evidence-preserving-reducer/receipt.ts:141 "missing-failure-evidence"`; `src/sol-pi/extensions/evidence-preserving-reducer/index.ts:142 "receipt-not-smaller"`.
- stage: tool-result event, before the frontier model reads.
- corpus: prompt-assembly/summary-evidence-gate models the forces (local, model-free admission of a model-written reduction); fleet-orchestration/completion-claim-verification models the citation-resolves-against-a-ledger half. Boundary, not NONE: the gate names "misattribution to a real range" as the price of running without a model, and the quote-only form removes it.

**D2. Large tool results archived at capture, sent in full twice, then projected to a handle with exact paged recall.**
- decision: rewrite only at the projection layer; archive by content address; two full sends; placeholder with head/tail excerpt; recall by byte offset.
- forces: repeated replay of a large result on every later request; resume and fork must keep recall working; storage must not be a symlink hazard.
- buys: access without repetition; projection bytes stable across processes (send count derived from the transcript).
- rejects: editing history; summarizing the result.
- where: `src/sol-pi/extensions/observation-pack/observation.ts:15 "FULL_SENDS = 2"`; `src/sol-pi/extensions/observation-pack/index.ts:13 "never edits history in place"`; `src/sol-pi/extensions/observation-pack/index.ts:146 "priorAssistantCounts[index] = assistantCount"`.
- stage: context projection, per request.
- corpus: CATCH - elision-to-a-refetch-pointer + fold-only-acknowledged-evidence + tiered-history-projection (fetch by identifier). One boundary recorded in the application: archiving at capture makes the "tool that has since changed the world" class elidable.

**D3. Compaction authorized by break-even against a horizon estimated from the run's own boundaries.**
- decision: at each completed plan step compute `write × (ratio − 1) / (archive − memo)` and compact only when it is at or below the expected remaining requests, estimated from requests-per-completed-boundary, lower-bounded under small samples, capped by window growth; first compaction gets 2× horizon, later ones need 1.5× margin and carry unrepaid premium; the window reserve overrides.
- forces: a prefix cache prices writes above reads; compaction late by default; a fixed threshold ignores whether the rewrite repays.
- buys: compaction only when the run will live long enough to collect; every refusal carries a closed reason.
- rejects: a threshold at a window fraction as the only trigger; live price lookup (the ratio is session-fixed policy).
- where: `src/sol-pi/extensions/online-context-compact/economics.ts:152 "const breakevenRequests ="`; `src/sol-pi/extensions/online-context-compact/economics.ts:70 "export function estimateRemainingRequests"`; `src/sol-pi/extensions/online-context-compact/economics.ts:156 "const combinedBreakevenRequests ="`.
- stage: on every plan-step completion.
- corpus: **NONE** for the horizon and the debt. context-budgeting has the break-even in inclusions with the count left to the operator; amortized-compaction-cadence has the reclaim-size gate and says the cache trade is "decided by the deployment". HOME IF NEW: prompt-assembly (technique, not subject).

- where: `src/sol-pi/extensions/online-context-compact/extension.ts:35 "export const DEFAULT_KEEP_RECENT_TOKENS = 20_000;"`; `docs/compatibility.md:40 "default of 20,000 tokens"`.

## Phase 7.5 - applied

- **compaction-horizon-breakeven** - `experiment`, `not-better`, seam chosen to
  falsify. The rule replayed over 166 real coding-agent sessions on this registry's
  checkout (8 threshold compactions, window near 1M tokens): every compaction repaid
  within 0.8–2.2 requests against 34–791 remaining; the gate declines 0 of 8 at ratio
  12.5 and at ratio 3. What it refuted: the premise that the economic test bears on
  threshold compaction at all. The technique carries that boundary. Return: a fleet
  harness that compacts at plan boundaries.
- **summary-evidence-gate (quote-only receipts)** - `experiment`, `better`, seam: this
  registry's own review contract ("open one cited line"). Arm A, the manual sample,
  verifies 1 anchor per document. Arm B, `scripts/check-anchors.mjs`, verified 63 of 63
  anchors across the three applications - after its first pass reported **39 of 39
  unresolvable** on the director's own first draft (mechanism-relative paths, and line
  numbers taken from filtered reads that were off by 10–20 lines). The instrument also
  caught its own self-check quoting a literal that was in its source. Return: none.
- **Floor beside the target (method)** - simulation over three real `better` rows in
  `librarian/applied.md`: `boundary-applied-stop` (target: tools run, 8 → 7; floor:
  the answer's correctness, held by construction because the 8th result was never
  read), `gate-state-lifetimes` coverage fix (target: outcomes counted, (0,0) → (1,3);
  floor: existing monitor tests, ran green), `lens-versioning-as-invalidation` (target:
  verdicts staled 0 → 131; floor: **not declared** - whether the 131 re-judgments
  change any verdict was never measured, and the row's own next change is to make it
  measurable). Two of three would have been written the same; the third would carry
  its floor as "undeclared" in the return condition. Falsifier: a `better` row where
  the floor, once named, was already visible as broken - none found in the three.
