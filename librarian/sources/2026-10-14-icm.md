first-party prose (64,715 words against ~39,000 of non-README markdown), so crate and

## Memory lane: which arm is this?

The benchmark configuration (one store per test unit, 512-token chunks stored verbatim,
lexical plus vector rank fusion, no model at write) is the ladder's **verbatim + hybrid
retrieval** arm (0.87 on the replayed year). The product as installed adds rule-based or
model extraction at hooks, so in use it sits nearer the two-tier rows. The published
LoCoMo figure (`README.md:18 "92.9% on LoCoMo (1,540 questions, mean of three runs), level with Hindsight (92.0%)"`)
is a **lead, not evidence**, and the tree itself shows why: at `k = 50` it reads
`bench/amb/README.md:165 "| icm k=50 (published run) | 24,064 | 37.4 M |"` tokens per
question against `bench/amb/README.md:160 "| full-context | 39,971 (21,700 to 52,290) | 61.9 M |"`
for the whole conversation, with gold recall at that depth of
`bench/amb/results/locomo10-retrieval-legacy-vs-v2.md:19 "| Gold sessions any@50 | 99.2% | 99.6% | +0.5 pts |"`.
The figure measures the reader over most of each store. The tree instruments the
full-context comparison and does not publish its result.

Where the source is honest, it agrees with the lane's claim 2 from the vendor side: its
recall reference quotes the lexical floor on the same unit beside two other vendors' headline
recall figures and finds keyword search alone within two points
(`bench/amb/results/longmemeval-s-session-recall-reference.md:45 "either. Plain BM25 on the same unit is within 2.0 points of MemPalace's any@5"`).

No arm was built: the one mechanism here that is new to the corpus (a period in the question
as a vote) is expressible on the four-call contract, but the ladder's probe set has no
question that names a period, so an arm would tie by construction. It was measured instead
on a shrunk instrument, retrieval coverage over the fixed store with past-state questions
built from the world's own superseded facts (Applied, below).

## Design record (Phase 2d)

**D1 - Consolidation replaces only what it read, under a per-item check.**
- decision: a consolidation names the memories it read (with the summary it saw) and
  replaces only those, in one transaction, refusing with a stale result if any moved.
- forces: the summarizer is a model call held for seconds to minutes while hooks keep
  storing; prompt input is capped, so a topic does not fit; a whole-topic replace deleted
  what arrived mid-pass and what the prompt never held.
- buys: no write lost and no forgotten memory resurrected through a summary, without
  refusing every pass on a busy store.
- rejects: replace-the-topic, broken on purpose in 0.11.0
  (`crates/icm-cli/CHANGELOG.md:44 "needs `ids` to replace anything"`).
- where: `crates/icm-core/src/store.rs:163 "Replace the memories a consolidation read with the summary it wrote"`,
  `crates/icm-store/src/store/memory.rs:920 "return Ok(icm_core::Consolidated::Stale { changed });"`,
  `crates/icm-mcp/src/tools.rs:2020 "Only ids are known here, not what the caller read under them: the"`
- stage: the publish step of a model pass.
- corpus: `agent-memory/durable-store-failure-posture` § the long-window instance models the
  forces at store granularity. Boundary case: the comparison's granularity. Amendment.

**D2 - Recall filters inside each arm before the cut, and widens a saturated arm.**
- decision: lexical and vector arms fetched deep (four times the limit, floor 100), the
  caller's scope filter applied to candidates, an arm refetched four times deeper (up to three
  times) when it came back full and too few survived.
- forces: the old 40-candidate pool with a post-filter starved scoped recalls on two surfaces.
- buys: a scoped recall over a shared store that cannot be emptied by its own filter.
- rejects: the legacy engine (`docs/architecture.md:437 "It scores at most 40 candidates per query, applies filters after that cut"`).
- where: `crates/icm-store/src/store/ranked.rs:86 "A filter can discard most of a saturated arm. Fetch deeper rather"`
- stage: per-lane execution, before fusion.
- corpus: `retrieval/ranking-budgets` § Overfetch, then select, plus `hybrid-lane-fusion`
  (re-imposition). Models it; saturation-triggered widening is a boundary the technique does
  not state (row 3, untriaged).

**D3 - A period named in the question is a third ranked list, never a filter.**
- decision: parse a period from the question, precision first, against the caller's instant
  and offset; vote for the fetched candidates whose event time falls in it, fused by rank
  beside the two arms; a window matching nothing changes nothing.
- forces: a question about "back in March" ranks the newest statement on the topic first;
  a parse is a guess and a wrong filter empties the answer; a replay or benchmark asks about
  a past year; imported history has no ingestion-time meaning.
- buys: past-state questions reach the old statement; a wrong parse costs a position, not
  the answer (at a wide enough cut).
- rejects: temporal filtering (`crates/icm-core/src/temporal.rs:11 "A wrong window costs more than a missed one, so the rules give up when a"`).
- where: `crates/icm-store/src/store/ranked.rs:59 "adds a third list: kept candidates whose"`,
  `crates/icm-core/src/temporal.rs:8 "never against the wall clock, so a benchmark"`
- stage: between query decomposition and fusion.
- corpus: **NONE.** Nearest: `retrieval/query-decomposition-before-the-lanes` (specifics and
  themes, not a time constraint), `agent-memory/observation-clock` (the write-side clock).
  HOME IF NEW: `retrieval`. Converges with `[[2026-09-17-mem0]]` D5 (untriaged there).

**D4 - The session opens with the importance tiers that are never pruned.**
- decision: a deterministic snapshot plus a pack of critical and high memories (or a cached
  model briefing of them), built for prompt-cache stability.
- forces: no model call at session start; the prompt cache prefix; "critical = never forget".
- buys: a cheap, cache-friendly baseline every session.
- rejects: a query-dependent opening recall.
- where: `crates/icm-core/src/wake_up.rs:113 "matches!(m.importance, Importance::Critical | Importance::High) || is_pref"`,
  `crates/icm-store/src/store/memory.rs:586 "Never prune critical or high importance memories."`
- stage: the always-include tier.
- corpus: `agent-memory/decay-and-forgetting` (the always-on tier governed at least as
  strictly) and `recall-injection` (packing). Catch; the tree inverts the rule.

**D5 - Near-duplicates merge by appending; supersedence lives in a table agents never use.**
- decision: same-topic cosine above 0.95 appends into the old entry, importance never
  lowered; a keyed facts table closes old values on an entity and key.
- forces: a similarity threshold proves closeness, not identity (two distinct dialogue turns
  scored 0.98 and one erased the other).
- buys: zero data loss at the merge door.
- rejects: overwrite on similarity.
- where: `crates/icm-core/src/store.rs:100 "conversation scored 0.98 and the second overwrote the first)."`,
  `crates/icm-store/src/store/facts.rs:73 "UPDATE facts SET superseded_at = ?1 WHERE id = ?2"`
- stage: the write door, and the store's supersedence.
- corpus: `agent-memory/consolidation` (contradiction before duplicate; a code-owned key).
  Catch.

**D6 - Hooks are split by cost, and the workers they spawn cannot re-enter them.**
- decision: the prompt hook recalls keyword-only and read-only; the tool hook only counts and
  queues; extraction runs in detached singleton workers at session end, which mark their
  subtree and start the summarizing agent CLI isolated from settings, tools and persistence.
- forces: a hook on every prompt and tool call; an agent CLI child fires the same session-end
  hook, which spawned an unbounded loop.
- buys: millisecond hooks and no self-sustaining spawn chain.
- rejects: inline extraction in the hook.
- where: `crates/icm-cli/src/main.rs:4694 "Reentrancy guard (#322): if this hook is firing inside an"`,
  `crates/icm-cli/src/summarizer.rs:404 "about 41k input tokens per briefing for a prompt of 3-5k."`
- stage: capture.
- corpus: `agent-cli-transport` (child isolation) and `agent-runtime-assembly/nested-run-is-not-its-parent`. Catch.

**D7 - A benchmark arm names its engine or is refused.**
- decision: the adapter, every runner and the job template refuse to start without an
  explicit engine, and record what went over the wire.
- forces: the binary's default engine changed between releases under one label.
- buys: two builds measured under one name measure the same code.
- where: `bench/amb/README.md:373 "builds, the previous engine before), so an unnamed run measured one thing on an"`
- stage: evaluation.
- corpus: `agent-memory/baseline-ladder` (the score's predicate) and
  `cross-provider-benchmark-operations`. Catch.

**Routing count.** Systems: the store and its recall (D1, D2, D3, D5), the agent integration
(D4, D6), the benchmark tree (D7). NONE per system: store 1 (D3), integration 0, benchmark 0.

## Applied (Phase 7.5)

**named-period-vote / personas / experiment / better.** The product's retrieval has keyword,
vector and recency lanes and nothing that owns a period. Measured on the memory harness's
hybrid verbatim arm, the arm closest to the source's design: 140 past-state questions from the
70 superseded facts, coverage only. Target at cuts 5/10/40: A 78/94/105, full vote 104/112/127,
quarter vote 101/103/114, filter 103/110/125. Floor: the parser fires on none of the 194
standard probes (asserted), so their contexts are identical. **The seam was chosen to falsify**
the source's claim that a wrong window costs less than a missed one, and it partly did: with
the window one month early, a full-weight vote lost 24/35/1 against the filter's 24/36/41. At
a cut of five the source's design is a filter in all but name, and the source's default cut is
five. A quarter weight lost 7/8/0. Committed as a harness check, personas `4933593909` on main,
not pushed.

**durable-store-failure-posture (read set) / pof / code / better.** The shared notes file's
prune already carried the store-wide check, applied by an earlier run. **Chosen to falsify**:
its window is milliseconds, so the read-set rule could add nothing. Paired harness with writers
injected into the real I/O: with an append during every attempt's planning the shipped check
gave up after five attempts with nothing written, the read-set check finished in one with no
line lost; no concurrent writer, byte-identical file, archive and output; an append at every
temporary write refused under both (the residual window is unchanged); an in-place edit
re-planned under both. Magnitude stated: the window removed is the planning time. pof
`64a5452e` on master, not pushed (that checkout is 2 ahead, 10 behind its upstream; not
reconciled).

**baseline-ladder / personas / experiment / not-better.** Seam hunt as a second source: the
harness's hybrid arm carries a 40-chunk constant that binds before its 6,000-token budget and is
missing from its run header, the exact signature the technique names (1,918 of 6,000 tokens).
The source moved its own engine off a 40-candidate pool. Lifting the cap to 200: covered gold
118/123 both, stale exposure 101 both, 5,865 tokens. The cap costs nothing here; the ladder's
0.87 row is not cap-bound on coverage. Return: put the cap in the arm's header.

the replay that would falsify it named in its return condition.

## Rust practice (operator's secondary focus)

`trust-base` (landed the same day from duvet) already owns the rule this tree most needs. The
census: 73 `unsafe` sites, 64 of them environment writes in tests (the 2024 edition makes those
unsafe), none with a note; 5 of the 9 production sites carry a `SAFETY` note that names its
dependency well (the environment write before any thread starts). Two findings went into the
application: the test suite's mutex is declared three times, so three modules each hold "the"
lock while running in parallel, and the sqlite-vec registration's transmute sits under a lint
allowance that removed the prompt to name its target type. A sibling run (vox) landed a census
technique in `trust-base` the same hour; this tree is a second instance of its shape, recorded
here rather than written into their subject while they hold it. Worth learning from, with no
action owed: `max_importance` (a re-store may raise and never lower), a cross-process claim
guard on a refresh marker, the read-only store open, and char-boundary truncation applied at
every slicing site after one panic.
