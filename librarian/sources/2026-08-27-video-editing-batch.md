# Video editing batch, 2026-08-27 - the cut becomes source, and the probe that opened the editor

Run 27, dispatched by the operator to feed the connected app's Cut step
ahead of the render seam. Small batch, dense yield: the four videos
converge from three unrelated directions on one idea the corpus carried
only as an escape clause - the edit authored as a compilable source
document - and the run closed with a live headless probe of a professional
editor on the dev machine.

## Accepted (4)

1. **`cut-compiled-from-source`** → NEW technique in video-assembly
   ("compiled" had zero prior art): the edit as a declarative composition
   (styled elements + timing data) rendered to video. Edits are source
   edits; style swaps scoped; edit styles reusable as standing contracts;
   iterations are versions; and the critique pass READS the composition
   (interval arithmetic over collisions and boundaries) instead of
   watching pixels. Promoted from partial-regeneration-seams' symbolic-
   source escape clause to an authoring decision. 2 sources + corpus-
   internal convergence.
2. **The voice-led cut derives from the timed transcript** → amendment on
   `derived-turn-markers`: captions, overlay entrances, cut points and
   highlight selection all derive from word timings; three authors reached
   it independently (an overlay compiler transcribing before placing; an
   editor exporting timed subtitles as the design handoff; an assistant
   doing highlight selection over the editor's transcript).
3. **The plan lands as annotations in the working surface** → amendment on
   `edit-plan-over-regeneration`: proposals as native markers with notes,
   approved in place, applied by a deterministic script - propose/approve/
   apply with the mechanical step containing no judgment. 1 source, strong
   convergence with operations-not-artifacts and refuse-before-apply.
4. **Generate to see, rebuild natively** → decision rule on
   `anchored-variation-slate`: when the production tool can express the
   winner, adopt the idea and rebuild it natively; the generated render is
   a probe that never enters the timeline. From a 10-year editor who
   priced both paths (the direct-adopt loop: weekly-quota cost, fiddly
   change round-trips, half-hour misses on 5-second animations).
