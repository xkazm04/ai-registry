## Leads

- **Law candidate: "a denominator counts what was asked, not what was answered."** First sightings, all this run:
  - the faithfulness verdict count
  - claim-by-passage matrix stacking that spreads one verdict across four claims
  - factual-correctness recall that mixes two claim sets

  Tracklight's `agreement` deliberately takes the opposite choice ("measured over the samples that actually scored, not the requested count"), and that is defensible there because parse failures are reported beside it. That is exactly the boundary a law would need to state. **Return:** a second independent run, from a different source, reaches the same rule.

## Directions (7.6 / 7.7)

The fleet map lists five candidates for grounded-answer-scoring: tracklight, pumper, politicas,
personas and athena-everywhere. One proposal was written.

**Proposed: tracklight** (`.ai/directions/2026-09-15-grounded-answer-scoring.md`, `c53352e`). Scope is
"score with judges"; the judge has input, reference and output slots and no evidence slot. The
operator **accepted** it at the gate (`e82d2f1`). It was built on `direction/grounded-answer-scoring`
in a worktree. **Outcome: merged into tracklight main as `8d1bfa5` (branch commit `3cf08ba`), not
pushed.**

What was built:
- an evidence entry point for the rubric judge
- a `grounding` dimension kind: cut, then per-claim check, scored over claims issued; zero claims void
  the dimension
- per-claim verdicts stored in the score detail
- an instruction-hash pin on every verdict

Gate (the director re-ran the engine suite): core 291, engine 212, runner/api/store green, fmt, clippy.

**Size crept:** 17 files and +1032 lines against the proposal's ~350. About 310 lines are tests and 55
are docs, which leaves roughly 670 of product code, twice the estimate. The creep is recorded, not
hidden.

**Worker overrides:**
- The granularity pin lives on each verdict, not on the rubric version, because the instructions live
  in code.
- The evidence slot lives on the engine case, not on stored datasets.

Verdict `unmeasurable` until a labeled grounded golden set exists. Runner callers do not pass evidence
yet, so a stored grounding rubric is refused by name. That wiring is the next context.

**Directions not proposed:**
- **athena-everywhere:** the gap is coverage (capture tool results), recorded as its next change.
- **politicas:** claims are bound to sources structurally, which the subject's own "when not to use" excludes.
- **pumper, personas:** no judge scores answers against supplied evidence; their scope does not face the forces.
