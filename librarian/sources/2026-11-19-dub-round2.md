## Research

**Can ElevenLabs make a voice from an image? Not natively.** The Design a voice API reference
([api-reference/text-to-voice/design](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-voice/design),
fetched 2026-11-19) takes these fields:

- `voice_description` (text);
- `model_id` (`eleven_multilingual_ttv_v2` default, or `eleven_ttv_v3`);
- `text` (100-1000 characters);
- `auto_generate_text`, `should_enhance`, `seed`, `quality`;
- `guidance_scale` (default 5);
- `loudness` (-1 to 1, where 0 is about -24 LUFS);
- `stream_previews`;
- on v3 only, `reference_audio_base64` with `prompt_strength`.

There is no image parameter, and the
[Voice Design guide](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-design.md) describes a
text-prompt tool only. A web search found no image-to-voice feature. ElevenLabs' own character
workflow sends the picture to another vendor's avatar tool. So image-to-voice here is two steps: a
vision model reads the sheet into a vocal brief, and the brief becomes `voice_description`.

The guide gives three rules that shape the description:

- Its order is language and accent, gender and age, a quality phrase ("studio-quality recording"),
  persona and emotion, then timbre, pacing and delivery.
- FX words (reverb, echo, phone, tape) stay out of it.
- Preview text is charged once per generation, not once per preview.

That matches the corpus's
`character-vocal-brief` (identity, function and arc, each trait sent to one carrier). The row's
vision prompt asks for identity only, because function and arc come from the scene, not the image.

**ElevenLabs features that bear on the mix:**

- Voice Design's `loudness` sets a voice's level at design time.
- [Sound effects](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/text-to-sound-effects/convert) take
  `duration_seconds` of 0.5-30 and a `loop` flag. That makes an ambience bed, which matters because
  removing the native track removes the rain. The docs page states no price.
- Nothing in the API pans or mixes. Placement is our job.

**Sides, by convention.** A course wiki
([UT RTF318](https://wikis.utexas.edu/display/rtf318/Speech+perception)), a 1986 AES paper
([elib 4690](https://www.aes.org/e-lib/browse.cfm?elib=4690)) and mixer forums agree on four points:

- Dialogue stays near centre.
- Two characters talking to each other get at most a slight off-centre lean, about 30 % in one
  forum report.
- Off-screen lines are the usual exception.
- Moving speech is more jarring than a visual cut.

This is practitioner opinion and no measurement. So the row's conventional arm leans 0.3 and holds
each line still, and a separate wide arm exists only so the operator can hear what he asked about.

**ffmpeg on Lynx (9.0.2 full build):** `pan`, `stereotools`, `afir` (convolution with an impulse
response stream), `aecho`, `equalizer`, `highpass`, `lowpass`, `loudnorm`, `sidechaincompress` and
`adelay` are all present. The syntax for `aecho`, `afir` and `adelay` was checked on
[ffmpeg-filters](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html), and the rest with `ffmpeg -h filter=`.

**Corpus first.** `registry-query` on dialogue panning and room returned game spatial-audio subjects
(`two-d-vs-three-d-spatialization-choice`, `room-type-to-acoustic-profile`) and
`video-assembly`. None of them covers seating a dubbed line in a filmed scene. Loudness is covered:
`node--loudness-and-peak-acceptance` holds gravitone's -1.5 dBTP export margin. The query for voice
from a description returned `character-voice-design`, and its `character-vocal-brief` is the method
STEP 1-2 follows.

## Rows banked

**`shift-replica-dialogue-el-dub-r2`** (held, media queue, CPU, estCpuMin 30) runs on the same
15.417 s A1 cut, and the native track is in no arm. It works in eight steps:

1. **Vision brief.** `codex exec -i` reads each character sheet with a fixed prompt, and the image is
   the only character input.
2. **Description.** A fixed template builds the description. The persona comes from the scene, and
   the accent is fixed by the row, because no image carries one.
3. **Voice Design.** `eleven_ttv_v3`, seed 1119, three previews per role.
4. **The man's re-cast** follows a rule set before anything is heard: the lowest preview whose
   median f0 lies in 85-145 Hz. If none does, one more design call adds a baritone sentence and
   guidance 8.
5. **Speech.** Two voices are created (two slots), and the six lines go through round 1's
   `speak-ts.mjs`.
6. **Placement.** The file's energy onset goes on the native onset, which removes the
   with-timestamps lag of up to 307 ms.
7. **Sides.** The seat reads the speaker's side from the cut frame at each line's onset and
   cross-checks it with a red-stain centroid.
8. **Mix.** `mix-r2.mjs` runs per-line gain by direction, tone EQ, constant-power pan held per line,
   a seeded small-room impulse response through `afir`, an optional ducked rain bed, and two-pass
   linear loudnorm to -14 LUFS.

The arms:

- **a:** round 1's s track with no native audio.
- **b:** new voices, dry and centred.
- **c:** b mixed, with sides at 0.3.
- **d:** c with a rain bed.
- **e:** c with sides at 0.8.

The spend is capped in the row:

- Voice Design: at most 3 calls, about 520 audition characters.
- Voice creation: 2 calls, 2 voice slots.
- Text-to-speech: under 500 characters (about 207 expected).
- Sound effects: 1 call, with credits read from the header.
- Forced alignment: none, because round 1's alignments are reused.

**`shift-lead-gravitone-dialogue-mix`** (held, no queue) proposes gravitone `lib/cut/dialogueMix.ts`.
It would be a pure filter-graph builder in `narrationMix.ts`'s style: per-line gain, tone, side pan,
room, an optional bed ducked by `DUCK_FILTER`, the native track dropped by default, and loudness at
`EXPORT_LOUDNORM`'s target. Its seam is `layArgs`, and its tests are a golden graph string and a
rendered L-R and LUFS measure. It is blocked on the replica's verdict, because the stage values are
one replica's guesses until heard. The replica's `mix-r2.mjs` is written stage by stage so that a keep
lifts it into this module. Under `authority.md` this lands in gravitone on its current branch as a
seam with green gates. It is not built now.

**Second seam (proposal, no row).** `voiceDesign.ts` `designVoices` takes a written `brief`. A
vision brief from the character's sheet would feed that field if the replica's voices are kept.

  creates anything.
