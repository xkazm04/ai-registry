## M7: what the lander's read found

The miner scored this 2/0/2 as a new technique for `video-assembly`, with a fetched primary.

- **Primary, re-fetched by the lander:** Remotion's CameraMotionBlur doc. "The final image is an
  average of the samples." "For a value of 10 the component will render ten frames with different
  time offsets and combine them into a final image." Shutter angle defaults to 180; recommended
  samples 5-10; "The technique is destructive to colors"; quality costs render time.
- **No knowledge home.** A grep for shutter, subframe and motion blur across `knowledge/` finds
  only drawn cues: the smear in `motion-cue-budget` and "slide with motion blur" (horizontal blur
  plus skew) in `event-card-stack`. No technique says how a frame-pure renderer gets shutter blur.
  `seek-stable-composition-authoring` supplies the precondition: subframe offsets need a frame
  derivable from its time alone.
- **The promotion read found convergence the miner did not have.** The consuming project's
  music-clip craft notes (2026-10-30, written from three research passes after the operator called
  a board "a PowerPoint presentation") already state the rule: average N renders across the shutter,
  N 5-8, 180°, N times the cost, and **use it only on whip pans and zoom-throughs**. That is an
  independent source, so GAIN takes +1, and the score is 3/0/2, which clears +3.
- **And it contradicts the miner's seam framing.** The project wrote the rule and never built it.
  Its span renderer makes one render per frame, and the film starter's post chain has DOF, bloom,
  AO and gate weave but no blur pass. So the gap is the measurement, not the knowledge.
- **The render clause holds it.** The home is `video-assembly` and the claim changes how output is
  prepared, so it is render-bound and may not land without a render verdict. It becomes a lead with
  held row `shift-proof-shutter-blur-ui-ad`. **Discriminator to settle there:** the project limits
  blur to fast camera moves for cost. Mira lists its absence as a fault across a whole ad. Type-led
  flat graphics may smear. The proof renders the kept 2D ad whole with and without blur.
