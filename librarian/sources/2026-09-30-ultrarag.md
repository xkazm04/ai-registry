- Application **python--padded-slots-are-misses--ultrarag** (read; anchors 3/3 held).
- Application **python--paired-per-case-testing--ultrarag** (executed the tree's own permutation functions on
  synthetic scores; anchors 8/8 held). Measured: the same two runs give mean difference 0.280, p = 0.0000 under
  the tree's union-and-zero pairing and 0.000, p = 1.0000 under the intersection; five repeat calls on eight
  fixed differences gave p = 0.065 to 0.120 (unseeded); a constant effect gave p = 0.0 exactly.
