## Design record

Five entries landed; the decision and forces are in the tree's terms.

1. **decision:** the compaction target is an aim and the window is the limit.
   **forces:** a headroom-derived target pinned at a floor; a protected tail counted in
   messages against a target counted in budget; the last rung hands off to a pass that
   drops the head first. **buys:** the task and the live turn survive. **rejects:** only
   raising the floor (shipped as the interim step; the structural fix is written up and
   not shipped). **where:** `src/context.rs:886 "return keep_within_budget(messages, target);"`
   and `docs/evals/compaction-defaults.md:302 "only the budget is a hard limit."`.
   **stage:** the last rung of the compaction ladder. **corpus:** NONE
   (history-compaction models pairing, resume and size invariants, not target against
   protected set). Landed: `compaction-target-is-an-aim`.
2. **decision:** compaction is idempotent and deterministic so the cached prefix stays
   byte-stable. **forces:** a marker that shrinks per pass, a counter in marker text,
   a cap applied late. **buys:** rewrites only when history changed (415 to 70 over a
   2,400-turn replay, source-reported). **rejects:** compacting constantly to a small
   context to keep the hit rate. **where:** `src/context.rs:658 "The marker is charged against"`,
   `docs/concepts/prompt-caching.md:190 "Markers carry no drifting state."`.
   **stage:** every compaction rung plus the append path. **corpus:** NONE for the
   mechanism (cache-breakpoint-allocation and amortized-compaction-cadence price the
   rewrite and take it as given). Landed: `compaction-is-a-fixed-point`.
3. **decision:** the cache-affinity key is derived from the standing prompt, not the
   first message. **forces:** compaction replaces the head with a constant marker.
   **buys:** a key stable across a compaction. **rejects:** mixing in the first user
   message, which shipped once and was caught in review. **where:**
   `src/provider/traits.rs:755 "fn derived_key_survives_compaction_that_rewrites_the_head"`,
   `CHANGELOG.md:2046 "Session identity is"`. **stage:** request assembly, key
   derivation. **corpus:** NONE (fingerprinting-and-cache-keys is the session
   staleness stamp, a different key). Landed: `cache-affinity-key-from-the-unrewritable-layer`.
4. **decision:** one facade owns every host-dependent primitive; a lint bans the raw
   calls only in the portable job; the portable job runs on the real host with a
   timeout. **forces:** calls that compile everywhere and panic or hang on one host.
   **where:** `src/rt.rs:23 "pub trait MaybeSend: Send {}"`,
   `.github/workflows/ci.yml:89 "A broken timer or spawn shim hangs under Node rather than failing."`.
   **corpus:** partial: io-free-core is the answer where the module is logic over
   events; this is the case where the module drives work. Landed: `host-primitive-facade`.
5. **decision:** a weekly, non-blocking mutation lane with a triaged baseline over the
   modules where a silent failure costs most. **forces:** survivors need a human; a run
   is hours. **where:** `.github/workflows/mutants.yml:52 "fail-fast: false"`,
   `docs/evals/mutation-baseline.md:144 "The cause was deterministic."`. **corpus:** NONE
   (no mutation technique in the corpus; unreached-decisions-pin-nothing is the manual
   probe). Landed: `mutation-lane-with-a-triaged-baseline`.
