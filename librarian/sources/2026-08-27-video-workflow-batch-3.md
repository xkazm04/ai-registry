# Video workflow batch 3, 2026-08-27 - one voice, and the corroboration ran the other way

Run 26. Third batch in two days, and the first that is a **channel corpus**:
all 11 videos one creator. That inverts the batch economics - within-batch
convergence is worth nothing (one voice, one sighting), so the value ran in
two channels only: mechanics the corpus did not own, and independent
corroboration of runs 24-25 landings from a voice none of those runs
contained. Both paid. Six amendments, no new techniques, no fetches.

The dispatch half of this run is the operator's: the connected media app is
about to grow a video/image generation step, and the batch series exists to
feed that. See Cross-repo below.

## Accepted (6)

1. **Paired-panel anchors** → generated-shot-sourcing, rung 3 construction
   rule: head and tail frames generated separately disagree about everything
   the prompt did not pin, and the model glitches mid-interpolation;
   generate both anchors as two panels of ONE image (same scene, only the
   camera changed) and split. Shared provenance leaves nothing to reconcile.
2. **The blocking frame is the camera's schematic** → scene-grammar's
   schematic section: angles prose cannot place (over-the-shoulder is the
   canonical case) are placed in a neutral 3D stand-in scene, exported as a
   frame, and scoped hard as "camera reference only - ignore the
   placeholder's content." Third member of the annotate-don't-describe
   family (positions, routes, now pose).
3. **The built previz plate** → motion-plate-library's fourth source: a 3D
   blockout with meter-exact camera path and human-pace motion, viewport
   render as the plate; an LLM driving the 3D tool makes it cost a prompt.
   Second independent sighting of the plate concept (run 24 was a different
   creator) - the technique's first corroboration.
4. **Boards chain, panels repair, count is a pacing contract** →
   storyboard-grid-conditioning (third independent author for the
   technique): board N+1 conditioned on board N; single-panel repair with
   identity re-stated; a clip that runs out before the last panel is a
   pacing defect fixed in the brief or by splitting the board.
5. **Voice minted from either direction** → identity-split-from-state voice
   section widened: extract from the first accepted take OR author up front
   in a voice tool and condition the animation on the audio; the ordering
   (reference exists before the second line) is the rule, not the route.
   Second independent sighting of the voice-reference amendment.
6. **A sample set must agree before it teaches** →
   style-onboarding-from-sample: multi-sample capture reads back what the
   frames SHARE; a mixed set captures a hybrid nobody chose; read the block
   against each sample and evict outliers at collection time.

## Method notes

- **The channel corpus is a real sub-class**: one voice, so in-batch
  convergence is void, and triage leans entirely on corpus-vs-source and
  cross-run corroboration. Its yield profile: amendments and corroborations,
  not new subjects - a channel teaches one practitioner's method, and the
  method's novel mechanics are the only rows worth deep verification.
- Sponsorship density was the highest yet (every video sponsor-linked, two
  platforms). The strip test continued to delete the sponsor cleanly; the
  tool-ranking video was the only one whose substance was mostly unusable
  (dated comparative facts).
- 0 fetches, third batch running. The 3-fetch budget has not been touched
  by this source class across 31 videos.
