# ai-literacy-superpowers Concepts, 2026-08-28 - four sentinel pages were one missing stage

Thirty-five explanation pages from one plugin family's documentation site: the
flagship harness-engineering plugin (34 pages), a diagnostic-legibility plugin (2),
a model-cards plugin (1). Every page describes a mechanism the authors built and the
reasoning for its shape - "the boundary is the mechanism", "this is the third time
the project reached this design point". The operator's dispatch carried two things:
mine the batch with only `real gap` rows advancing, and add a paired-proof phase to
the cross-repo lane. Split at Phase 3, both executed.

## Class, and the expected yield said out loud

many catches, possibly one cluster. That is what arrived.

## The finding: the cluster

Four pages - cadence governance, adversarial review, decision archaeology, the
challenge-refine protocol - each mapped near `hitl-approval` and none inside it.
`hitl-approval` owns the pause (trigger, durable pending state, decision surface,
decision record, continuation) and says "the human decides on the real thing, not a
summary produced by the gated party" - then stops. For a *plan*, the real thing is
mostly what was not written down. Nothing in the corpus owns what a human is shown at
the plan gate, in what order, and which questions are answered by a reader that is
not the author. That is a stage, not an opinion, and it is `XL`:
[`docs/subject-proposal-plan-review.md`](../../docs/subject-proposal-plan-review.md)
folds the four fragments in as proposed techniques with the routing rule, the lens
priority, the inadmissibility rule and the escalation ladder each carrying its
decision rule, placement verified against `taxonomy.json` (orchestration holds 6 of
10), and five open questions the drafter decides.

**Executed in the same session** (operator instruction, and now the skill's rule at
0.17.0): one forge worker, read-only reconciliation across all seven bridged trees,
gate-clean on first report, two process applications against this registry's own
`architect` plan gate and the forge/deepen director pattern. The subject note carries
the five resolved questions and the two overrides.

## Two asymmetry findings, both inverse

The asymmetry hunt paid twice, and in the direction the skill has not recorded
before: the corpus had the instrument and the source supplied the case where its
domain is one case wider than stated.

- **`decision-records` reads its distribution for an actor.** Its trigger loop says
  approval near 100% means the gate is below the judgment threshold. When the record's
  subject is an *objection* raised by a reader chartered to disagree, the same six
  fields read backwards: objections mostly rejected with rationale is the healthy
  pattern, critical objections mostly accepted means plans arrive underprepared, and
  the `why` field becomes mandatory on every disposition because it is the engagement
  the gate exists to force. Amended in place; the polarity flip is now written beside
  the loop it inverts.
- **`human-gate-capacity` measures the gate with four numbers.** At the subject's own
  floor - one person and a fleet - those numbers describe the person who is the
  queue, and the overload signature (verdicts arriving faster late in a session) is a
  claim about their state. The measures are right; what changes is what may be done
  with them: count, never score; advisory, never a gate; local, never a persisted
  judgment; and the stop decided before the session, because the metacognition that
  would notice fatigue draws on the capacity being spent. Corroborated from training
  data, not the source: the popular decision-fatigue account rests on an ego-depletion
  effect a 23-lab 2016 replication measured at d = 0.04, and what is robust is
  task-switching cost and the vigilance decrement. The source stated the same
  boundary; the amendment cites the literature, not the page.

## Skill changes (S lane, version 0.15.0 -> 0.16.0)

- **Phase 8 gains a paired-proof step before any cross-repo commit.** The operator's
  dispatch, made concrete: name the measurable, prefer A/B at any scale (both arms,
  same instrument, arm count travels with the number), fall back to before/after
  under `before-after-outcome-pairing`'s rules and never invent the missing half,
  record a status from a closed set, and `unproven` does not commit. The proof
  measures impact on the tree; the structural fact measures whether the tree confirms
  the standard - different claims, and a commit needs the first.
- **Corroboration: tier sources, never count them.** From the model-cards page, which
  implemented it well: relays are downstream of primaries, so relays agreeing with a
  stale primary are evidence of its *past* state, not a vote against its current one.
  The ledger already deduped carriers at publisher granularity; this is the rule that
  granularity was serving.
- **Phase 5: unattended runs advance only `real gap` rows.** Operator rule from this
  dispatch, codified.

## Leads

- **A subject for readers whose object of care is the human** (the source's
  "sentinel" category: read-only, advisory for a human, explicit epistemic honesty
  rule; near-miss test - a code reviewer is read-only and advisory and is *not* one,
  because its object of care is the code). The corpus has `hitl-approval` for the
  pause and `proactive-nudges` for machine-initiated contact; nothing names the
  category. **Return when a second independent source describes agents built to this
  shape**, or when `plan-review` is forged and its golden path finds it needs the
  category name to state its own boundary.
- **Calibration refusals as signal** (row 5): a capability repeatedly proposed for
  promotion and always refused reveals an unspoken constraint; one nobody proposes to
  move is a removal candidate. **Return when `enforcement-demotion` or `quality-gates`
  takes a deepen pass** - it is a one-section amendment to whichever owns the
  promotion loop.
