# deer-flow - v2 back half

mechanical XL trigger, the spec for `agent-runtime-assembly`, three drafted
source-tree applications) is [[2026-09-02-deer-flow-v2-replication]]. This run

ship, then the ledgers. The claim was contended at first (the replication run
still held the source), then taken after it released; both runs were the

**Declared foci.** (1) Fragments-first: not re-run - the front half already
swept the tree, and its own finding stands (the changelog is an index, the
module guides are the content). (2) *Name the condition under which the
technique would NOT hold before choosing the seam, and prefer the seam that
tests it* - applied to both apply rows, and it decided them:
`bounded-projection-of-external-work` was tested against two paired peers
with no shared store (the technique presumes one), and held on custody with
a twist while failing on delivery - the row is `better` on the clause the
falsifying seam exposed; `semantic-hook-placement` was tested against a
closed single-author pipeline and came back `not-better`, with the condition
now in the technique. (3) The previous row's *ship the falsifier's
instrument*: the unit test committed with the personas change is that
instrument.
