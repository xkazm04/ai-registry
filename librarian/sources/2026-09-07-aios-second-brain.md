## The one row that survived

**Elicitation as a memory write path** → `agent-memory/gap-directed-elicitation` (new technique).

`agent-memory` carries 19 techniques and every one assumes memory is a function of *observed
activity*: working state is observed, episodes captured at boundaries, beliefs distilled from
episodes, procedures promoted from repeated runs. The golden path says so in an enumeration —
*"the subject covers the whole pipeline: operative state inside a session, **capture of what
occurred**, distillation…"* — and an enumeration invites exactly one question. What feeds the
first stage? Only what the agent witnessed.

A fact that was never transacted produces no event. The principal's goals, the constraint they
work under, the alternative they rejected, the reason a convention exists — these are missing
not because the pipeline is mistuned but because they never happened *to* the agent.
`coverage-instrumentation` can measure that hole and explicitly stops there (*"answers exactly
one question"*, and its closing section routes the open-ended case to recall's empty rate). No
technique closed it. That is a missing **stage zero** in a subject thorough from stage one on —
the shape Phase 6 says to hunt for, found where it says it hides.

Two things kept the landing honest:

- **Framed as an acquisition path, not a new stage.** An interview produces a transcript that
  enters at `episodic-capture` and consolidates normally, inheriting governance, provenance and
  decay. That keeps every existing sentence in the golden path true, so the landing is an
  append rather than a restructuring of a shared spine — which took the row's RISK from 2 to 0
  and is what carried it over the threshold.
- **The gaming boundary.** `coverage-instrumentation` warns that the cheapest way to raise
  coverage is to write a worthless item per uncovered scope. Elicitation is the expensive way to
  raise the same number, and the only thing separating them is provenance. The technique says so.

**Convergence, recorded not linked.** `recruiting/assessment/interview-run-of-show` carries
`question-kit-grounded-in-named-evidence-gaps` — the same mechanism (questions targeted at named
gaps in evidence), in another bundle, for another purpose. Cross-bundle links are forbidden and
this is not an inversion to discriminate, it is corroboration for a mechanism reached twice
independently. It is the `+1` in the row's GAIN and it stays in this note.

## Applied

One landing, one apply row owed, **one written** — `1/1`.

`gap-directed-elicitation` → `personas`, mode **experiment**, verdict **unmeasurable**, and the
seam was chosen to **falsify** the technique rather than to confirm it. It was not falsified.

The technique claims the entire apparatus that ranks memory designs is blind to knowledge nobody
transacted. The year-replay harness's generator turned out to mint every fact and immediately
speak it — seven construction passes, `_new_fact()` then `_emit()`, no exception and no assertion
guarding it — and its docstring names the property as a virtue for an unrelated and correct
reason (probes derived from the world cannot feed on themselves). The nearest existing probe class
is `distractor`: a key never stated **and not true**, graded UNKNOWN, so a store is right to
disclaim it. Its inverse — true, and never stated — did not exist. So no arm on the nine-row
ladder has ever been asked a question whose answer was never in the event stream, and the 0.89
ceiling is a ceiling on *witnessed* knowledge.

The instrument named in the technique was then built: an opt-in `held-out` fact class, drawn from
a separate RNG and appended after all other generation so the default world stays byte-identical.
Paired at seed 7 against the generator at the previous commit:

| | facts | events | probes | classes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `held_out=0` | 176 | 3,571 | 194 | 10 |
| baseline | 176 | 3,571 | 194 | 10 |
| `held_out=8` | 184 | 3,571 | 202 | 11 |

Arm A identical on all three lists; arm B leaves the event list and every non-held-out probe
identical, and **0 of 8 held-out facts are referenced by any event**. Proof status `ab-paired`.

`unmeasurable` rather than `better` because no arm has been *scored* on the class — that needs a
paid run — and the method forbids reporting `unmeasurable` without naming the instrument. The
instrument is named and now exists. The prediction is recorded before the measurement rather than
after: every current arm should score at or near zero, including the leader.

## Notes on the class

Two things this run confirms about the build-walkthrough class, both already in
`references/source-classes.md` and both load-bearing here:

- **Route per half, never per source.** Every catch came from the tour half — the 3D brain, the
  synced dashboard, the resource pack. The one landing came from the operating half, and so did
  all four untriaged rows. The discriminating question (*is the creator describing what the tool
  does, or what happened to them while using it?*) sorted twelve rows without a single ambiguous
  case.
- **Length is not yield, and neither is polish.** 5,151 words, a large fraction of it promotion,
  and the single surviving row came from one sentence about what a coding agent reads before it
