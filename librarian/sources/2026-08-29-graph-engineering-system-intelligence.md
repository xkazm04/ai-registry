## Leads

  paper-aggregator source in its own right, to be triaged at cluster level. Return

- **When does a topology pay over a loop?** The critic's strongest point ([00:26:08]):
  the survey never establishes when graph infrastructure nets a benefit over a
  simpler agent loop. Nothing in `agent-chaining` or `fleet-orchestration` carries
  that decision rule either - they decide *which* mechanism, never *whether*. Return
  condition: a second independent source with a measured comparison, or an opened
  tree that took a loop out and put a graph in (or the reverse) and kept the numbers.
