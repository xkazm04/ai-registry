# The Composer announcement, 2026-08-26 — a 76-word source, and the run that built a category anyway

Run 16, and a first for the ledger twice over: the thinnest source ever
ingested (76 words; `research-ingest` exited 3, correctly), and the first of
a new class — **vendor product announcement**. The run's yield came almost
entirely from the two lanes the source did not gate: the web-research lane
the operator asked for, and the operator's structural question about where
audio lives in the registry. That question became the session: a new
`audio-generation` category (three subjects, 14 techniques, 3 applications),
one seam technique in `production-ops`, boundary prose across three bundles,
and a music engine landed in a consumer tree the same day. The source's own
contribution was one catch and one clock — which is exactly what the class
is for.

## Source class, first characterization

**Vendor product announcement.** Reliable for exactly one thing: the vendor
shipped the capability. Not reliable for: why it matters, whether it is
new, or any rule about using it — a 76-word script of feature verbs carries
no craft at all. Mine it as a currency signal and a pointer at the vendor's
*documentation*, which is where the actual schema lives; the announcement's
one strip-test survivor came from the docs it pointed to, not from anything
it said. Expected yield: one currency signal, zero techniques. Anything
more comes from lanes the source merely occasioned.

## Candidates

**1. Continuity at the seam of a partial regeneration — ACCEPTED (technique).**
Anchor: "Reshape one section... while the rest stays untouched" [00:00:26].
The announcement located the gap; the primary schema docs authorized the
finding: per-section `context_adherence`, kept-sections-by-reference, and
the vendor's own "first chunk sets the whole" note generalize to any
continuous medium. Landed as
`review-iteration-loops/partial-regeneration-seams` — the physical seam the
edit-plan discipline's causal seams cannot see. Corroboration: vendor
primary docs (fetched in-run) + training-data convergence with image
inpainting masks and video extension practice.

**2. Audio's structural home — ACCEPTED (category, the session's body).**
The operator's question, not the source's. Mapped first: audio prior art
scattered across four bundles (voice-io in software-engineering,
spatial-audio in game-production, spotting and delivery in media-generation,
voice-interview-fidelity in recruiting), each correctly placed by its host's
stated job. Decision: **no standalone audio domain** — every domain in this
registry is named for a job, never a substrate, and an audio domain would be
the first substrate-named one; the consumer test splits audio users across
jobs (a recruiter needs only the fairness subject, and moving it under
"audio" would be actively wrong). What was missing was one category:
`media-generation/audio-generation` — the bundle's job is "produce media to
a brief" and it did so for pixels only. Instrument evidence: research-map,
asked where a music subject would go, answered `visual-generation`, a
category that cannot hold it. Built: `music-prompt-composition` (5t),
`generated-music-acceptance` (5t), `sound-effect-generation` (4t).

**3. The four-way discriminator — ACCEPTED (boundary prose).**
Producing / placing / plumbing / judging. Stated in each side's own voice on
`voice-io` (software-engineering) and `spatial-audio-scene-authoring`
(game-production), baked into all three new golden paths. No cross-bundle
links — the question itself is the treaty, per the discriminator rule.

**4. Currency: section-editable music shipped — ACCEPTED (dated facts).**
Landed inside `process--section-plan-as-the-brief` (the 2026-08 music
landscape: plan-native vendor vs the no-API vocal-quality leader vs the
fast-instrumental tier) and `process--rights-and-provenance-record` (plan
tiers, the non-retroactivity trap, registration doctrine). `refresh_by`
2026-11-26 on both.

**5. "Edit a song like a document" — ALREADY COVERED.**
The product's headline is `edit-do-not-regenerate`, a law of this bundle
since before the product existed, cited by 13 documents. The corpus states
the vendor's pitch at higher altitude. Recorded as the run's best catch.

**6. Standalone audio domain — DECLINED, banked as lead.**
Declined for the substrate-naming argument in candidate 2. **Return
condition:** revisit if `audio-generation` grows past ~8 subjects (the
category cap making the seams visible), or if a connected project arrives
whose *primary* domain is audio production rather than audio-in-service-of
trailers/games/products.

## What the run teaches

- A source too thin to mine can still be worth a full run when it arrives
  with an operator question attached; the honest accounting is to credit
  the question, not the source. The ingest instrument's exit-3 said "stop
  the mining lane", not "stop the session".
- The altitude discipline (0.10.0) held: the landing is one law-adjacent
  technique (seams), one doctrine decision (no substrate domains), and the
  dated facts filed where dated facts go — with the numbers citing into
  the technique rather than becoming it.
