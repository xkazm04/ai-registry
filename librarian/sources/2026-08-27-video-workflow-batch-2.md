# Video workflow batch 2, 2026-08-27 - the second batch, the author-count caveat, and a law reaching convergence

Run 25, second batch ingest, one day after the first. The headline method
fact: **12 sources is not 12 voices.** Six videos are one creator, two more a
second, so the batch carries ~5 independent voices, and same-author repetition
was excluded from the convergence count (one practitioner's habit repeated
six times is one sighting). The convergence that mattered this run was
**cross-run**: four candidates were second sightings of run-24 landings from
independent sources, each landed as a widening of a day-old amendment - the
cheapest content this skill has ever produced. And one shape reached its
third sighting in three consecutive runs and was landed as a law.

Mid-run discovery: a parallel session had landed
`visual-generation/character-identity-continuity` (from the connected
project's measurement rig) between runs. Triage was re-based on the
post-commit corpus; that subject now owns identity-at-the-cut and reference
timing/strength, and none of this run's landings collide with it.

## Accepted (10)

1. **Reference role map** → NEW technique
   `image-prompt-composition/reference-role-map`: every attached asset gets
   one named role declared at the top of the prompt, with negative scope
   where bleed is likely, names reused in the beats, and one card
   multiplying into a group. Generalizes the style-labeling sentence to
   heterogeneous multi-reference calls. (2 authors + 2 platform surfaces.)
2. **The moving reference** → amendment on `generated-shot-sourcing`: a clip
   as conditioning, two uses with opposite handling - whole-previous-clip
   continuity carry (a frame anchor resets mood; the clip carries how the
   scene was moving) and scoped choreography transfer ("controls blocking
   and pacing, never identity or style"); trim to the exact span.
3. **Storyboard grid conditioning** → NEW technique in `video-assembly`:
   numbered textless panel grid as one conditioning image; panel count by
   beat density; text on panels leaks as watermark-like glyphs; pairs with
   the annotation discipline. Zero prior art corpus-wide for "storyboard".
4. **The import is also the lever** → appended to run-24's
   anchor-imports-texture section: grade the reference stills before
   animation and the look rides into every clip at still prices - the
   constructive corollary of yesterday's hazard, cross-run convergence.
5. **State variants are about recurrence, not characters** → widened
   run-24's identity-split-from-state amendment with the location case
   (intact + destroyed bridge, edited with change-nothing-else). Second
   sighting, independent source, one day apart.
6. **Drawn annotations as control** → widened run-24's schematic section: a
   path drawn on the image is a trajectory the model follows literally;
   when the claim is spatial, annotate rather than describe. Second
   sighting.
7. **The edit pass is itself lossy** → amendment on
   `review-iteration-loops/partial-regeneration-seams`: generative edits
   re-encode and soften; chains accumulate artifacts; every edit applies to
   the stored original, composite the edited region, deterministic tools
   for mechanical deletions. Three independent practitioners across two
   runs.
8. **Dialogue is a duration claim** → paragraph in generated-shot-sourcing's
   clip-caps section: lines take performance-pace seconds; overloaded shots
   rush the cast; split dialogue-heavy beats at brief time.
9. **The cutaway insert as seam device** → appended to scene-grammar's seam
   section: a neutral insert owes continuity to neither side; bankable in
   strips from one location image (corroborates run-24's harvest).
10. **A typed input owns its channel** → LAW, `_laws.md`, on third-sighting
    convergence across three runs: camera path (run 23, two sibling systems),
    beat grid (run 24), per-character emotion dial (run 25). Cited from
    movement-motivation and performance-direction; the emotion-dial instance
    landed as a sentence in performance-direction.

## Method notes

- **Count voices, not videos.** Same-author repetition is one sighting; the
  batch's convergence column must dedupe by creator or it manufactures
  corroboration. 12 videos ≈ 5 voices this run.
- **Back-to-back batches compound.** Four of ten landings were widenings of
  the previous day's amendments - a second batch mined while the first is
  fresh converts single-source landings into corroborated ones at almost no
  cost. Sequencing related batches close together is a real lever.
- **Re-map after a parallel session lands.** The corpus moved between runs
  (new subject, same bundle); Phase 4's map was re-read against HEAD before
  homes were chosen. A batch run in a shared checkout should expect this.
- 0 fetches again; the class continues to never bind the corroboration
  budget.
