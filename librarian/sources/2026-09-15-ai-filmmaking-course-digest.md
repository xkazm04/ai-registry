### Row 11 - the promoting question, executed

*Does the corpus already tell a motion brief to avoid negated states?* Read
`performance-direction` in full. Rule 5 is headed "State what the performer does
*not* do", and its examples are all positive holds ("he stays under the tree", "the
backpack stays on both shoulders"). The zero-beat section writes stillness as "say they
do not move ... nothing else moves", and quotes a practitioner who re-rolled several
times under "no motion". `negative-prompting` states the adjacent rule for images: a
negative cannot beat an invitation the positive prompt extended, and on models with no
negative channel exclusions are carried as positive phrasing. So the corpus licenses
negated phrasing inside a motion brief's positive text and never tested it. That is a
real boundary question with a visible falsifier, and it is decidable only by rendering.

**Why the floor was high: exposure, not performance.** A luma-range read per clip
found a global exposure pulse (range above 50 of 255) in three of eight clips, all at
the first seed: shot1 under both arms, and shot2 under arm A only. With each frame's
mean removed, deviation from the first frame fell to 6-12 in every clip that did not
pulse. At this length and resolution the seed decides whether the model breathes the
exposure, and a content-blind distance mostly measures that breathing. The one case
where phrasing plausibly mattered (shot2, same seed, A pulsed and B did not) is n=1.

## Leads

1. **Location plates for a camera move: depth along the move axis, not "avoid
   head-on".** The source's rule is half right. A frontal view of a wall plane gives a
   motion model no parallax; a frontal one-point view down a hall's long axis gives it
   the most depth there is, and the corpus already treats that composition as a
   deliberate "designed world" choice. The discriminator a technique would carry is
   whether the plate shows recession along the axis the camera will travel, not the
   angle. Return condition: a render pair on the local image-to-video model with one
   brief, a dolly forward, from a wall-facing frontal plate, a one-point axial plate
   and a three-quarter plate, gated on sheet discrimination. Home if it lands:
   `production-ops/video-assembly/generated-shot-sourcing` (plate authoring before
   rung 3), not `cinematic-language`, whose depth staging is narrative framing.
2. **Negated state phrasing in a motion brief, as a rate rather than a pair.** The
   effect, if real, is not visible in one render. It shows up as how often an unasked
   event happens across seeds, so a two-clip pair cannot carry it. Return condition:
   at least 6 seeds per arm on the two shots above, an instrument that sees small
   gestures and not only global exposure (per-region optical-flow energy with the
   figure masked in, or a calibrated vision grader asked "did anything happen that
   the brief did not ask for"), and the operator shown a per-arm contact sheet of all
   seeds, gated on the sheet distance. If B wins, the amendment is one sentence in
   `performance-direction` rule 5: write the bound as the held state, not the negated
   one, because the negated noun is an invitation (the image-side rule
   `negative-prompting` already states). If A holds, the source's claim is a catch
   against the corpus as it stands.
