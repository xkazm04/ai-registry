## Design record (Phase 2d)

Routing count: **10 load-bearing decisions, corpus models 0** - no `marketing` bundle
existed. The XL trigger fired mechanically and, per the operator's explicit ask, the
handoff was executed in this session rather than banked.

1. **Intent is a verdict read off the live results page, never a tool label.**
   forces: tool labels wrong often enough to route money pages to blogs; modifier
   lists over-label as informational (82% of measured errors). buys: the page type
   that can rank. rejects: keyword-tool intent column. where:
   `references/search-intent.md:17-41`, `keyword-research.md:94-100`. stage: after
   junk cut, before volume cut. corpus: NONE -> `search-intent-classification`.
2. **The difficulty ceiling governs blog posts; money pages are governed by what the
   business sells.** forces: a service page has jobs beyond ranking (ads, profile,
   links, referrals). buys: a site that is not missing its flagship. rejects: one
   ceiling for both. where: `keyword-research.md:62-84`. corpus: NONE ->
   `keyword-metric-reliability` (`authority-ceiling-blogs-not-money-pages`).
3. **Volume is an ordinal, never a forecast; local terms are ranked by revenue per
   job.** forces: 1,462 distinct values over 101,897 keywords; median 0.51x. where:
   `keyword-research.md:104-119`, `keyword-strategy.md:12-29`. corpus: NONE ->
   `keyword-metric-reliability`.
4. **AI answer boxes split the keyword universe; weight toward transactional and
   local, treat informational as linking fuel.** forces: 61% CTR fall concentrated on
   informational queries. where: `keyword-research.md:113-119`, `geo.md`. corpus:
   NONE -> `answer-engine-visibility`.
5. **One primary keyword per page; two registries (map, site index) are the build
   plan; quarantine, never delete.** where: `CLAUDE.md:87-89`,
   `keyword-research.md:200-231`. corpus: NONE -> `site-architecture-and-topic-clusters`.
6. **Audits change the mechanical layer only, never the copy; deletion is a
   recommendation awaiting a yes.** where: `CLAUDE.md:82,86`, `audit.md:14-59`.
   corpus: NONE -> `on-page-and-metadata-craft` (`mechanical-layer-only-never-the-copy`)
   and the law `a-gate-before-money-and-copy`.
7. **Never invent proof; label convention as convention.** where: `CLAUDE.md:44-46`,
   every `[E]`/`[C]` tag in `references/`. corpus: NONE -> laws `never-invent-proof`,
   `label-convention-as-convention`; `grounded-marketing-generation`.
8. **Three-layer pyramid, cities only at layer three, thank-you page off-tree as the

## The forge

**Scouts (6, read-only, parallel):** five over the consumer `systedo-case` (paid
campaigns; analytics and measurement; AI content, twin and creative; organic, local,
leads and catalog; product marketing surfaces and doctrine documents) and one over the
seo-agent clone. Reports saved under the run's scratch directory and deleted with it;
their anchors live on in the applications. Every scout brief carried the line "Your
job is NOT software engineering review."

**Design (director):** 30 subjects in 7 categories, 4-7 techniques each assigned at
design time, 10 laws, purity profile `marketing` added to `check-bundles.mjs` and
pinned in `REQUIRED_PURITY`, stack `prompt-pipeline` declared for applications against
a command-driven agent whose knowledge lives in prompt files. Categories:
search-and-content (6), content-production (4), paid-advertising (5),
measurement-and-economics (6), local-and-zero-budget (3), conversion-and-leads (4),
positioning-and-proof (2). One subject, `attribution-and-incrementality`, was forged
from expert knowledge and web research against the trees' *negative* half (no
attribution model in either tree) - the operator asked for the area populated beyond
what the trees carry.

**Waves:** 30 workers, cap 10, topped up one per completion. Every worker ran
expert-draft-then-reconcile, 2-4 web searches where the dispatch said so (22 of 30),
and the gate on its own subject. Two mechanical corrections mid-wave: the gate's
`verified_against` regex rejects hyphens, so the pipeline stack is pinned
`prompt-pipeline@2026.09.06` (dotted); and the law-link depth from `techniques/` is
three `../`, not four. The brief was amended and every worker after that landed clean.

**Director review (diff, not report):** gate green per subject; an extended purity
sweep over the upper layers (engine, platform, vendor, community, model and fleet
product names plus repo paths and extensions) returned zero hits, asserted against the
applications layer where the same pattern found 631 legitimate hits; `use_when`
present on every technique; boundary statements in every golden path naming
neighbours by slug. A sibling run's committed media-generation application was
gate-red at HEAD (undeclared `next` stack, malformed `verified_against`); repaired in
two lines so the shared gate could go green, recorded here.

**Convention labelling as the bundle's spine.** Every worker was told to say which
of a technique's thresholds are documented platform behaviour, published measurement,
or practitioner convention. The reports list roughly 150 labelled conventions - the
6-of-10 intent count, the difficulty-below-authority ladder, the 100/month floor, the
subhead interval, the 4-field form, the cadence cap, the 5-minute service level, the
BANT point values, the 60/40 score weights, the 3x spend cap, the +-5% dead-band -
and a smaller set of documented facts (character limits, attribution windows,
availability values, the 364-day shift as arithmetic).

## What the scouts said the trees do worse than the standard (the deviation backlog)

Paid: no bidding layer, structure-blind reallocation, last-click and window-naive
measurement, absolute search-term constants, no experiment runtime or ratio-mismatch
check. Measurement: no attribution model, cohort economics always sample,
currency-anchored constants, revenue-only pacing, overhead by revenue share. Content:
social rules asserted not measured, intent classifier folds comparison into
informational, decay never checked against real data, "E-E-A-T" chips measure counts.
Organic/local: every weight asserted, no citation layer or geogrid, organic ranked on
clicks only, feeds omit link/image/sale price, a demo ad fallback fabricates a claim.
Surfaces: no conversion measurement on the marketing site, unsourced competitor
facts, unvalidated pricing anchors. Pipeline: no demand-side model, paid search
absent, internal numeric inconsistencies, CRO rules untested, affiliate routing in
rules. Each is recorded as a deviation in the relevant application, standard kept.
