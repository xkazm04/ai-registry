# ICSE 2026 SEIP — a conference program as a degenerate reference index

Mined 2026-08-31. Three siblings live at claim time (`module-design`; an
agentic-coding-trends report holding `hitl-approval` + `adoption-measurement`;
a mailing-list archive holding `test-harness` + `quality-gates` +
`runner-fleet`). The third one mattered: it held two of the subjects this run
routed into, which is why row 4 below was deferred rather than landed.

## The class, and what made it degenerate

A conference track page is a **reference index**: its own prose is a
call-for-papers, and its value is 74 pointers. But unlike an awesome-list it
carries **no annotations** — title, authors, affiliation, nothing else. That
removes the signal `reference-waves.md` step 3 weights highest, the curator's
sentence about each entry.

What substitutes for it is the track's own admission rule, quoted from the page:
papers must *"present evidence for the paper's conclusions."* Peer review at an
industrial-practice track **is** the annotation, and it turned out to be a good
one: 8 of 8 references read returned something usable, which is a far higher
hit rate than a title-ranked sample has any right to.

**Two numbers for the enumeration, because the first was wrong.** A raw grep
for lines ending in the track name returns **150**. The structural parse of the
Accepted Papers table returns **74**, and an independent parse of the program
schedule also returns **74**. The 150 was counting session headers and
navigation entries. Only the agreement of two independent parses made 74
trustworthy.

## The boundary finding — the index's own, and the reason to expect a small yield

A bibliography is a stated opinion about a field's edge, and this one and ours
overlap in exactly one region: **the measurement of automated verdicts.**

Of the 74, roughly 46 are single-vendor tool papers in domains this registry
does not carry — control-logic engineering, satellite anomaly detection,
automotive quality planning, mobile OS static analysis, production tracing.
Meanwhile `librarian-scan`'s highest-attention subjects — markdown-vault,
client-state, diff-comparison, import-normalization — appear **nowhere** in the
74. This index cannot feed the corpus's measured demand. It can only feed the
seam where both fields already stand, and that seam produced everything below.

## The convergence I ranked on was fake; a real one appeared unranked

Wave 1 was built around an expected convergence between *The SWE-Bench
Illusion* and *What's in a Benchmark?* — two papers on benchmark validity
accepted independently into one track. **That premise failed.** The second
paper cites the first as reference [17] and relays every validity number it
carries from four other papers. Per the tiering rule the pair is **one
observation, not two**, and the ranking that produced the wave was wrong about
its single strongest signal.

The genuine convergence was unplanned and arrived from two references ranked
for unrelated reasons — different authors, institutions, countries and problem
domains, neither citing the other. It is finding 1.

## Accepted

**1. `false-positive-economics` — the spiral needs an author who can dismiss.**
Landed as an amendment section. Two independent references reach the same
missing discriminator: the death spiral runs on **the author's unilateral power
to dismiss**, not on the false-positive rate. One reference ships a repair gate
at precision 0.30 against a population base rate of 0.19 and is right to,
because the refused artifact is machine-generated and a false positive costs
one wasted generation rather than a person's obedience. The other measures a
mandatory pre-integration detector running at a 76% false-alarm rate (>90%
counting exclusions) for ten months, unbypassed and undeleted, because an alarm
called false by the first reviewer goes to a *second* reviewer before it may be
closed. The technique previously stated "precision is the survival property"
unconditionally. It now carries the discriminator, plus two obligations the
references measured: read precision against the population's prevalence (the
no-skill policy's precision **is** the base rate), and grade a downstream
suppressor on its **recall**, not its suppression rate — the degenerate
suppressor that calls everything a false positive scores a perfect suppression
rate and an accuracy equal to the base rate.

**2. `measurement-honesty` — a seventh datum state, `compromised`.** The golden
path declared "A datum has six states, not two", with state 6 *Refuted* defined
strictly internally: "another number **the same system publishes** proves that
value cannot be true." A score taken on an instrument the subject was already
exposed to passes all six states and every technique in the subject, and is
invalid anyway. The heading now reads seven, and the new technique
`instrument-exposure-control` carries the protocol: the deprived-input negative
control, the matched-twin population read as a *gap* rather than a level, the
rule that a fixed harness is not a fixed instrument when the subject can learn
it, the one-directional-drift signature that inverts
`noise-band-and-hysteresis`, and the input-leak rate published as a pair. The
enumeration hunt paid for the thirteenth consecutive run, and this time on an
enumeration the file had already extended once — state 6 was itself "added late
because it is invisible from inside a single number." State 7 is invisible from
inside the system.

## Applied

Both landings owe a row and both got one; neither was a simulation.

- **`instrument-exposure-control` → tracklight, mode `experiment`, verdict
  `better`, shipped.** A census of the tree: 88 raw matches for
  comparability/reproducibility vocabulary across 44 files, hand-verified to 8
  cross-run comparability claims, of which **0** condition on exposure. Zero
  occurrences of contamination/memorization/training-cutoff vocabulary anywhere
  — every `cutoff` is data retention, every `leak` is PII. The service models
  harness stochasticity with a three-value determinism lattice folded to its
  weakest member and is blind to the variable that decides whether two runs
  months apart are comparable. Three sites claimed freezing a dataset makes
  runs comparable; a docs-only correction shipped naming what freezing does and
  does not guarantee. The structural fact nobody designed: `Dataset.source`
  already partitions `events:recent` from `import`, built for privacy, and it
  is exactly the partition state 7 needs — the project's privacy design is its
  contamination defence and nothing said so.

- **`false-positive-economics` → pumper, mode `experiment`, verdict
  `unmeasurable`, no code change.** The amendment's second precondition is
  countable, so it was counted: 12 raw sites set the taxonomy's `enabled` flag
  true; hand-verifying, all twelve are compile-time seeds, test fixtures or
  unrelated subsystems, and production paths promoting a machine proposal
  without a human are **0**. The proposer writes the record with the flag false
  and emits a next-step instruction for a human to flip it. The tree already
  implements the amendment, so there is no behavioural arm to run.
  **Instrument that would make it measurable** (named, as the method requires):
  per-proposal review-outcome telemetry — proposals emitted, accepted and
  rejected per run, plus reviewer time per accepted entry. The service records
  new and changed counts and no acceptance outcome, so the proposer's precision
  is unknown in both arms and no threshold on it can be defended.

## Already covered — the honest catches

- **Retry-lane platform failures.** A flakiness reference reported a constant
  block of test-framework initialization failures appearing after a move to a
  new runner fleet, firing before the first test. `platform-quirk-absorption`
  owns this and owns it better: the reference observed it and did not absorb it.
- **Retriever complementarity.** A retrieval reference measured lexical search
  collapsing on narrative text while winning on tables. The `retrieval` golden
  path states the complementary-blind-spots argument with the mechanism.
- **Overlap where structure is weak.** A chunking reference measured 0% overlap
  as the worst configuration tested, apparently contradicting "overlap is a tax,
  not a default" — but `chunking-and-indexing` already carries the escape hatch
  ("buy overlap only where structure is genuinely weak") and the reference's
  setting is precisely that case. Direction already covered and covered
  correctly. Only the *stopping rule* is new, and it is banked as a lead.

## Leads

Each carries a return condition.

1. **The extraction→chunking handoff nobody owns.** `chunking-and-indexing`
   presumes "whatever structural units the source format declares"; a
   layout-only container declares none, so extraction deletes the affordance
   the chunker is told to rely on. `document-text-extraction` ends at the
   verdict; neither subject states the handoff. Measured beside it: overlap
   benefit **peaks at 25% and reverses at 50%**, which is the stopping rule our
   text lacks, and an OCR-class parser costing **629x** a stream read while
   ranking third. *Return when a second source measures the seam, or when
   `document-text-extraction` is next swept.*
2. **Retry is banded, not uniform.** Sporadic flakes are 74.9% of flaky tests
   but only 27.5% of blocking failures; the frequent band is 25.1% of tests
   causing 72.5% of failures. `flake-lifecycle`'s broken-vs-flaky dichotomy
   collapses the middle band that causes most failures. Beside it: a declared
   **pollution budget** before arming a state-pollution detector, from a
   measured negative — 5,064 auto-inserted cleanups produced 5 fixes and 27
   breakages — and the narrow detector outperforming the broad one. *Return
   when `test-harness.md` is committed by its current holder; it had uncommitted
   foreign edits and a live sibling claim throughout this run.*
3. **Best-of-N publishing.** Where the reporter selects which of N runs to
   publish, the value estimates the maximum of an unreported distribution.
   `lower-bound-disclosure` asks three questions — can an observation happen
   without a record (undercount), can one event produce many records
   (overcount), or both — and none of the three is "many events, one
   published." *Return on a second independent sighting.*
4. **Provenance requirements select the population.** Raising the evidence bar
   on a signal changes *who supplies signals*, not only their label. The source
   hedges it as one reading of its own cross-section. *Return when a second
   source shows a stricter provenance rule measurably changed who reported.*
5. **The proxy-substitution escape.** A first-party productivity paper endorses
   substituting a correlated operational metric when the outcome instrument is
   underpowered — the exact move `adoption-vs-outcome-separation` exists to
   forbid. It names a condition our technique has no answer for: what a team
   does when the top-line metric is genuinely underpowered for their true
   effect size. ⚠ **Venue unresolved**: the arXiv header for the document read
   carries an FSE 2025 companion block, not this track, so either the index row
   is mis-attributed or a successor paper exists. *Return when the venue is
   settled and a second source shows the same endorsement.*

## Method note — a hazard this run found

Two workers independently reported that wave lanes wrote generic filenames
(`paper.html`, `paper.txt`) into the **shared run scratchpad root**, and one
lane's conversion read a sibling lane's document before catching it by content
check. Phase 9's "scratch is per run" rule needs a per-**worker** corollary:
a wave of 8 parallel readers is 8 writers to one directory. Filed to
`LESSONS.md`.
