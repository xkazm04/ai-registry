# Upwork as the `messaging` connector

What was learned mapping this recipe onto contracts run on Upwork. Nothing here is part of
the recipe: swap the venue and this file stops applying while the recipe does not change.
Venue terms move; the current terms of service are authoritative over anything below.

## What the mapping has to decide

**Fixed price work runs on funded milestones, so put the acceptance conditions in them.**
The client funds a milestone into escrow before work on it starts; the freelancer submits
work against it; the client approves it, asks for changes, or does nothing, and after a
review period the payment releases on its own. The recipe's confirmed restatement belongs
in the milestone descriptions: when each milestone names the conditions the client agreed,
the submission and its evidence answer to text both sides can see, and a dispute has
something to be decided against.

**Do not start work on an unfunded milestone.** The escrow is what makes a confirmed scope
binding on the client as well as on the operator. Work begun before funding is the same
unpriced work this recipe warns about, with the venue's protection removed.

**A change of scope is a new milestone or an amended contract.** A request that arrives in
messages is answered with a proposed milestone and amount, not absorbed into the current
one. The messages themselves are the record of what was agreed, and the venue reads them in
a dispute, so agreements made anywhere else do not exist for that purpose.

**Submit the work through the milestone, with the evidence attached.** A delivery sent as a
message leaves the milestone without the artifact it is supposed to pay for. Attach the
evidence pack and the handoff notes to the milestone submission itself.

**Keep contact and payment on the venue before and during the contract.** The terms
prohibit moving communication or payment off the platform to avoid its fees. Handoff notes
that tell the client how to reach the operator elsewhere belong after the contract, and
only where the terms allow it.

**The operator sends, from their own session.** The terms forbid automated access to the
site. The agent prepares the milestone text, the evidence and the notes; the operator
reads, edits and submits them.

## What transfers to any venue that holds payment

- Where a venue holds the money, the acceptance conditions belong in the object the money
  is attached to.
- A scope change is priced in the same place the original scope was.
