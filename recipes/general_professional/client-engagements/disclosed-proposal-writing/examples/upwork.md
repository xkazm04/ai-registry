# Upwork as the `messaging` connector

What was learned mapping this recipe onto proposals on Upwork. Nothing here is part of the
recipe: swap the venue and this file stops applying while the recipe does not change.
Venue terms move; the current terms of service are authoritative over anything below.

## What the mapping has to decide

**Every proposal has a price in Connects.** Submitting a proposal spends the venue's
bidding credits, and boosting one spends more. That turns the recipe's rule to pass on
briefs the operator cannot demonstrably do from a courtesy into a budget line: record the
credits spent against replies and contracts won, so the cost of an unfocused week is a
number rather than a feeling.

**Screening questions are their own fields.** A client can attach questions to a job, and
they arrive beside the cover letter rather than inside it. Answer each one in its field,
specifically; a reply that says "see my cover letter" reads as the reused paragraph this
recipe exists to avoid.

**Keep contact and payment on the venue.** The terms prohibit moving communication or
payment off the platform to avoid its fees, and a proposal that carries an email address,
a phone number or an outside link offered as a way to talk elsewhere is a fast route to
account restrictions. The draft never contains direct contact details.

**The account is one verified person's, and the terms forbid automated access.** Identity
verification ties the profile to an individual. The agent drafts; the operator reads,
edits and submits from their own session. Nothing about this mapping should ever involve
the agent holding the session.

**Disclosure.** Whatever the venue's current policy on AI assisted work says at adoption,
the recipe's default of stating assistance in the proposal satisfies it. Record the policy
text and the date it was read in charter memory, because policies on this are changing and
the operator will want to know which version they were complying with.

## What transfers to any bidding venue

- Where a bid costs something, the pass decision is a spending decision and deserves the
  same record as any other spend.
- Questions a client asked are answered where the client asked them.
- A venue's rule against leaving the venue binds the draft, not just the conversation
  after it.
