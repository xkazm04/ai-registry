# X as a direct `social` destination

What was learned mapping this recipe onto X specifically. Nothing here is part of the
recipe: swap the connector and this file stops applying while the recipe does not change.

## What the mapping has to decide

**A thread is several publications, so partial success is a real state and a binary outcome
does not fit it.** Four posts in and the fifth fails, the audience is looking at a truncated
argument that reads as finished. Report which parts landed with their links, hand back only
the remainder, and never re-publish the parts that succeeded. Whether a half posted thread
should be completed or removed is an adoption question with no good default: removing loses
engagement already earned, leaving it publishes something nobody approved.

**The character limit is per post and the approval was for a piece.** Splitting a piece into
posts is an editorial decision rather than a mechanical one, and it changes where the
emphasis falls. If the split was not part of what was approved, the split itself is the
thing to hand back for approval, not something to perform at publish time.

**Rate limits here are aggressive enough to be part of the design.** A thread is several
calls in quick succession and a burst can be throttled midway, which produces exactly the
partial state above from a healthy account doing nothing wrong. Pace the posts, and treat a
throttle as a pause rather than as a failure of the piece.

## What transfers to any multi part destination

- Where one piece becomes several publications, the outcome is a list rather than a boolean.
- Never re-send the parts that succeeded. Recognising a repeat has to work per part.
- A split, a truncation or a reflow is an editorial change and belongs on the approval side
  of the gate, never on the publishing side.
