# Xiaohongshu as a direct `social` destination

What was learned mapping this recipe onto Xiaohongshu specifically. Nothing here is part of
the recipe: swap the connector and this file stops applying while the recipe does not
change.

## What the mapping has to decide

**Acceptance is not reach, and the destination will not tell you which one you got.** The
post is accepted and then ranked, and several ordinary editorial choices suppress how far it
travels: an external link in the body, an installation command or a block of code, a
superlative, a body that is all product with no scene the reader recognises, a cover that is
large text on empty background. Each of those is accepted and quietly buried, and the
publish result is identical either way. So this recipe's live-or-not verification, which is
the right question on most destinations, is not the question that matters here. Everything
that decides reach has to be settled on the approval side of the gate, because after
publication there is no signal to act on and nothing to act with.

**Publication is irreversible, so the approval gate is the only gate there is.** Nothing
published here can be edited or removed afterwards. The recipe's rule about handing a piece
back rather than trimming it to fit is a preference on most channels; here it is the whole
design, because a trim that publishes something nobody approved cannot be walked back, and
neither can a typo.

**The obvious recovery fails while looking like it worked.** The standard move for a link
that cannot go in the body is to put it in a comment, and comments are filtered on their own
separate rules. A filtered comment stays visible to its author. The publishing account sees
the comment sitting there exactly as intended, and no one else ever does. Any confirmation
this recipe draws from the publishing identity is therefore a confirmation the platform is
not making, and the only honest check is one made from somewhere that is not the poster.

**The publish path cannot read back its own post.** There is no way to fetch what was just
published; the post's identifier is obtained only by searching for it, sorted newest, some
minutes after it went up. An absence in that search is ambiguous rather than negative: the
post may not be indexed yet, or it may have been suppressed. So the recipe's rule of reading
the destination back before retrying costs a wait here, and a retry decision made on the
first empty search is a decision made on no information.

**What the approver saw is not what the audience sees, and the title is counted in
characters.** In the feed the cover is composited over by the title and the author avatar
across roughly the bottom sixth of the image, so anything placed low is gone at the size
most people meet the post. A cover approved as a full frame is published as a cropped one.
The title cap is about twenty characters rather than words, and overrunning it is the single
commonest reason a publish fails outright. Tags are a separate field, so a hash written into
the body is body text and buys nothing.

## What transfers to any destination that ranks what it accepts

- Ask whether the destination can suppress something it accepted. Where it can, the publish
  result is not the outcome, and every editorial rule that governs reach belongs in front of
  the approval gate rather than behind it.
- Where publication cannot be undone, a fit check is never a repair. It has to run before
  the gate or it is decoration.
- A confirmation read from the publishing account is not a confirmation. Check from an
  identity the platform does not know as the author.
- Where the only way to find a post is to search for it, an absence is ambiguous, and the
  wait before that absence means anything is part of the recipe's timing rather than an
  implementation detail.
