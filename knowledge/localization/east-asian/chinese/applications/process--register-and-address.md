# Process - holding a recorded 您 ruling across a 24,778-key zh catalog

**A (as shipped).** The product's style guide rules 您 everywhere and cites the
catalog at 428 您 to 81 你, about 84% formal, as the reason to standardize the
minority. A scripted count of the shipped catalog (24,778 strings) found 757
strings with 您 and 415 with 你: 65% coherence, and none mixed inside one string.
The drift is not in one place; 你 sits in 34 sections, largest in the companion
voice (athena 64, companions 26) and then plugins 55, monitor 32, council 35,
studio 29, agents 28, twin 26. The ruling is recorded and the catalog fell away
from it by growing: the guide's own number was true when it was written.

**The decision the technique does not make.** Two surfaces are a companion
speaking to its user in warm first person (greetings such as 你好,我是 Athena,
陪你一起思考). The technique's exception names a marketing surface inside a 您
product, and says a split must itself be a recorded ruling with a boundary. None
is recorded for the companion voice, so those 90 strings were left alone rather
than flipped in either direction.

**B.** The 325 strings outside the companion sections were standardized by a
scripted 你 to 您 over parsed values (你的, 你自己 follow for free; one 你们双方
recast to 双方, the plural the technique says to avoid).
Coverage gate 0 gaps across 13 locales, key count unchanged. 65% became about 92%
formal by string (1,082 您 of 1,172 pronoun-bearing strings: 757 + 325 against the 90
left); 90 strings remain by design.

**Calibration.** Re-measure the ratio before citing a guide's percentage; a ruling
written at 11.5k keys was 65% by the time the catalog reached 24.8k. Not
measured: ZH-PRONOUN-ONCE (27 strings still carry three or more pronouns, mostly
tour narration, which is a retranslation and not a substitution) and ZH-QING (26
short strings begin with 请, but they are 请求 nouns, not the polite marker, so a
naive 请-prefix grep over-counts).
