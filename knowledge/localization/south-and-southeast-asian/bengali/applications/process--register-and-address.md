# Counting register and politeness tells in two shipped Bengali catalogs

Seam chosen to falsify: the consuming project's style guide asserts zero
তুমি forms and no stacked "please" against hundreds of আপনি. A count could refute
either claim, so the count was the arm.

## Arm A: counted

| Tell (technique anchor) | App catalog (24,778 strings) | Guide (sentences, floors) |
|---|---|---|
| আপনি / আপনার (BN-APNI, expected) | 1,128 | 110 |
| তুমি family (BN-APNI, mixed register) | 14, all in one companion voice | 0 |
| familiar imperative করো-class | 0 addressed to the user; several addressed to the machine (licensed) | 4, all prompt examples |
| দয়া করে / অনুগ্রহ করে (BN-NOPLEASE) | 16 strings, 11 of them routine error or instruction copy | 0 |

BN-NOGENDER produced no findings and no false positives: nothing was flagged for
missing gender marking.

## Arm B

The 11 routine strings (retry prompts, OAuth timeouts, relay failures, a
Docker update line) were rewritten to the bare formal imperative. Five stayed: three
strings that are prompts to an AI, a refine suffix and a quoted release-note phrase. The companion's তুমি lines were not rewritten: the technique's
exception requires a catalog-wide recorded house ruling, which does not exist, so
the finding was filed for the operator rather than resolved by guess. The
coverage gate (14 locales, 0 gaps) held after the edit.

## Reading

Target moved (11 stacked-please strings removed), floor held (gate green, no
other locale touched). The technique's own exception text is what stopped a
wrong rewrite: a per-string choice of register is the defect, not the voice itself.
