# Pre-linked causal mechanisms

A mechanism is a causal story the script will need, written down at research
time as an explicit alternating chain — so the narrative spine is authored
*under evidence*, once, and every render inherits a validated causal
skeleton instead of improvising one at writing time. This is where the
bundle's first law is enforced structurally rather than aspirationally.

## The form

Each mechanism is a chain whose every link carries a typed connector:

```
X happens
  THEREFORE  Y
  BUT        Z complicates it
  THEREFORE  the outcome
```

**Every link is BUT or THEREFORE.** If the only honest connector between two
steps is "and then", you do not have a mechanism — you have a sequence, and
the correct responses are exactly two: find the missing link, or drop the
step. There is no third option where the sequence ships anyway with a softer
word over the joint.

Keep the connector as *data*, not prose. A chain whose connectors are lifted
out of the sentences and into a typed field can be validated mechanically —
counted, gated, diffed — where prose connectors can only be read. If a
rendered-prose form of the chain exists beside the typed form for human
review, the two must not drift; the typed form is authoritative.

## Evidence per link

Each step carries the identifiers of the facts that support **that step** —
not a pile of citations attached to the mechanism as a whole. Mechanism-level
support alone creates a blind spot: a chain can be "well sourced" in
aggregate while its one load-bearing link is supported by nothing. A step
asserting a causal claim that no graded fact supports is flagged, and by the
output-never-outruns-evidence law the rendered beat can be no more causal
than the fact behind it: a correlation-grade fact licenses "moves with",
never "because of".

## The third connector: the non-causal step

A hard-won extension: two connectors are not enough vocabulary for every
honest chain. A deduction in a ledger, a hand-off between parties, a
recognition event — these are real steps with no causal arrow, and a rule
that says "find the missing link or drop it" mutilates them: a five-step
royalty flow loses the two parties whose shares are the subject, because
there *is* no missing link in a deduction. The law was derived from arguments
and was being applied to ledgers.

So the vocabulary admits a typed **transfer** connector for non-causal steps.
Two rules keep it from becoming a loophole:

- **A transfer step is research vocabulary, not a render licence.** The
  script layer's bar stays at zero "and then"; a transfer step that becomes
  an on-screen beat must earn a BUT or a THEREFORE there, or stay off screen.
- **The anti-shape it exists to prevent is the fake THEREFORE** — a causal
  connector extorted from a ledger to pass the link check. That is the
  timeline defect wearing the law's own uniform, and it is worse than a bare
  "and then" because it asserts causation the evidence never contained.

The connector vocabulary running out is a real event: under a bar permitting
zero, researchers write the forbidden word anyway when no permitted word is
honest. Extend the vocabulary; do not rely on discipline against it.

## Procedure

1. For each causal story the eventual script plausibly needs, list the steps
   in prose first.
2. Type every joint: THEREFORE, BUT, or transfer. Anywhere you reach for
   "and then", either hunt the missing link in the evidence or cut the step.
3. Attach supporting fact identifiers to each link; flag any causal link with
   none.
4. Record what the mechanism explains and whether it needs an analogy — the
   render's concrete-assignment step reads both.

## When not to use it

Do not force chains onto material that is genuinely enumerative — a ranked
list, a survey of independent items — where no causal spine exists; there
the honest structure is a list and the craft burden moves to the framing.
And do not build mechanisms speculatively for stories no render will need:
each chain must have a consumer, like every other field of the asset.
