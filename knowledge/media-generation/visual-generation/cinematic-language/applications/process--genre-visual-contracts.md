# A worked genre-contract catalogue — thirteen registers with prompt templates, August 2026

The genre-visual-contracts technique says a genre is a layer table with
an imperfection budget, carried per call. This application IS the worked
catalogue: thirteen contracts compressed from craft research (ASC,
StudioBinder, DP interviews; four research lanes, 2026-08-24), each with
its ready prompt-template core in effect language. Templates are written
for timeline-capable text-to-video models (MiniMax H3 local, Veo 3.x,
Kling 3, Runway Gen-4, Wan 2.2) — prepend shot/timeline structure per
the model's dialect; on the local H3 rig they slot into the trial-matrix
workflow unchanged.

## The thirteen contracts (compressed: light / camera / palette-texture / imperfection budget → template core)

1. **Noir / neo-noir** — hard single-source low-key, venetian-blind
   shadow bars; composed frames, dutch tilts; crushed-black monochrome
   OR slate gloom pierced by neon; smoke prized. → "Hard single-source
   light carving one side of the face, the other in true black; bars of
   venetian-blind shadow raking walls; smoke-thick air; wet pavement
   mirroring signage; deep focus, figure caged in a doorway frame."
2. **Horror, slow-burn** — motivated domestic light curdling; inhumanly
   smooth prowling glide, one-point symmetry, static wides with hungry
   negative space; muted naturalism; restraint itself is the budget. →
   "Quiet interior lit only by household lamps against encroaching dark;
   camera glides forward at a slow, inhumanly smooth prowl down a
   symmetrical corridor; the figure small in a wide static frame that
   holds too long."
3. **Horror, slasher** — night pools of porch light vs blue dark;
   floating first-person stalking, wide frames with loaded empty space;
   autumn warm vs night blue; 70s-80s grain. → "Very wide suburban
   night frame, subject bright at one edge, an ocean of empty dark at
   the other; floating breathing first-person camera drifting toward a
   lit window; a pale shape resolves silently behind the oblivious
   figure."
4. **Psychological thriller** — low-key drained interiors, rain on
   glass; near-lens eyelines, faint cant, mirrors; bleached silvery
   grade, one deep-red accent; 90s grit tolerated. → "Dim interior
   drained of color, rain streaking the window, single practical losing
   to the dark; tight close-up, eyes nearly meeting the lens; a mirror
   doubles the figure; silvery bleached grade with one deep red accent."
5. **Action, chaos school** — found light; handheld reacting like a
   bystander, fragmentary inserts, staccato cutting; desaturated urban
   grey; blur and instability embraced. → "Urgent handheld camera
   reacting to the fight like a bystander, whipping between faces and
   hands, framing tight and partially obstructed; motion blur embraced;
   desaturated cool palette under found light."
6. **Action, precision school** — constructed neon-noir light; gliding
   machine-smooth wides holding unbroken choreography, subject locked
   center; saturated color blooming off wet black; zero-noise gloss;
   slow-motion impact beats. → "Smooth gliding camera holding wide
   full-body framing while the choreography plays unbroken, subject
   dead-center; saturated neon reflections on wet surfaces against rich
   black; a speed ramp into savored slow motion on impact."
7. **Romance / romcom** — high-key soft diffusion, golden even indoors;
   calm classical two-shots and mirrored close-up pairs; creams and warm
   skin, lifted low-contrast; polished, soft bloom. → "Bright high-key
   scene, soft diffused shadowless light, faces glowing warm; balanced
   two-shot of the couple, then matching close-ups whose eyelines meet;
   lifted low-contrast grade, gentle bloom."
8. **Sci-fi** (three modes) — *sterile-clinical*: "shadowless diffused
   white from the architecture itself, slow exact symmetrical corridor
   glide, whites and chrome with a single cold accent, immaculate."
   *Used-future*: "cramped industrial interior lit only by machinery —
   flickering consoles, strobing beacons, backlight through smoke;
   oil-stained greys; heavy vintage grain and halation." *Neon-cyberpunk*:
   "rain-slicked megacity night, hard light-shafts through steam, neon
   smearing across wet streets and faces, figures silhouetted against
   luminous haze; slow monumental drift dwarfing people."
9. **Period drama** — era-honest sources only (candle, window, fire);
   still painterly tableaux, at most one very slow pull-back; amber into
   painterly darkness; grain and flame-halation prized. → "Lit as if by
   candle flame alone, warm flickering amber falling into deep painterly
   darkness, faces modeled like an old-master painting; composition
   still and symmetrical; camera nearly motionless."
10. **Western** — hard high sun exteriors, oil-lamp murk interiors;
    monumental locked wides against extreme brim-shadow close-ups, low
    horizons; sun-bleached earth; dust and heat shimmer. → "Vast
    sun-hammered vista, horizon low, a lone rider tiny against rock
    formations; then a punishingly tight close-up, weathered eyes under
    a hat brim; a dark interior doorway opening onto blinding daylight."
11. **Documentary / vérité** — whatever light is there, blown windows
    kept; shoulder camera finding the shot a beat late, snap zooms to
    reactions; drab ungraded color; mistakes are the register — focus
    hunting, a smudged lens, shake-and-settle. → "Handheld shoulder
    camera reacting a beat late, reframing, settling, then a quick snap
    zoom to a face; lit only by what is there; imperfect opportunistic
    framing, subject half-hidden by foreground clutter; visible focus
    hunting."
12. **Commercial / product** — beauty light built on highlight shape:
    crisp edge-lines, silky gradients on gloss; machine-precise arcs,
    extreme macro; saturated appetite color; zero noise tolerated;
    ultra-slow-motion event beats. → "Pristine studio tabletop, product
    hero-centered on a seamless backdrop, a long silky highlight sliding
    across its glossy curve; extreme macro on condensation beads; the
    camera glides in a perfectly smooth machine-precise arc; a pour in
    luxurious ultra-slow motion."
13. **Coming-of-age / indie naturalism** — golden-hour available light,
    backlit hair halos, flares drifting; close unobtrusive handheld
    breathing with the subject; warm faded milky grade, soft grain;
    lingering observational pace. → "Late-afternoon golden hour, low sun
    backlighting her hair into a warm halo, lens flares drifting;
    handheld camera hovering close and unobtrusive; warm faded grade,
    milky highlights; lingering on small gestures."

## Mixing, parody, and model dialects (2026-08 facts)

- **Layer-split mixing** (iconography carries genre A; light/grade/camera
  carry genre B) is the pattern the successful hybrids use — the
  tech-noir lineage carries noir in the lighting layer and sci-fi in the
  production-design layer.
- **Parody calibration**: played-dead-straight duplication of a look with
  absurd content is the strongest comedy register; unintended parody is
  almost always one over-committed layer (usually texture or trope
  density).
- **Vendor prompt dialects** (all official guides, accessed 2026-08-24):
  Veo 3.1 leads with cinematography in a five-part formula and supports
  timestamped multi-shot; the Sora 2 cookbook wants modular
  scene/cinematography/action blocks and notes shorter = freedom;
  Runway Gen-4 wants motion directed in plain language, no keyword
  stuffing; Kling 3 wants 20-50 precise words; MiniMax H3 wants the
  six-block timeline with integrated camera sentences (see the
  trial-matrix application — timeline blocks, coarse shot grammar and
  gaffer-speak lighting all verified honored on the local rig).
  Decompose director-style requests into explicit layer specs rather
  than name-dropping.
