# Process: a spec ad whose style block was the attractor, written down

The case is the operator's *Personas Production Bible* v2.2 (13 Oct 2026), a 42-second
wordless spec ad for a fictional brand made for the ElevenLabs "The Search" contest. The
bible is kept outside the registry. Keyframes were made in Leonardo with GPT Image 2.5
("Sunburst", Medium), and dry-run motion in Veo 3.1 Lite. The prompt system is the
strongest application of this subject's control techniques in the fleet:

- every prompt is compiled from verbatim blocks;
- references come before the storyboard, with a role map;
- identity is split from state;
- end frames are edits of start frames;
- exclusions are positive prose.

All 27 final frames hold the faces, the box and the glasses. The operator's verdict on
the result was "lacked execution quality and creativity".

## The style block

Restated verbatim on every shot prompt:

> "Cinematic photoreal film still from a premium commercial, shot on a large-format cinema
> camera with anamorphic lenses: shallow depth of field with the background softly
> defocused, oval bokeh, faint horizontal flares from bright practical lights, natural
> skin texture, fine film grain, rich but restrained contrast with detailed shadows.
> 16:9 widescreen frame."

Against step 2 of the technique, five of its phrases are category-quality register:
"cinematic", "film still", "premium commercial", "large-format cinema camera" and
"anamorphic". Three more pin the attractor's optical signature directly: shallow depth of
field, oval bokeh and practical flares. The block contains no light *source* and no
palette. The location blocks supply the palette (magenta and cyan neon, blue screen
light, overcast daylight), and the model supplied the rest.

## What the frames show (the step 1 list, read off the contact sheet)

Counted on the 27 final keyframes, 2026-10-15:

| Axis | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Warm practical lamps against a cool night | 21 of 27. The six Copenhagen daylight frames are the only exception, and the location block put it there, not the style block |
| Camera at or near eye level | 24 of 27. The lid insert is overhead; the Mumbai street pair sits slightly low |
| Subject centred or near centre | every glasses-on frame (8 of 8, by design for the match cut) and most others |
| Shallow focus with bokeh | most frames with a background. The Mumbai street pair and the Copenhagen studio frames hold more depth |
| Magic rendered as glowing threads, strokes or particles of light | 5 of 5 magic frames (M1 to M4, plus the convergence shot) |

The framing rule was in the action blocks, and most action blocks said "Medium shot".
So the sameness of distance came from the shot list, and the sameness of look came from
the style block. That is exactly the scope split the golden path draws.

## Three cases through steps 2 and 3 (simulation)

- **S1a, the guitarist at the low table.** The attractor items are: neon through
  blinds, a warm lamp, eye level, medium distance, bokeh. A passing rewrite keeps the
  neon as the single light source and names what falls to black. It moves the camera to
  floor level across the table surface, keeps deep focus so the crumpled staff paper on
  the floor reads, and drops the practical lamp.
- **M2, the tiger leaving the wall.** The attractor items are: light strokes and
  particles, and a glowing animal. A passing rewrite makes the effect out of the scene's
  own material: wet paint peeling off the concrete in sheets, drips running *upward* in
  the rain, the tiger still half brush-stroke. It is wide and locked off, with the
  painter a small figure on the ladder.
- **G-Ga, glasses on.** The match cut legitimately needs one framing across four
  makers. That is a formal device and it is kept. The attractor items it inherited are
  bokeh, the warm key light and a glossy face. The rewrite keeps the device and changes
  the register around it: hard frontal light, deep focus, the room sharp behind each
  maker.

## Verdict

**Unmeasurable.** The diagnostic half of the technique is measured: the attractor list
above is counted, not felt, and it traces one-to-one to the style block's words and the
shot list's distances. The prescriptive half has not been rendered. No inverted prompt
has been generated on the target model, so whether the rewritten frames are better is
open.
