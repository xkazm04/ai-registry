# Round 3 of a data-video studio: concept/landing challengers won all four cases

The data-video studio from `process--leader-adaptive-theme.md` (StatReel)
ran a third triage round on five new cases, rendered as stills with a
React video framework.
- **Rejected:** one case (exoplanets), whose case and visuals were "not
  creative enough".
- **First pass:** the owner picked data-chart winners: a fan chart (GDP),
  a vital-signs monitor (life expectancy in 1918), a share dial (OS share,
  March 2017) and a vertical number line with a ghost (F1 wins at Spa 2001).
- **Challengers:** one vertical concept/landing still per case, briefed from
  the landing mode of the `contest` skill.
- **Verdict:** **all four challengers won**: "the contest/landing approach
  turned the concepts into more creative visuals."

## The four winners

- **GDP, cut-paper skyline.** Each economy is a tower on one height scale.
  Solid windowed floors show the latest year and scaffolding shows the
  IMF projection, with SPECULATIVE brackets. There are three depth rows and
  a gold sun behind the leader. A ×30 rooftop lens shows the gap
  (≈ $5 bn).
- **Life expectancy, candle chapel.** Candle height is 1918 life
  expectancy, and a dashed "burned-away wax" ghost shows 1913. There are two
  shelves under a chapel arch, a smoking flame on the worst-hit country, and
  the world average at headline size (36.8 → 24.3).
- **OS share, balance scale.** A drawn PC faces a drawn phone, each with
  its own share sparkline on its screen, at 39.02 vs 39.04. The headline
  reads "Tipped by 0.02 points". The rest of the top 10 are brass weights
  sized by the square root of share.
- **F1 wins, ghost on the grid.** One lap of a drawn circuit equals 90
  wins. Each driver is a top-down car in team colours placed at distance =
  wins. A dashed, glowing ghost car sits at 87 with a dotted trail, and a ×4
  infield lens shows 52 passing 51.

## Costs observed (and the fixes)

- **Squeezed entries:**
  - The GDP back row ran small.
  - The F1 pack of six drivers between 22 and 27 wins sat in about 120 px,
    so their labels were moved into the infield as a stacked legend.
  - These cases are why the procedure says "legend strip or zoom lens".
- **Lost change over time:**
  - The provisional winners had won on trajectory.
  - The candle's burned-away ghost and the sparklines drawn on the devices
    were the devices that kept time in the frame.
  - The motion plan borrows the runners-up's traces: towers growing floor by
    floor, candles burning down, the scale swinging, cars lapping.
- **Overflow:**
  - The first balance-scale render put the phone's pan inside the right
    icon column and left a dead band above the beam.
  - Narrowing the beam and adding the headline claim fixed both.
