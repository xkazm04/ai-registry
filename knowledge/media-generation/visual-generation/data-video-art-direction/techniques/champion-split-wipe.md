# Champion split-wipe

The preferred transition when a title or lead changes hands, from the boxing
experiment (t2, "the upset"): a **jagged diagonal seam splits the frame**.
The outgoing champion is desaturated and pushed out on one side, and the
incoming champion enters from the other in their own palette. The colour
change *is* the story beat.

## Procedure

1. Give each champion a palette (leader-adaptive-theme), e.g. Tyson red and
   Douglas blue.
2. Run a diagonal seam (top x ≈ 700 → bottom x ≈ 330 at 1080 wide) across
   the frame. Add a hot white edge and sparks as the one motion cue.
3. The outgoing side: desaturated portrait, dimmed ground, its record struck
   through ("37–0" → "37–1 · reign ends"). The incoming side: full colour
   and the new title data.
4. Pair it with a rolling clock or counter as the second cue, at most.

## Decision rules

- Use it only for real changes of champion. Ordinary rank swaps use eased
  row moves.
- Portraits follow photo-to-poster-portrait and photo-rights-ledger.

## When not to use it

- **Frequent leader changes** (several per minute). The wipe loses meaning;
  use the background cross-fade instead.
