# Ending sequence

The question set up by the hook is answered once, clearly. Then the video
asks for the follow and signs off.

## Procedure

1. **Winner spotlight (~2 s):** the timeline stops. Everything except the
   winner dims to about 20 % (a notable runner-up can stay around 65 %).
   The winner's block scales up ~8 % and one verified card states the
   result (e.g. "#1 in all 213 months", plus the runner-up's record).
2. **Like & subscribe (~2 s):** "Like & subscribe for more like this", with
   thumbs-up and bell icons in the video's palette. One motion cue (the
   ringing bell).
3. **Powered by StatReel (~3 s):** the **Frame Stat** logo (a film frame with
   sprocket holes and a rising bar chart inside). The frame takes the
   video's ink, the bars take the video's palette, and the bars rise as the
   build-in. "StatReel" uses the ink plus the accent colour, with the
   tagline "data in, short video out". At most one ambient cue, e.g. a faint
   film strip drifting behind.

## Decision rules

- The spotlight card repeats only facts checked against the data.
- Total ending ≈ 7 s inside a 36–38 s Short.
- The logo is recoloured per video, never re-drawn: the geometry is fixed
  (100×100 grid), and the colorway is chosen per case.

## When not to use it

- **Series episodes inside a compilation:** keep only the spotlight and
  sign off once at the end.
