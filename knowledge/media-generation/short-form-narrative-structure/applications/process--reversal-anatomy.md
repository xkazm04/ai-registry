# Reversal anatomy, measured and corrected — a video-studio pattern study

How one generative video studio (repo: `gravitone-gcloud`) derived,
falsified, and repaired its own reversal doctrine across transcript studies
— a worked example of treating narrative craft as a measured process
rather than taste.

## The six-step derivation

`knowledge/templates/short-educational-video/steps/01-script/PATTERNS.md`
lines 198–215 extract the anatomy from a long-form economics essay, with
each step quoted against the transcript: (1) let the viewer settle — the
obvious reading built generously ("more exports means more output… brighter
lights"); (2) signal the turn ("But, this is where things get economically
interesting"); (3) mechanism, not verdict — the GDP-accounting argument,
net effect roughly zero; (4) make it concrete — the December fridge that
counts toward this year's GDP; (5) generalize so it isn't a trick — the
same rule applied to a US petroleum-reserve thought experiment,
"pre-empting the objection that the argument was rigged"; (6) escalate once
— two-way trade means the boom could show up as *shrinkage*.

The doctrine layer beneath it, `knowledge/CRAFT-BASELINE.md:13-39`, supplies
the law the anatomy instantiates: between adjacent beats you must be able to
say "but" or "therefore" — a reversal is the "but" made load-bearing — and
the anti-pattern list at PATTERNS.md:355-372 names the failure the anatomy
prevents: "the unearned reversal — 'but actually, it's not that simple!'
with no mechanism behind it."

## The correction — the part worth copying

PATTERNS.md:217-247 records a first-pass inference (a 60–90 second floor
per reversal, hence "shorts can hold at most one") being **falsified by
measurement**: a 64-second physics short runs two full reversals at ~18
seconds each (transcript timestamps at PATTERNS.md:225-229). The repair is
not a new duration rule but a *structural* one, tabulated at
PATTERNS.md:233-239: steps 1, 2, 3, and 6 survive compression (settle
drops to ~11s with data; the turn signal to four words); **steps 4 and 5
are what a short script cannot afford**. The cost is named rather than
hidden (PATTERNS.md:245-247): without the generalization step a short
reversal can feel like a trick, and both short sources compensate by
staying rigorously quantitative through the turn — the numbers do the work
the generalization would have done.

The same file's Appendix A (PATTERNS.md:417-458) keeps delivery downstream
of the anatomy: 197–252 wpm measured across four sources, budgets stated
as essay time net of sponsor reads, and surface metrics (hedges, numbers,
second person) reinterpreted as consequences of engine and subject —
"never dials to set independently."

## Transferable shape

The process pattern is bigger than reversals: state each craft claim with
an evidence grade (the file tags every paragraph OBSERVED / MEASURED /
INFERRED, and corrections are kept inline with dates, e.g. "CORRECTED
2026-08-11" at PATTERNS.md:217), re-measure inferences against new
sources, and prefer repairs that say *what to drop* over repairs that say
*don't try*. A studio that writes its narrative doctrine this way can hand
it to a generator as an executable checklist — and knows which rules are
load-bearing when the next measurement disagrees.
