# Process: "Personas — put them on", production bible v2.2 (13 Oct 2026)

The case is the operator's own spec ad for the ElevenLabs contest (rubric in
[the companion application](./process--rubric-as-spine.md)). It is documented in a
production bible kept outside the registry. Version 2.2 is dated 13 Oct 2026: 27
keyframes final, 19 dry-run motion clips, sound effects generated, score planned.
The bible and its contact sheet were read on 2026-10-15.

## The premise as written

A 42-second wordless spot for a fictional brand, "Personas": glasses in an oak box,
with an AI orchestration platform as subtext. The structure:

1. **Struggle (0-14 s).** Four makers (a guitarist in Tokyo, a muralist in Mumbai, a
   developer in Bangalore, an architect in Copenhagen) each stall on their work.
2. **Discovery (14-21 s).** Each finds a glowing oak box and puts on the glasses
   inside, intercut as a match cut.
3. **Magic (21-35 s).** "Their craft comes alive":
   - golden light threads from the strings form an orchestra that plays itself;
   - light strokes complete the mural, and the tiger leaps out;
   - a swarm of light streams from the lenses and fixes the code;
   - light lines build the missing half of the model while the architect "opens her
     arms wide".
4. **Convergence and end card (35-42 s).**

Sound is an instrumental psychedelic-rock score with a recurring four-note motif and
wordless vocals at the peak only. The production engineering is strong. There are
identity reference sheets, face sheets and glasses-on variants, prompts compiled from
locked blocks, end frames made as edits of start frames, and a measured credit
budget.

## Fate: fails the technique

Run the procedure against the bible's own shot descriptions:

- **Agency line.** It cannot be written in the required form. In every magic beat the
  causal agent is the light. Strokes "complete the mural by themselves". The
  instruments play "on their own, with no players". The swarm fixes the code while
  the developer's hands move on the keyboard. The architect "opens her arms wide" as
  the model builds itself. The person's action at the climax is awe. The technique
  names that expression as the replacement premise's signature.
- **Reversal test.** Remove the glasses, and what remains is four people who stopped
  working. The premise is a replacement premise.
- **Visual signature.** The product's effect is autonomous coloured light and
  particles in all four beats: the model's default "magic" and a convention viewers
  read as "something special" without knowing what.
- **Subtext.** The orchestration product's real human role is the conductor's, and
  that seat is absent. Nobody directs anything. Things happen to them.

Two adjacent findings sit in sibling techniques and are only noted here:

- **The rubric.** The wordless instrumental score with a motif and no sung,
  name-carrying hook gives up most of the contest's heaviest criterion (see
  rubric-as-spine).
- **The montage.** Four parallel vignettes with identical beats are the montage of
  equals named in the golden path's failure modes.

## What a passing premise looks like for this brief

These are sketches of the *form* only. Writing the redesigned ad is the operator's
next round and not this document's job.

- **The person conducts.** One maker's ordinary actions (a tap, a hum, a pencil
  stroke) are heard as parts of a song the glasses let them hear, and each action adds
  a voice. The hook carrying the brand name is what they build. The glasses reveal
  and connect, and the person performs.
- **Extreme consequence.** The glasses work too well. Every small gesture of the
  wearer is amplified into something absurdly large, and the wearer has to learn to
  direct it, which is the orchestration subtext played as comedy. The final beat
  hands the act back to the person.
- **The ensemble.** The four makers stay, but each contributes one layer of the hook
  from their own craft, and the layers lock into one chorus across the cities. The
  montage becomes a mechanism, and the glasses connect the makers rather than
  finishing their work.

## What the case adds

The bible fails the technique, measured by reading its shot text against the
procedure. No production comparison has been run yet. The case also marks a boundary the technique did not state before this
run: **the premise failed despite exemplary control.** The identity locking, block
compilation and end-frame editing in this bible are among the most careful in the
fleet's media work. Control disciplines cannot detect this failure, because by their
measures nothing failed.
