# Path over conclusion

A data video whose payoff is the last frame ("and the winner is...")
asks the viewer to sit through a minute of motion that doesn't matter.
Viewers leave. The ideas that hold are interesting **all the way
through**: the lead changes hands, someone crashes, a newcomer jumps, an
old giant comes back. Path over conclusion makes that explicit with a
written keep-watching rationale and a format choice that favours the
middle of the video.

## Procedure

1. **Write the keep-watching rationale** before scoring: one or two
   sentences that walk through the moments that will keep someone
   watching second by second. It must name entities and dates ("X leads
   until 2007, Y passes it in March 2011, Z collapses in 2016"). A
   rationale that only says the topic is "interesting" or "keeps
   changing" fails.
2. **Find the middle.** If the rationale only describes the start and the
   end, the path is empty. Look for a framing with more mid-video events:
   a finer granularity, more entities, or a different ranking.
3. **Prefer comparative views.** A top-10 or top-20 race, many countries
   on a map, or many flags at once produce overtakes. A single line or a
   single record holder produces a conclusion.
4. **Put the hook in the tension**, not in the answer: the expectation
   the viewer brings ("Apple was always first") against what the path
   shows (four different leaders).

## Decision rules

- Rationale checks are mechanical where possible: minimum length, at
  least two dates, at least two named entities, no filler words.
- A conclusion-only idea can be rescued by changing the framing (a map
  of winners becomes a cumulative race of totals), not by better
  animation.
- Documentary human stories (one person's comeback, one season) fail
  this gate in a narration-free format, however strong the story is.

## When not to use it

- **Reveal formats on purpose**, such as a guessing game where the final
  frame is the point and the format tells the viewer to wait.
- **Reference charts** meant to be paused and read rather than watched.
