# Saturation check

One comparable with millions of views proves that a slot was open when
that video was published. It doesn't prove the slot is open now. The
saturation check looks for **counter-evidence**: recent videos on the same
topic in the same form that did badly.

## Procedure

1. Search the topic plus the form ("<topic> bar chart race",
   "<topic> every year") and sort the results mentally by recency, not by
   views.
2. Record the views of the most recent five to ten matches.
3. If recent matches consistently sit an order of magnitude or more
   below the headline comparable, the slot is **saturated**. Score the
   idea on the recent median, not on the historical outlier.
4. Record the counter-evidence in the research log next to the
   precedent. Both are kept.

## Decision rules

- A cluster where competent recent uploads sit at a few thousand views
  is closed to a plain version of the idea. Reopen it only with a
  distinctly different angle (a new cast, a new measure, a new span) and
  re-check.
- Sports totals, energy mixes, crypto, export rankings, causes-of-death
  and "fastest to N users" races were saturated when this was written.
  Treat them as closed until fresh evidence says otherwise.
- Saturation is a property of topic plus form, not of the topic alone.
  The same subject can be saturated as a bar race and open as a map or a
  record ladder.

## When not to use it

- For genuinely new data (a dataset released this year): there can't be
  saturation yet, and the check would only find the precedent's own
  sparseness.
