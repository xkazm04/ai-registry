# Saturation evidence from a data-video ideation loop

The `StatReel` ideation loop (see process--precedent-evidence-gate) kept
counter-evidence next to precedent in `content-ideas/research-log.json`.
These clusters were treated as closed on 2026-10-03 because competent
recent uploads sat between 1K and 30K views:

| Cluster | Typical recent views |
|---|---|
| World Cup top-scorer bar races | ~12K |
| Super Bowl winners races | ~10K |
| NBA titles / all-time scorer races | under 1K to ~2K |
| Solar, energy-mix, crypto, top-exporter races | low thousands |
| "Fastest to 100M users" shorts | low thousands |
| Causes-of-death races | low thousands |
| Single-player goals races (Ovechkin, Euro scorers) | low thousands |

In the same period, every-year map histories and size ladders kept
earning millions. The rule that came out of it: saturation belongs to
topic plus form, so the same subject was often re-pitched as a map or a
record ladder rather than dropped.
