# Engine catalogue by viewer pleasure

The catalogue is the selection system's vocabulary: a small, named set of
narrative machines, each defined by the specific pleasure it delivers to the
viewer. This technique is how the catalogue is built, keyed, evidenced, and
kept honest — because a bad catalogue poisons every selection made from it.

## Key by pleasure, not mechanics

Each entry's primary key is **what the viewer gets**: being corrected; the
disproportion between labour and reward; pattern recognition across domains;
watching a question get settled; being oriented on something new; one idea
compounding; a contradiction that must resolve. The pleasure is the
distinguishing property because it is what the structure must keep delivering
beat after beat — two engines can share a subject, a length, even most of
their facts, and still produce completely different videos because they serve
different pleasures.

Keying by surface mechanics fails in both directions. "Uses an analogy"
merges the pattern-recognition engine (where the analogy IS the machine) with
every engine that merely decorates with one. "Has a twist" merges the
correction engine (whose twist attacks the viewer's belief) with the
contradiction engine (whose twist resolves a paradox the viewer never held a
position on). When two catalogue entries blur together, the repair is always
to re-ask what the viewer is enjoying in each — if the answer is the same,
they are one engine; if different, the mechanics were never the key.

## Each entry carries a contract, a beat skeleton, and witnesses

A usable entry states four things:

1. **The material precondition** — the shape the research must contain: a
   claim a reasonable person could dispute; a mechanism a viewer could
   operate; a principle with a surprising instance; several competing
   explanations; genuinely new news; naturally ordered difficulty; a flat
   contradiction. This is what fit assessment tests against.
2. **The beat skeleton** — the ordered moves, each linked to the next by
   "but" or "therefore," never by "and then"
   ([causality-over-sequence](../../../_laws.md#causality-over-sequence)). The
   skeleton includes the engine's *load-bearing move*: the one beat that
   makes it this engine and not a neighbor — the self-attack in a correction
   chain, the "notice what's missing" in an effort/payoff demonstration, the
   defeat-link between rungs of a ladder. An entry without its load-bearing
   move named will be imitated by shape and faked in substance.
3. **Witnesses** — real published videos whose full read grounds the entry,
   with counts and timings taken from them. State n honestly: an engine with
   one witness is a hypothesis; measured figures inherit the witness pool's
   date window and may not be quoted as current practice.
4. **Length budgets, not length identity.** The engine is the same machine at
   every duration; what the entry records per length is the budget — how many
   turns, rungs, or candidates fit — because that is the only thing that
   actually varies.

## Maintain it as an open instrument

- **Open, not settled.** New witnessed videos either strengthen an entry,
  split one entry into two (the pleasures turned out to differ), or found a
  new entry. A catalogue that stops admitting witnesses becomes taste with
  headings.
- **Record composition patterns.** When a witnessed video runs one engine as
  spine and another as final act, file that as a composition precedent on
  both entries — composition is learned from witnesses like everything else.
- **Keep the exit row.** The catalogue's last entry is "none of these," and
  its verdict is "not a video yet." Removing that row converts the catalogue
  from a diagnostic instrument into a forcing function (see
  no-engine-means-no-video).
- **Separate skeleton from execution when filing a flawed exemplar.** A video
  can witness an engine's structure while modeling dishonest execution. File
  the skeleton, and explicitly mark the execution as not-to-encode, naming
  which moves are persuasion rather than explanation — otherwise a generator
  trained on the catalogue reproduces the flaws as faithfully as the shape.

## When not to use

Do not build a catalogue from first principles with no witnesses — an
unwitnessed entry is a plausible shape with no evidence it holds attention,
and it will be selected with the same confidence as the measured ones. And do
not fork the catalogue per duration or per platform; fork only when a
pleasure genuinely splits, which is rarer than it looks.
