# No engine means no video

The engine catalogue's last row is an exit: when the material fits none of
the machines, the verdict is **not a video yet** — it is a topic, and the
correct next action is to go find the tension, not to force the nearest
engine. This technique is that verdict as a first-class pipeline state: how
to reach it honestly, what forcing looks like, and the cheap diagnostics that
catch a forced render after the fact.

## Zero fit is a blocker, not a challenge

Every engine's precondition is a shape in the material: a disputable claim,
an operable mechanism, competing explanations, ordered difficulty, a
contradiction. When none of those shapes exists, the material has no source
of obligation between beats — nothing that makes beat n+1 *necessary* given
beat n — and no amount of production craft supplies it downstream. Under
[causality-over-sequence](../../../_laws.md#causality-over-sequence), beats that
cannot be joined by "but" or "therefore" are a list; rendering a list with an
engine's costume produces the wiki timeline: true, complete, competently
narrated, and abandoned, because the viewer is never given a reason to stay
that the next beat then honors.

The blocker verdict is *productive*, not terminal. "Go find the tension"
names concrete work: research deeper until a disputable claim or a genuine
contest surfaces; narrow the topic until one mechanism becomes operable;
wait, if the subject is too young to have facts. A topic failing the gate
today routinely passes it after one more research pass — what the gate
prevents is spending full production cost learning that.

## Two forcing patterns to know by name

- **The chronology dressed as a ladder.** Dates supply order for free, so any
  production timeline trivially "fits" an ordered-difficulty engine — and
  falsely, because that engine's contract is that each rung *defeats the
  previous solution*, while a timeline's rungs merely follow one another.
  Worse than boring: monotonic escalation over an identifiable team's
  decisions is a blame frame, asserting by shape that it got steadily worse
  and someone let it. Require the "but" between rungs; if the only honest
  link is "and then," this is the timeline in a ladder's costume.
- **The rule imported to mask an evidence vacuum.** The pattern-transfer
  engine needs a rule from a *different* domain, so it fits topics about
  which almost nothing is established — its availability rises exactly as
  every other engine's falls. A pipeline whose only surviving fit on a thin
  topic is the analogy engine is usually looking at a zero-fit topic wearing
  one engine's costume; audit whether the subject-side facts carry any load
  before treating it as a fit.

Both patterns share a signature: the "fit" was supplied by something order
and analogy give away for free, not by the material's own tension.

## Diagnostics — catching a forced render mechanically

Selection claims are testable on the finished script, cheaply and without
judging the subject:

- **AND-THEN count: must be zero.** Any adjacent pair of beats whose only
  honest connector is "and then" is the timeline surfacing inside whatever
  engine was claimed. This is the one diagnostic with an absolute threshold,
  and a zero can be forged. A "therefore" written where the research holds
  only sequence passes the count and asserts a causation the evidence never
  contained, which is worse than the honest "and then" it replaced — so the
  count is only as good as the rule that every causal joint names what it
  rests on
  ([pre-linked-causal-mechanisms](../../../research-grounding/content-research-grounding/techniques/pre-linked-causal-mechanisms.md)).
  Viewers infer causation between adjacent beats by default, so a
  deliberate non-link ("the schedule did not respond to the scope change")
  must be spoken, not merely left unconnected.
- **Causal-opener density** — the share of sentences opening with a causal
  or additive connector (but, so, and, because, therefore). Observed across a
  ten-video witness corpus it ranged roughly 15% to 40%. It measures how
  *explicitly* a script marks its links, and that is largely the writer's
  voice: two same-engine writers of comparable quality in that corpus ran
  14.6 and 32.0 connectives per thousand words, and cohesion counts do not
  predict rated writing quality. Compare a script with its writer's own
  baseline; a low score is a reason to read the joints, not a verdict that
  the script asserts.
- **Turn count** — reversals or transfers per minute. The witnessed band runs
  from about one per thirty seconds in shorts to one per four minutes in long
  essays; a script with no turns at all never engaged any engine.

Wire these as a render-exit gate, and report them honestly: a script that was
never measured is *unmeasured*, not passing —
[unmeasured-is-not-pass](../../../_laws.md#unmeasured-is-not-pass). The gate
exists because the drift is one-directional: unstructured material relaxes
into a list by default, so a pipeline that stops measuring will re-grow
timelines while still stamping engine names on them.

## When not to use

Do not swing the blocker into a quality bar for *finished* research — a topic
with a real but modest tension fits an engine modestly, and that is a
legitimate small video, not a blocked one. The gate tests for *absence* of
shape, not weakness of shape. And do not apply the AND-THEN rule to
intra-beat narration mechanics (a two-item enumeration inside one beat);
its jurisdiction is the joints *between* beats, where the engine lives.

Nor is every sequence a forced render. A ranked countdown or a survey held
under one declared, unanswered question keeps viewers across beats joined by
nothing but order, because the obligation is curiosity about a stated gap
rather than derivation. That is a different contract from the engines here,
not a failure of theirs: judge it by whether the gap is declared up front
and paid at the end, not by its AND-THEN count. The rule's origin is a
screenwriters' rule of thumb that "and then" beats are boring; no retention
study behind it has been found, so treat "viewers leave" as the reason for
the rule, not a measurement of it.
