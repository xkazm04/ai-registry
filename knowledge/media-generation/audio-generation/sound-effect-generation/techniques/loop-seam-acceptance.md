# Loop-seam acceptance

An ambience that repeats has one property that dominates every other: the
**seam** — the joint where the end meets the beginning again. A loop's
seam is a defect multiplier: inaudible once, it becomes a landmark by the
third repeat and a metronome by the tenth, because the ear is a
change-detector and the one thing it never stops detecting is the same
change arriving on schedule. Every quality the ambience has is rented
against the seam holding.

## Declare the loop at generation

Where the scheme supports it, loop intent is declared in the request —
current schemes expose it as an explicit flag that generates the ending
to blend into the beginning — and a loop generated as a loop beats a
loop cut from a linear take in exactly the way a planned property beats a
recovered one. Declaring it also changes the brief: a loopable body wants
**no event structure** — nothing distinctive enough to become the
landmark that marks each pass. The enemy of a loop is a feature; the
distinctive gust that makes a linear wind bed interesting is precisely
what makes the looped version count time out loud.

## Test at the seam, in use

The acceptance check is mechanical and cheap, and skipping it is how
seams ship:

- **Play across the joint at least twice through** — not once. The first
  pass calibrates the ear; the second is where a level step, a spectral
  jump, or a truncated tail at the joint becomes audible.
- **Test at the repeat count the use implies.** A bed under a 90-second
  scene loops its 20-second self four times; the test is those four
  minutes as the audience gets them, not one boutique listen. Fatigue
  and pulse-detection are properties of repetition, and only repetition
  reveals them.
- **Check the joint's spectrum, not just its level.** A seam can match
  loudness and still jump timbre — a tail rich in low end meeting a head
  that starts dry reads as a cut even at matched level.

A loop that fails only at the seam is a candidate for the fallback
repair — a short crossfade over the joint — which trades a possible
soft blur for the hard edge. Take the trade when the body is otherwise
right; re-generate with loop intent when the scheme supported it and the
request never said so, because that failure was free to prevent.

## Refusal shape

Some material will not loop: a bed whose body evolves directionally
(building wind, thickening rain) has no honest joint, and the "loop" of
it is a contradiction the brief should surface rather than the generator
paper over. The honest outcomes are the loopable re-brief (the same
world, static body) or the linear deliverable long enough for the scene
— and which one is a spotting-level decision, made where the duration is
known, not defaulted at the generator.

## When not to use this

One-shot elements — hits, risers, anything with an event shape — never
loop and need none of this. And music beds that repeat are the
composition craft's problem at a different scale: a musical loop seams
on the bar line by construction, and its acceptance is tempo and key
continuity, not the featureless-body discipline above.
