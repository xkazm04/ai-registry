# The owner's read against twenty model panels

mage-arena (public, github.com/xkazm04/mage-arena) ran a two-day look push toward a stylised concept. About
twenty blind panels of three model judges each scored it, with a control frame in every set. The record is
in the tree, read at `0dc4a18` on 2026-10-29.

## What the tree records

## What happened

- The panels plateaued at 24 to 26 of 36 for four rounds, against a go bar of 30.
- One frame scored 26.3 in one set and 25.3 in the next.
- One panel preferred thin drawn strands for an effect over broad sheets. The builders switched, and the
  next panel preferred the sheets. The switch cost a round.
- The owner read the last round at about 20. That put the panels about 4 to 5 points generous, and the
  real gap at about 10 points, not 4 to 6.

## Verdict: better

The owner's reading decided the project; the panels' totals had suggested two more rounds would reach the
bar. Taking the anchor at the first round would have moved the stop decision about two days earlier.

## Deviation

The panels' per-judge marks and the owner's read were taken in the session and are summarised, not
committed. The return condition for `reconciled`: a run note per round with the panel marks, the control
score and the owner's anchor.
