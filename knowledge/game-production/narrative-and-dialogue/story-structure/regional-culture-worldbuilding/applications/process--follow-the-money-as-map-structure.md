# The league's supply chain as Death Ride's region order, and its balance sheet

This application realizes the supply-chain map and, with it, the political economy of the
spectacle on Death Ride, a top-down vehicular combat racer for a television whose campaign
runs from a salvage yard to the league boss Marrow's speedway. The tree is the
`firetv-deathride` worktree at `9ceb02d7`, read on 2026-10-04; the material is the research
dossiers in `docs/narrative/research/` (R4 sections 1, 7, 10 and options 12 and 13; R1 P14, D3
and D13). Nothing has been played; this is a plan reconciled against research. It is a
`process` application: it specifies the region order, the results screen and the crowd as a
faction, not code.

## The order is the chain

R4's option 13 states the technique on Death Ride's existing order, Scrap, Foundry, Salt and
quarry, Switchback, Crown:
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:151 "The player follows the money upstream to its owner"`.
Its recommendation is the technique's defend-the-order rule, with the trade made explicit:
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:151 "Swapping Salt and Switchback would reach the sibling earlier but break both the supply-chain logic and the altitude arc."`
and
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:151 "Recommendation: do not reorder."`
The altitude arc — a climb into thin air before the descent to the Crown — is the upward lesson
the technique took: the terrain can be made to follow the chain, so the route has a crescendo.

The goods are kept few, per R4's high-confidence pattern from a film's on-screen world:
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:12 "define it by two or three goods everyone needs and who controls each"`
(Cinephilia & Beyond, https://cinephiliabeyond.org/mad-max-fury-road/). R1 adds the crossing
rule the technique adopted, rated medium because the reading is partly the researcher's:
`docs/narrative/research/R1-prestige-series-craft.md:185 "and through who can cross"`, with the
implication rule beside it,
`docs/narrative/research/R1-prestige-series-craft.md:185 "Characters should rarely name the system. They should live inside its prices."`
(Fantasy/Animation essay, https://www.fantasy-animation.org/current-posts/how-arcane-riot-games-fortiche-production-2021-visualizes-class-conflict-through-its-fantasy-world).

## Where the plan falls short

**The chain is claimed, not tuned.** Nothing in the dossiers sets prices, purses or repair costs
per region from the chain's position; the edge is called poor and the centre rich in prose
only. Under the technique's single-authority rule the race economy's tables own those numbers,
and until they are checked against the chain the claim is unreconciled — the same gap the
antagonist subject's application records for scarcity.

**Crossings are not staged.** R1's "who can cross" has no Death Ride realization yet: the gate
Rook controls is the only crossing the bible names, and the passes between the other regions
are transitions, not places.

**Every link here is inference.** The supply-chain ordering, the altitude crescendo, the
draining crowd and the results-screen split are the research's own proposals
(`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:230 "every option and table entry in sections 11 and 12"`);
none is an owner decision and none has been played.
