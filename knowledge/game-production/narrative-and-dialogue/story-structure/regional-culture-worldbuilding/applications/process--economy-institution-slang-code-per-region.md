# From palette to culture: Death Ride's five regions

This application realizes the per-region culture fields, the dissenter and the price of joining
on Death Ride, a top-down vehicular combat racer for a television whose campaign crosses five
regions toward the league boss Marrow's speedway. The tree is the `firetv-deathride` worktree at
`9ceb02d7`, read on 2026-10-04; the material is the region bible
`docs/concepts/deathride/G0-region-bible.md` and the research dossiers in
`docs/narrative/research/` (R4 section 8 and the region cultures table, R1 P14). The campaign
has not been played, so everything below is a plan reconciled against research, never a
result. It is a `process` application: it specifies the bible's fields and their readers in
play, not code.

## The palette that was there first

The region bible is a palette in the technique's exact sense — climate, ground colours, props
and an accent per region, written for art generation:
`docs/concepts/deathride/G0-region-bible.md:40 "Accent: faded hazard ochre"`, and for the
capital `docs/concepts/deathride/G0-region-bible.md:149 "a hollow spectacle"`. It is not empty
of economy, though, and that is what made the derivation cheap: its props already carry work,
such as the Ash Yards scale plate,
`docs/concepts/deathride/G0-region-bible.md:59 "worn flush vehicle-weighing deck fragment"`.
The material conditions were decided; nobody had yet read them as causes.

## The derivation, confirmed

R4 states the technique's order as its own pattern, high confidence on primary sources (a
novel's ecology; a game writer's statement on politics through material conditions):
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:91 "Climate makes economy, economy makes custom, custom makes slang."`,
with the naive failure
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:92 "culture assigned by mood board feels like costume"`.
Sources: LitCharts on Dune (https://www.litcharts.com/lit/dune/themes/environment-and-human-culture);
Skidmore, "Water discipline" (https://bryceskidmore.medium.com/water-discipline-the-ecology-of-dune-edaea5f55c57);
Platypus Review, Helen Hindpere (https://platypus1917.org/2025/02/01/forward-looking-return-an-interview-with-disco-elysium-writer-helen-hindpere/);
PCGamesN, Robert Kurvitz (https://www.pcgamesn.com/disco-elysium/disco-elysium-detective-politics).

The Ash Yards row shows the derivation working from the palette's own prop:
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:163 "Salvage is sorted, weighed on the scale plate and sold by weight to the foundry"`,
which yields the coined word *weighed* (paid out, or dead) in the same row. The scale plate is
therefore the slang's touchpoint: a prop the player drives over carries the word the cards use.

## Upward lessons the dossier gave the technique

**The memory object.** R4's institution rule asks for more than an institution:
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:95 "plus one civic institution and one memory object"`,
sourced to Tony Gilroy on building a town through its civic rituals (StarWars.com,
https://www.starwars.com/news/tony-gilroy-andor-season-1-interview). The technique now asks
each institution for one recurring object whose state the player can read.

**The reader for each field.** R4's in-play rule assigns readers:
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:169 "at most one slang word per card, taught by context"`
and
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:169 "each moral code supplies the boss's taunts before the turn and loyalty lines after it"`.
Both entered the technique: the one-word cap for short text surfaces, and the code heard from
both sides of a boss's turn.

**The euphemism of the centre.** The Crown's slang row is labelled differently from the others —
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:167 "*equity* (people)"` — and the
technique generalises it: edge slang names the thing, centre slang hides it. This is the
dossier's own table work, so it is stated as inference.

**The price that costs the code.** Thin Air's price of joining is
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:166 "Neutrality, the thing that kept the road alive."`,
and Cinder Row's boss joins on terms:
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:164 "he turns only when the receipts protect the crew"`.
Both became rules in the joining-cost technique: prefer the price that costs the region's own
code, and let a local power join on terms rather than on defeat. R1 confirms the visible price:
`docs/narrative/research/R1-prestige-series-craft.md:282 "costs the boss something visible with Marrow"`.

## Where the plan falls short

**The dissent is mostly a group, not a person.** R4 claims
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:159 "Each region has a dissenting voice (P24)."`,
but the voices are collectives — `docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:163 "young sorters say the board keeps them owing the old men"`,
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:164 "Dissent: young hands say Ox's caution keeps them poor."`,
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:166 "Dissent: some say neutrality is how the road stays Marrow's."`
Two of five are "the young", one is "some say", and none is named or priced. The source
pattern itself asks for voices who
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:95 "disagree and challenge each other"`
(PC Gamer, Josh Sawyer, https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fallout/fallout-new-vegas-director-josh-sawyer-says-the-ncr-and-caesars-legion-feel-so-real-because-their-members-disagree-and-challenge-each-other-you-have-to-wrestle-with-the-fact-that-it-contains-multitudes/).
The standard stays: each region needs one named dissenter met twice. Thin Air's dissent is
nonetheless the best shape in the table — it accuses the code of serving the central power —
and the technique adopted that shape.

**Dissent has no reader in play.** The in-play rule at line 169 gives slang, institutions and
codes their readers and gives the dissent none. Until a card carries it, the dissent is a bible
field nothing reads.

**The table is the research's own.** R4 rates
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:230 "every option and table entry in sections 11 and 12"`
as its own low-to-medium inference, so every cell here is a proposal awaiting the owner, and
none has been voiced, carded or played.
