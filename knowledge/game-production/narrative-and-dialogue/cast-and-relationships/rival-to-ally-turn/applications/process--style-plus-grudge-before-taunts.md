# Death Ride's rivals: a driving signature, a grudge that persists, then the line

This application reconciles the before-the-turn half of the subject — style and grudge before
taunts, respect earned in the race, and the culture the rival keeps — against the research
written for Death Ride, a top-down vehicular combat racer whose four regional bosses (Rook, Ox,
Vex and Mica) are rivals the player beats and recruits against the league's owner. Anchors are
relative to the Death Ride worktree root and were re-opened on 2026-10-04. No rival has yet
been driven against by a player; this is a reconciled design, not a played result.

## What the dossiers confirm

The ordering is the dossier's rule, almost verbatim:
`docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:146 "The player reads character from how a car moves long before they read a taunt."`
Death Ride's use is a driving signature per rival — line choice, aggression trigger, weapon
habit and
`docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:261 "a visible tell before an attack."` —
after which
`docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:263 "Taunts then name the behaviour the player just saw."`
The menu trap is confirmed by a contemporary critique of a combat racer whose rival personality
lived in a menu:
`docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:147 "players won't have time to worry about this during a race"`.

The slice of life is confirmed at
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:63 "sympathy borrowed afterwards feels like a retcon"`,
and Death Ride schedules it as the first of
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:137 "Three earned beats per rival (P13 to P16)."` —
the rival under the leash away from racing, a race of mirrored competence, a line on what joining
costs. The fight-as-character rule is confirmed at
`docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:109 "make the fight express who they are"`,
with its naive failure
`docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:110 "The player never met X; they met a health bar."`

Retained culture is confirmed at
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:60 "let a former enemy keep his vocabulary and rituals, turned toward the new side"`,
and Death Ride's region table carries it as data: each rival's coined slang and code, with
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:169 "each moral code supplies the boss's taunts before the turn and loyalty lines after it"`

## Upward lessons the dossiers taught the draft

Four. The draft derived taunts only from the grudge; the dossier derives them from the behaviour
the player just felt (R2:263 above), which is cheaper and more reactive, and the technique now
allows both. The draft treated the grudge as backstory; the dossier makes it a state that
persists — `docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:263 "persist it across races until it is resolved"` —
and the technique now requires that. The draft had respect running one way; the dossier makes
it mutual and grounded in opposite strengths —
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:54 "let each see the other's excellence in a race"` —
and has the defeat line reframe the race rather than announce friendship:
`docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:284 "The line reframes the fight just played instead of announcing friendship."`
And the draft kept the rival's style after the turn without saying what it buys; the dossier
names it — `docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:286 "Past dread becomes present delight"` —
which is now in the culture technique.

## Where the dossiers fall short of the standard

**The persistent grudge is unproven.** The shipped racing system the dossier cites resets its
nemesis at the flag —
`docs/narrative/research/R2-game-narrative-craft.md:149 "it resets at the flag"` — and persistence across races is the dossier's
extension, and nothing yet shows players read a multi-race grudge as intended.

**Respect evidence is about respect, not alliance.** The racing film behind mirrored competence
ends on mutual regard between men who never joined forces, and the dossier notes
`docs/narrative/research/R4-wasteland-and-rivalry-craft.md:54 "The film exaggerates the real enmity for this payoff."`
It supports the respect beat; it does not show that respect converts.

**No check that a declared style is driven.** The signature is specified in prose and the
dossier does not say how anyone confirms the agent drives it. The standard requires the style be
handed to the behaviour authors with a check in play, because a declared style no behaviour reads
ships the default opponent under the rival's name.
