# The consent overlay, as German works-council law and the AI Act have it

**Pin.** Retrieved 2026-09-28 with curl from gesetze-im-internet.de (the
federal justice ministry's consolidated text) and the EU Publications Office.
Every quotation below was matched against the fetched text after drafting.
English glosses are this page's own.

## Consent to the guideline (BetrVG s.95)

s.95(1): "Richtlinien über die personelle Auswahl bei Einstellungen,
Versetzungen, Umgruppierungen und Kündigungen bedürfen der Zustimmung des
Betriebsrats. Kommt eine Einigung über die Richtlinien oder ihren Inhalt nicht
zustande, so entscheidet auf Antrag des Arbeitgebers die Einigungsstelle."
*Selection guidelines for hires, transfers, regradings and dismissals need the
works council's consent; failing agreement, the conciliation board decides on
the employer's application.*

s.95(2a), added in 2021: "Die Absätze 1 und 2 finden auch dann Anwendung, wenn
bei der Aufstellung der Richtlinien nach diesen Absätzen Künstliche Intelligenz
zum Einsatz kommt." *Paragraphs 1 and 2 also apply where AI is used in drawing
up the guidelines.*

## Consent to each hire (BetrVG s.99)

s.99(1): in undertakings "mit in der Regel mehr als zwanzig wahlberechtigten
Arbeitnehmern" the employer must inform the works council "vor jeder
Einstellung", "ihm die erforderlichen Bewerbungsunterlagen vorzulegen", and
"die Zustimmung des Betriebsrats zu der geplanten Maßnahme einzuholen". *Above
twenty employees entitled to vote: before every hire, inform the council,
present the application papers, and obtain its consent.*

s.99(2) lists the grounds on which consent may be refused, among them
"2. die personelle Maßnahme gegen eine Richtlinie nach § 95 verstoßen würde".
*The measure would breach a s.95 selection guideline.*

s.99(3): "Verweigert der Betriebsrat seine Zustimmung, so hat er dies unter
Angabe von Gründen innerhalb einer Woche nach Unterrichtung durch den
Arbeitgeber diesem schriftlich mitzuteilen. Teilt der Betriebsrat dem
Arbeitgeber die Verweigerung seiner Zustimmung nicht innerhalb der Frist
schriftlich mit, so gilt die Zustimmung als erteilt." *A refusal must be given
in writing, with reasons, within one week; silence is deemed consent.*

s.99(4): "Verweigert der Betriebsrat seine Zustimmung, so kann der Arbeitgeber
beim Arbeitsgericht beantragen, die Zustimmung zu ersetzen." *On refusal, the
employer may ask the labour court to substitute consent.*

## The EU duty is to inform, not to obtain consent

AI Act Art. 26(7): "Before putting into service or using a high-risk AI system
at the workplace, deployers who are employers shall inform workers'
representatives and the affected workers that they will be subject to the use
of the high-risk AI system." Art. 26 sits in Chapter III, Section 3, and for
Annex III systems Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 moved that section's application
to "2 December 2027". The German consent rights above apply now and do not wait
for it.

## What this changes in the technique

- The consent right is not a fourth mode: the employer still decides, and the
  output rules are those of whatever mode applies. It is an overlay with its own
  states, taken straight from s.99: requested (papers presented), refused
  (written, reasoned, within the week), consented, consented by lapse of the
  week, substituted by the court.
- The council receives "die erforderlichen Bewerbungsunterlagen", the
  application papers. Where a tool's output is part of what the decider relied
  on, it is part of what the council will ask to see, and the committee packet's
  rules (same evidence, same shape, limits beside claims) are the ones to meet.
- s.99(2) no.2 makes a departure from a consented s.95 guideline a refusal
  ground. If the tool's ranking logic is such a guideline, the decider needs to
  see a departure before submitting, not learn of it from the refusal.
- Inform (EU) and consent (national) are different duties with different dates.
  The golden path's "treating consultation as a checkbox" now keeps them apart.
