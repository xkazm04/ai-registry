# Laws — recruiting

Cross-cutting invariants. Techniques cite them by anchor; a technique that cites a law
must genuinely rest on it. These are the rules that recurred in every territory of the
extraction, usually with an incident attached — they are the shape of the domain, not
style preferences.

---

## <a id="absence-of-evidence-is-not-evidence"></a>Absence of evidence is not evidence

An unrecorded claim, an unscored candidate, an unmeasured metric, a check that did not
run, a competency the conversation never reached — none of these may render as a value,
a zero, a neutral constant, or a pass. Each needs a distinct state that says *not
measured*.

The failure is always the same shape and always flatters somebody: when "nothing was
recorded about how this skill was acquired" defaults to the strongest evidence tier, a
well-written résumé outranks one carrying real artifacts. When an unscored candidate is
coerced to zero, an unmeasured person is ranked worst and auto-rejected on a number
nobody computed. When an unevaluated module takes a neutral 60, it is indistinguishable
from a measured 60. The honest default understates rather than flatters, and the
unmeasured state is a type, not a magic number.

## <a id="uncertainty-resolves-toward-the-candidate"></a>Uncertainty resolves toward the candidate

Where the system is unsure and the outcome is adverse, it must fail closed — toward
the person, away from the automated action. An unclassifiable candidate is shielded from
automated rejection rather than defaulted into an unprotected class. A malformed or
unrecognised verdict becomes *hold*, never *advance* and never *reject*. A tie at an
irreversible cutoff spares the whole tied group rather than splitting it by arrival
order. An unknown education level skips a gate instead of failing it.

The asymmetry is deliberate and it is not symmetric with cost: a wrongly-held candidate
costs a review, a wrongly-rejected candidate costs them the job and costs you the claim.
A positive predicate that merely drives encouraging copy may treat unknown as false; a
safety predicate never may.

## <a id="a-claim-carries-its-sample-and-its-basis"></a>A claim carries its sample and its basis

Every rate, ratio, band and verdict states what it was computed over and how many
observations stand behind it, and refuses to render when that number is too small to
support it. A conversion rate needs a real stage transition, not an inferred one. A
selection-rate ratio needs a cohort large enough for a proportion to be stable. A
comparative claim needs at least two things to compare. A peer benchmark needs enough
contributors that no contributor is identifiable.

"Insufficient sample" is a distinct verdict, not a missing one, and never a pass. A
metric that cannot say when it does not know is not a measurement, it is decoration.

## <a id="a-predictor-cannot-grade-its-own-labels"></a>A predictor cannot grade its own labels

When a score causes an outcome, that outcome cannot validate the score. A screening
threshold that rejects below a floor produces the very rejections a reliability curve
would score it against, so a perfectly biased screener draws a perfect diagram. The
coupling must be named wherever the curve is shown, and broken by a clean arm — a
stable, deterministic holdout of candidates the score would have acted on but did not.

The holdout must be deterministic rather than random: a re-roll invalidates a signed
approval set, and a membership that moves when a threshold moves turns the threshold
control into a re-roll button for sparing one specific person. Where no clean arm
exists, the curve measures internal consistency and must say so.

## <a id="every-decision-names-its-actor"></a>Every consequential decision names its actor

A decision about a person records who or what made it: a named human, or the automated
process, with a third honest state for "cannot determine". A null actor renders as *not
identified*, never as a default person. A human reversing a machine's decision seals to
the reverser and never inherits the machine's attribution. Authority may be downgraded
from human to automated when the record is unclear, never upgraded.

Misattributing accountability is the one failure an audit surface may never have,
because the whole point of the record is to answer who is answerable.

## <a id="a-verdict-is-bound-to-what-it-judged"></a>A verdict is bound to what it judged

A quality verdict binds to the exact content and the exact rubric that produced it.
Re-produced content inherits neither the pass nor the condemnation. A verdict scored
under a superseded rubric is marked superseded rather than silently re-meant, and a
rating stamps the version of the scale it was scored on so a later revision cannot
retroactively mark an axis off-rubric.

Condemnation and elevation are deliberately asymmetric: an unprovable verdict still
condemns, because dropping a recorded failure is the optimistic lie this whole layer
exists to prevent — but it is labelled unverified, never passed off as current.

## <a id="no-adverse-outcome-is-solely-automated"></a>No adverse outcome is solely automated

A machine may rank, sort, surface and recommend. A person makes every advance, offer
and rejection. Rejection is never a routable automated outcome: a model may *recommend*
reject, but the machine-actionable route admits only advance or hold, and anything else
parks at a human gate.

Where automation acts in bulk, the human approves the *exact set* they reviewed — a
signed preview re-derived at commit, refused when the cohort has drifted — and the
adverse action stays reversible, with a reconsider path that reads the sealed reason
back. An unattended process queues; it does not execute.

## <a id="say-only-what-the-record-holds"></a>Say only what the record holds

A rejection reason, a feedback line, a rationale, an advertisement, a market figure:
each says what was actually recorded, in the words the record holds, or says nothing.
Never invent a gap the candidate's own evidence disproves. Never advise adding something
their profile already shows. Never imply a conversation that did not happen. Never
fabricate a testimonial, a benefit, or a salary the role never stated.

Silence beats invention. An empty feedback field is a better artifact than generic
advice, because generic advice is a claim about a person that nobody made.

## <a id="inference-must-look-like-inference"></a>Inference must look like inference

A model's guess must never render in the visual or linguistic grammar reserved for
measurement. A self-reported confidence is evidence about the model, not about the
person, and does not get the tone band a measured quantity gets. An enrichment is
labelled as an enrichment; a heuristic reading of a career is a hypothesis carrying a
suggested probe, not a verdict. A document yields hypotheses; a conversation yields
evidence; only a demonstration yields proof, and the three are never shown alike.

Where a system cannot see something, it says so: a bounded evidence budget is
enumerated, and "could not determine" never renders as "no concern found".

## <a id="a-candidates-process-never-stalls-on-your-constraints"></a>A candidate's process never stalls on your constraints

Your outage, your quota, your rate limit and your billing state are yours. A candidate's
own action — accepting an offer, booking a slot, submitting an application, asking for
their data — must not fail because of them. Metered work a recruiter initiates may be
blocked honestly; a candidate's action is debited and never gated.

The same rule governs degradation: when a model is unavailable the process continues on
a deterministic path with its provenance truthfully downgraded, rather than blocking a
pipeline or, worse, freezing a degraded verdict as though it were authoritative.

## <a id="meaning-does-not-live-in-a-label"></a>Meaning does not live in a label

Teams rename their stages, split their columns, invent their own titles and grade
people on their own scales. Nothing may derive meaning from a display string. Rules key
off a stable role vocabulary — entry, screening, interview, offer, terminal — so a
renamed board does not silently change what the fairness metric measures or tell an
offer-stage candidate that their résumé has been received.

The same applies across languages and systems: an audit record persists structured
facts and composes its sentence at render time, because prose frozen in the producing
machine's language is unreadable to the next reader and indefensible in the next
jurisdiction.
