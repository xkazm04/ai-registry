# A cancelled posting is not a non-selection: the texts

**Pin.** Retrieved 2026-09-28 with curl and matched verbatim after drafting.
The technique was confirmed, not changed, by this reading. This page records
the evidence it stands on. The clock is six months, because the federal texts
move with administrations.

## The national system keeps the two apart

- **OMB/OPM M-24-16 (14 August 2024), p.4** names the notification events as
  "application acceptance, determination of qualifications, issuance of
  certificates to hiring manager, and selection or cancellation of
  announcement". Cancellation is a notice of its own, beside selection.
- **OPM's FAQ (August 2024), p.2** restates four touchpoints, "application
  received, application assessed for qualifications, applicant referred to
  selecting official (or not); and applicant selected (or not)". Cancellation
  is not in this list, so the four-touchpoint reading alone would fold it into
  "not selected". The memo's wording is the stricter one.
- **The federal job site's help ("How to see your application and job
  status", updated September 2026)** gives the job, not the applicant, a
  status of its own: "Hiring complete — Hiring is completed and the position
  is filled." and "Job canceled — The agency withdrew the job announcement and
  did not hire anyone. An agency can cancel a job announcement any time after
  they post a job." The requisition's end is reported as a fact about the
  requisition, which is this technique's procedure step 3.

A coaching blog claims that when a federal referral list expires, everyone on
it is marked "Not Selected". This was seen only in a summary and is not relied
on. If it is true, it is a live instance of the collapse this technique
forbids.

## The research on what a decline may say

- **SIOP 2012, PDF p.15, Recommendation #6:** administrators giving negative
  feedback should "focus on the facts, such as the strong candidate pool, as
  opposed to commenting on idiosyncratic characteristics of the candidate.
  Such honesty does not, however, imply that organizations need to reveal all
  aspects of selection processes or decision-making procedures to
  applicants." That supports the split between the state (always said) and
  the cause (not always disclosable).
- **SIOP 2020, the practice table, honesty row:** "If providing negative
  results, focus on the facts and not personal characteristics."

No text found addresses the specific case of copy that implies an evaluation
when the requisition ended. The technique's rule there rests on
say-only-what-the-record-holds, and on the national system's structural
separation above.
