# The internal/external split in four products, and where pay disclosure attaches

Read on 2026-09-29. *Summary* means a fetch tool summarised the page or search results
were read, so wording is unverified against the page; *Text* means the source's own
words were read; *Lane* means a research lane reported reading it.

## The split is real, and there is a third value

- **Greenhouse:** external and internal job posts are managed separately, and a post
  goes live with its own toggle after the job is approved. Summary
  (`support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360020285311`).
- **Ashby:** a posting's job-board setting offers the external board, the internal
  board, or both, and posting is separate from job status (the fetched page says job
  status controls whether candidates can be considered and whether the role appears on
  the dashboard). Summary (`docs.ashbyhq.com/creating-and-opening-jobs`).
- **Workday:** internal, external and agency career sites; the recruiter chooses
  Internal, External or All. A frozen requisition that remains posted still accepts
  applications, and unposting is a step that can be added to the freeze process.
  Summary (job-requisitions page updated 2026-07-10).
- **Lever:** postings can be internal, external and unlisted, and internal and external
  are not mutually exclusive. Search summary only; the help pages returned errors.

*Unlisted* is the third value the technique now names: reachable by link, shown on no
board. It is the one that arrives by accident.

## Where a pay-disclosure duty attaches

- **Colorado** (Lane; adopted rule text, 7 CCR 1103-13, effective 2024-01-01): a "job
  opportunity" is a current or anticipated vacancy the employer is considering
  candidates for, interviewing for, or externally posts (rule 2.7). Rule 4.1 says
  notices of job opportunities, "including both those an employer 'externally posts'
  ... and those to notify current employees", must include compensation, benefits and
  application processes; rule 4.1.4 requires reasonable efforts to notify all employees
  the same calendar day and before a selection decision. The duty can therefore arise
  before any external posting. A summary suggests the rules were later moved into a
  different rule number; that was not verified.
- **Washington** (Lane; RCW 49.58.110(2), text): on request of an employee offered an
  internal transfer or promotion, the employer must provide the wage scale or salary
  range for the new position; employers of 15 or more.
- **New York** (Summary): Labor Law 194-b covers advertised job, promotion or transfer
  opportunities. **Illinois** (Summary): a pay scale in postings, and promotion
  opportunities made known to current employees; ilga.gov could not be read.
- **Ontario** (Lane; O. Reg. 476/24 s. 2): the definition of a publicly advertised job
  posting excludes a posting restricted to existing employees. Internal-only postings
  are exempt there.
- **EU Directive 2023/970 Art. 5(1)** (Lane, from the EU Publications Office text):
  applicants have the right to the initial pay or its range, provided "such as in a
  published job vacancy notice, prior to the job interview or otherwise"; Art. 34 sets
  transposition by 7 June 2026. Reported status as of June to August 2026 (Summary):
  Slovakia, Italy, Lithuania and Malta met the deadline, Greece followed on 6 July, the
  Commission gave no delay, and the Czech Republic missed it, with its bill in
  legislative process and most duties from 1 January 2027.

The sibling `stated-pay-and-place-test` owns the rules and already says "the gate
belongs on the step the law names"; what this page adds is that the step is sometimes
the internal notice (Colorado) or the interview invitation (EU), and sometimes nowhere
internal at all (Ontario).
