# Process: sentences in boxes redrawn as labels, paragraphs turned into tables

Witness: the two-round writing contest described in the `article-structure` application
(round two judged on 2026-10-05). The articles were single self-contained HTML pages with
their figures drawn in the page (inline SVG or HTML and CSS). Entry files are local run output; this records what they show, read on
2026-10-05.

## The finding, in both directions

Round one failed visually in two opposite ways, and the owner named both:

- **Too little visual**: "Large amount of pure text blocks. Creating diagram images and
  passing, or comparative tables and other visual elements would help."
- **Too much text in the visuals**: of the entry with the most figures in the field (17
  inline SVGs), "Attachments overflowing with text are doing poor job, images should
  abstract concept and visualize graphically, for text dominant information we have
  paragraphs." Two other entries were noted for weak or mediocre visuals.

The host's amendment: wherever three or more paragraphs carry a mechanism, a comparison, a
sequence or a set of numbers, they become a figure or a designed table; a figure abstracts a
concept into shapes and arrows and is not a paragraph typeset in a box; keep text inside a
figure to labels and short annotations. A pattern ledger kept across contests recorded it as
"figures abstract, text stays in paragraphs".

## How the winning revision realized it

- **The request-path figure redrawn**: round one's boxes held sentences; round two drew five
  nodes with two-to-six-word labels, the reasoning moved to the paragraph beside it.
- **Four multi-paragraph passages became tables**: where the cost is collected (four
  collection points), the quality evidence (for, against, limit tags), the vocabulary census
  per script (heat-tinted), and the twelve-language premium comparison (heat-tinted, with
  the previous-generation vocabulary as a grey history column).
- **The vocabulary-building explanation became a flow figure** with a ladder of positions
  where each script first enters each vocabulary.
- **Eight figures and four tables** in all, each with a caption whose first sentence is the
  message and whose last names the source numbers; one figure's caption labels it an
  illustration.
- **Craft source applied and logged**: the figure guide cited in this subject (Rougier,
  Droettboom and Bourne, 2014), for "message trumps beauty", the caption's role, and
  banning decoration that tells the reader nothing new.

## What this witness does and does not show

It shows the three-paragraph rule and the label budget are applicable by a drafting agent in
one revision, and that the reviewer who named both defects accepted the result. It does not
measure comprehension, and the label budget's word limits are a working convention, not a
measured threshold.
