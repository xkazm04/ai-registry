# Process: a preview and a settled close added in a refinement round

Witness: a two-round writing contest held in a local contest arena (round two judged on
2026-10-05), one technical article per seat on the per-language cost of tokenization,
reviewed by the owner alone. The entry files are local run output and are not published;
this application records what they show, read on 2026-10-05.

## What the owner review named

Round one produced eight articles. The owner's review of all of them included three
structural points:

- the openings were "quite well picked", and so was the length;
- there was no content preview, so a reader "has no idea what to expect below regarding
  length, structure and benefit";
- the final chapter did "not wrap content result and the story very efficiently".

The host turned these into amendments for the refinement round: keep the opening and the
length; add a preview with an honest read time, an outline and the benefit; rewrite the
close to restate the result with numbers, tie back to the opening scene and say what a
reader should do differently.

## How the winning revision realized the preview

- **Opening kept, re-measured.** The same refund sentence and the same rhythm, with every
  count re-based on current tokenizers (the round-one counts came from the
  previous-generation tokenizer the owner called "not very relevant nowadays"). The structure was what had been praised, so only the data changed.
- **Preview directly after the opening**: what the post is, the read time, counts of
  figures, tables and sources, a linked outline of every section, and three outcomes
  phrased as "after reading you will be able to".
- **Read time computed, not guessed**: 3,312 words of article text (prose, captions,
  tables, code and the preview itself, excluding the interactive token chips) divided by
  265 words per minute, plus image time for twelve figures and tables at 12 seconds for the
  first, declining to 3, for 14 minutes as labelled. The 265 rate and the image schedule
  are the publishing platform's own published read-time rule (stated on its help centre,
  which returned 403 to a direct fetch on 2026-10-05; the same figures, plus 500
  characters per minute for Chinese, Japanese and Korean, are quoted in a 2019 write-up at
  https://www.freecodecamp.org/news/how-to-more-accurately-estimate-read-time-for-medium-articles-in-javascript-fb563ff0282a/).

## How the close was rebuilt

The closing chapter was retitled "What the refund message showed" and given the three
moves of closing-chapter-settles-the-account: four recap numbers already established in
the body, a paragraph restating the result as a fact about the opening sentence, the
post's integrating request-path figure, and three concrete changes to make on Monday.

## What this witness does and does not show

It shows the technique is producible by a drafting agent from a short amendment list, and
that an owner who named the absence accepted the result (the revision won the round). It
does not show the preview's effect on real readers: no reader telemetry exists for an
unpublished post, and a contest verdict is one reviewer's judgment.
