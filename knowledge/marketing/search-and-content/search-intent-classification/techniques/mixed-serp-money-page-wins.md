# On a mixed results page the money page wins the term

A results page that reaches no group's threshold is the engine telling you it
serves two audiences for that query and has not committed. The wrong responses
are to round the count to whichever side is larger and to build both page types
against the same keyword. The right response is a three-part rule: read the
order, break ties toward the money page, and write "mixed" on the block.

## Procedure

1. **Confirm the page is mixed.** No group reaches the agreed threshold, after
   each result was classified by what it is. A page with a comparison-heavy
   top and a guide-heavy tail is not mixed between transactional and
   informational; it is a comparison page with informational spillover, and
   the comparison verdict applies.
2. **Weight by position.** The top three results carry far more of the
   engine's judgement than positions eight to ten. If the top three are
   service pages and the tail is guides, the verdict is transactional and the
   block says "mixed, top three transactional". The weighting is convention,
   but it is convention with a reason: the engine's confidence is expressed in
   order, and a fifth service page at position nine is the engine hedging, not
   deciding.
3. **When it is genuinely even, the money page wins the term.** The service or
   product page takes it as primary, and the article that would have been
   written for the informational half is still written, links to the money
   page, and targets the informational variant or a supporting phrase - never
   the same primary.
4. **Write "mixed" on the block**, with the count. A mixed term is a page that
   needs watching: it is the term most likely to drift after a core update,
   and a dated count is what lets the next reader see whether it moved.
5. **Re-read it after the next core update.** Mixed pages settle; the verdict
   is revised when the page has committed, not before.

## Why the tie breaks toward the money page

Two reasons, one measured and one economic. Measured: every source of error in
intent classification runs toward informational, so a tie is more likely a
transactional query the reading under-called than the reverse. Economic: a
money page ranking on a mixed page earns calls from the buying half and loses
only readers from the other half, while an article ranking there earns readers
from both halves and calls from neither. A business builds pages to be chosen,
and the money page is the one that can be.

The corollary is one primary keyword per page, always. Two pages of one site
aimed at one query split the links, the freshness signals and the engine's
choice between them; the pair usually ranks worse than either would alone. When
both audiences genuinely need serving, they are served by two pages with two
primaries and a link between them, not by two pages fighting for one.

## Decision rules

- When no group reaches the threshold, read positions one to three first, and
  let them decide if they agree, because order is the engine's confidence.
- When positions one to three disagree among themselves and the count is even,
  route to the money page and write the article against a different primary,
  because the money page is the only page that converts the buying half.
- When a mixed verdict is recorded, record the count and the date with it,
  because a mixed page is the one most likely to have changed by the next
  reading.
- When a money page and an article already exist against the same primary,
  one of them is re-targeted, never deleted, because deletion loses the links
  and the rankings the page accumulated.

## When NOT to use

- When one group reaches the threshold. The page is not mixed and the count
  decides; this technique handles only the case the count cannot.
- When the split is between local and transactional. A map pack plus service
  pages is a local verdict, and the local page wins, not the national money
  page.
- When the split is between comparison and transactional. A comparison page
  is built, and the money page is linked from it; the money page does not win
  a comparison query by being a money page.
- When the business has no money page for the service. A mixed verdict routed
  to a page that does not exist is an open item for the owner, not an article
  by default.
