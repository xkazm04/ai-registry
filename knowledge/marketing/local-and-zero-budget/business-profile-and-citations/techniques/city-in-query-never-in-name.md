# City in the query, never in the name

The city plays two roles in local-profile work and they must never be confused. As a
term inside a keyword query it is a measuring instrument: it isolates local demand for
a service from national demand, because keyword-data vendors have no sub-national
targeting and "[service] [city]" is the only way to read the local number. As a word
inside a name field - the business name, a service name, a product name, a category -
it is keyword stuffing on the engine's own property, and it is the documented cause of
profile suspensions.

## The two roles

**Measuring.** For every service the business delivers, search `[service] [primary
city]` in the confirmed country database of the keyword vendor, try the two or three
natural phrasings, and keep the highest-volume one. The city in the query is what makes
the number local. The country comes first: both major keyword sources default to a
single large market, and every number read before the country is confirmed is for the
wrong market.

**Naming.** The service goes on the profile without the city. The profile already
holds the location - that is what the profile is - so a service named "drain cleaning
[city]" tells the engine nothing it does not know and tells the policy judge that the
owner is optimizing a name field. The same holds for "best", "affordable", "24/7",
"near me" and any other modifier that describes the query rather than the job. Those
are name-field stuffing patterns wearing a different hat.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the country database before the first lookup.
2. For each service, query `[service] [city]` in the natural phrasings; record the
   highest-volume phrasing, written as searched, as the money term.
3. Strip the city and every modifier from that phrasing to produce the profile name of
   the service. "seo services [city]" becomes "SEO services"; "emergency plumber
   [city] 24/7" becomes "Emergency plumbing".
4. Where two phrasings are close in volume, use one as the name and the other inside
   the description, never both as names.
5. Order the list by the measured volume, highest first - never by the order the
   website happened to list them.
6. Check the business name last and separately: it is the real-world name and nothing
   else. See the suspension checklist for the verdict format.

## The one permitted mention

The city may appear once, naturally, inside a sentence of a service description or the
profile description: "serving homes across [city] since 2009". This is practitioner
convention - the platform's guidance neither permits nor forbids it - and the reason it
is held to be safe is that it reads as a sentence rather than as a keyword string. Two
mentions start to read as stuffing; hold the line at one, and never in a name.

## Decision rules

- When a phrasing wins on volume but only because it carries a modifier, name the
  service without the modifier and keep the phrasing as the money term for the page,
  because the profile name and the page title serve different judges.
- When every phrasing of a service returns zero, record "no keyword" and say which
  phrasings were tried, because a zero from an unconfirmed database is not a
  measurement, and a service row that says "no keyword" without the tried phrasings
  reads as a lookup that did not run.
- When the keyword vendor is not connected, read the live results page and the
  engine's autocomplete and mark the volume "not measured", never zero, because
  absence is not a verdict.
- When an owner asks to add the city to the business name because a competitor has,
  refuse and write the bad string out in full using their name and city, because a
  competitor's stuffed name is a suspension that has not happened yet.

## When NOT to use

- Location pages on the website carry the city in their titles by design; this rule is
  about profile name fields, and the page-side rules belong to the doorway-prevention
  subject.
- A business whose registered real-world name contains a city ("[City] Plumbing Ltd")
  keeps it, because the name field holds the name on the sign; the rule forbids adding
  a city, not having one.
- Category names are chosen from the platform's list and never typed; the rule applies
  to services, products and the business name, where free text is possible.
