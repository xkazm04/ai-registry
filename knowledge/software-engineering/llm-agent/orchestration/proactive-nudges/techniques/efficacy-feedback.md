# Efficacy feedback

Measure what happened after a nudge without equating contact with usefulness.
Distinguish accepted dispatch, confirmed visibility where observable, interaction,
requested action and task completion. A click alone is not a successful outcome.

## Evidence and denominators

Record acted, dismissed, pending and unobserved outcomes separately. Absence of
interaction after a declared observation window can be a useful non-response
measure, but does not establish that the user saw and rejected the notice. Vacation,
off-device delivery and a problem resolved elsewhere are counterexamples.

Report counts with their period, eligible population and observation limits.
Attribute direct actions through notice identity or explicit user reports. Temporal
correlation alone does not show that contact caused an improvement; controlled
comparisons are needed for causal claims. Minimize retained personal evidence.

## Bounded adaptation

Declare sample thresholds, uncertainty handling, hysteresis and step sizes before
changing allowances. Sparse or censored feedback cannot support confident rankings.
Do not respond to silence by increasing urgency or contact frequency. Recovery can
use demonstrated value within the user's existing ceiling and enabled kinds.

A positive floor is an exploration choice, not a requirement. A zero allowance
may follow opt-out or an explicit policy disabling harmful or ineffective kinds.
Make automated changes visible and reversible; never silently re-enable an opt-out.
Adaptation does not guarantee convergence or substitute for evaluating unwanted
contact and actual task outcomes.

## Immediate control

Provide per-kind and broader contact controls. Apply changes to pending notices and
in-flight dispatch authorization, not just future admissions. Separate cancellation
of a requested reminder from disabling speculative suggestions, according to what
the user selected. Expire individual evidence under a declared retention policy.
