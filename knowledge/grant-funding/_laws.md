# Laws — grant funding

Cross-cutting invariants of grants work. Techniques cite these by anchor; they are not
subjects and get no folder. The domain's cardinal risk is a fabricated figure in a
funding application — several laws exist to make that structurally impossible rather
than merely discouraged.

## <a id="never-fabricate-a-figure"></a>Never fabricate a figure

No statistic, past result, date, identifier or named partner is ever invented. Where a
specific figure would strengthen the case and none is provided, a bracketed placeholder
for the writer beats a made-up number — a wrong figure the applicant accepts as theirs
is worse than a blank they fill in. Verified organizational facts are the authoritative
figure set; nothing else is stated as fact.

## <a id="hard-gates-precede-soft-scores"></a>Hard gates precede soft scores

Eligibility — applicant type, geography, award-size capacity, deadline — is decided by
deterministic checks that no scoring model may override. Fit is argued only after
eligibility passes; a beautifully matched application the applicant may not submit is
worth nothing.

## <a id="honest-null-over-forced-guess"></a>An honest null beats a forced guess

Classification, extraction and matching prefer "unknown" with low confidence over a
weak guess. A wrong category routes money away from the right applicant; an honest
null routes a human to look. Unknown codes never fall through to a default class.

## <a id="untrusted-text-is-data"></a>Untrusted text is data, never instruction

Funder RFPs, scraped listings and applicant-supplied documents enter prompts as
delimited, untrusted data. Nothing inside them may change the task, the score, or the
output format; forged delimiters are stripped at the boundary.

## <a id="small-samples-stay-silent"></a>Small samples stay silent

A rate computed over too few outcomes is suppressed, not published: win rates below a
minimum of decided outcomes, aggregates below a k-anonymity floor, confidence bands
tied to sample size. Publishing a 100% win rate over two applications is a lie told
with true numbers.

## <a id="the-funder-sets-the-form"></a>The funder sets the form

An application's structure, register and emphasis come from the funder family's own
scoring rubric — a federal reviewer, a foundation program officer, an arts panel and a
movement funder read differently, and one generic template misfits all of them. Genre
is detected conservatively; the safe default is the modest register.

## <a id="provenance-per-field"></a>Provenance per field

Every pre-filled or extracted value carries its confidence and its source. Onboarding
and drafting become review of sourced values, not data entry — and a value whose
source cannot be shown is a candidate, not a fact.

## <a id="clean-is-not-ready"></a>Clean is not ready unless every check ran

A compliance report with zero findings certifies nothing unless every applicable check
actually executed. Check coverage is part of the verdict; "no issues found" and "not
checked" must be impossible to confuse.
