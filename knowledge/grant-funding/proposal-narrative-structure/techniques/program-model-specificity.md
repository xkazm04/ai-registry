# Program-model specificity

Between the problem and the evidence sits the section reviewers read most
skeptically: what the applicant actually *does*. The failure mode is the
**category model** — "we provide programming", "we deliver services", "we
support the community" — language that names a class of activity instead of
an intervention. A reviewer cannot fund a category, because a category has
no mechanism, no dosage and no way to fail; they read it, accurately, as the
applicant not having a model.

## The reconstruction test

A model description passes when a stranger could reconstruct the
intervention from it: **who** delivers **what**, to **whom**, **how often**,
for **how long**, and — the load-bearing part — **through what mechanism**
the activity produces the change. "Weekly ninety-minute mentoring sessions,
one trained volunteer per three students, across the school year, because
sustained adult relationships are the strongest lever on attendance" is a
model. "Youth mentoring programming" is not.

## Procedure

1. **State the mechanism explicitly.** Why does this activity cause that
   change? If the connection needs the reader's charity to hold, the logic
   is not on the page. The mechanism sentence is the same causal claim the
   theory of change carries (see
   [theory-of-change-framing](theory-of-change-framing.md)) — say it here in
   prose.
2. **Quantify the delivery from verified operational facts** — cohort
   sizes, session frequency, staffing ratios, duration. These are the
   figures the organization actually knows; use its confirmed numbers, and
   where a figure is not confirmed, a bracketed placeholder — the pressure
   to sound specific is exactly where invented operational numbers creep in.
3. **Name what is distinctive.** Every field has a default version of the
   intervention; say what this organization does differently and why —
   population access, delivery adaptation, an unusual partnership, a
   sequencing insight. "Why this organization, not any organization" is a
   scored question on arts panels and an unspoken one everywhere else.
4. **Keep activities and outcomes distinct.** The model section describes
   what is done; what changes belongs to the outcomes claim. Blurring them
   here is what makes the whole narrative circular downstream.

## Voice and register

Specificity has a register dimension. The credible rendering of "specific"
differs by applicant type: an academic institution writes evidence-forward
and methodical; a municipal applicant writes measured and mandate-driven
with figures; a business writes capability and value delivered; a nonprofit
writes concrete and grounded. What is constant is the ban on generic
mission-speak — and on the vocabulary that signals it: "leverage",
"synergy", "world-class", "innovative" without an object. The strongest
voice source, where it exists, is the organization's own proven material —
match the voice of a past winning application, while copying no fact from it.

## Decision rules

- When a sentence would be true of any organization in the sector, delete
  or sharpen it — it is costing credibility, not adding safety.
- When the model has several components, order them by the causal chain,
  not by organizational chart; the reader is following the argument, not
  the staffing plan.
- When the solicitation asks for an "approach" or "project design" section
  under a rubric, the model prose lives there, labeled in the funder's
  terminology and sized to the criterion's points.
- When the draft's specificity outruns the verified facts — a ratio, a
  frequency, a partner named that nothing in the inputs supports — pull it
  back to a placeholder rather than letting a plausible detail stand.

## When not to use it

There is no genre where the model goes vague — but there are genres where
its *content* shifts. Advocacy work replaces service-delivery mechanics with
strategy mechanics (organizing tactics, coalition structure, policy levers);
arts applications express the model as the artistic program itself. The
specificity bar is identical; only what must be specific changes.
