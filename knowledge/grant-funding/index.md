# Grant funding

The craft of finding, winning and accounting for philanthropic and public grant money:
the funding-source landscape, eligibility and matching, proposal writing shaped by what
each funder family actually scores, and the operational discipline — deadlines, filing,
post-award reporting — that keeps an applicant defensible. One bundle covering
discovery through post-award on purpose; the categories mark the seams.

The upper two layers are transplant-clean — no repo paths, no product names — so they
apply to any grants operation, not only the one they were forged against. Applications
cite real code and prompts and name their stack in the filename.

Cross-cutting invariants live in [`_laws.md`](./_laws.md); techniques cite them by
anchor. Graph consumers group subjects with [`categories.json`](./categories.json).

Format: [RKB profile v0.1](../../docs/rkb-profile.md), an OKF profile.
Evidence: consumer-local by design — see the profile, §5.
